## Summary
"What's 2 + 5?" asked teacher Wilhelm van Osten. The answer, of course, was 7. The crowd that had gathered to witness this spectacle was amazed. Because it wasn't a human who answered, but a horse called "Clever Hans". Clever Hans could do math -- or so it seemed. 2 + 5? That's seven taps with the horse's foot and not one more. Quite impressive for a horse.

## Key Claims
- **Don't blindly trust model performance** — In machine learning, we have our own versions of this clever horse: Clever Hans Predictors, a term coined by @lapuschkinUnmaskingCleverHans2019.
- **A young field with old roots** — Linear regression models were already used at the beginning of the 19th century.
- **How to read the book** — You don't have to read the book cover to cover, since Interpretable Machine Learning is more of a reference book with most chapters describing one method.
