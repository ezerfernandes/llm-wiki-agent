## Summary
Chapter 7 of OpenStax [[Prealgebra]] 2e ([[OpenStax]]) names and organizes the **number system** the prior six chapters built piece by piece, then catalogs the **algebraic properties** that the four operations obey on it. §7.1 defines the [[RealNumbers|real numbers]] as the union of the **[[RealNumbers|rational]]** numbers (any `p/q` with `p, q` integers, `q ≠ 0` — equivalently all terminating or repeating decimals) and the **irrational** numbers (non-terminating, non-repeating decimals such as `π` and `√` of a non-perfect square), and arranges counting → [[WholeNumbers|whole]] → [[Integer|integer]] → rational ⊂ real in a nested hierarchy. §7.2–7.4 then state the field properties as identities holding for **all** real numbers: the **[[CommutativeProperty|commutative]]** (`a+b = b+a`, `ab = ba`) and **[[AssociativeProperty|associative]]** (`(a+b)+c = a+(b+c)`, `(ab)c = a(bc)`) properties of addition and multiplication, the **[[DistributiveProperty|distributive property]]** `a(b+c) = ab+ac` linking the two operations, and the **[[IdentityInverseZeroProperties|identity, inverse, and zero]]** properties (0 the additive identity, 1 the multiplicative identity, `−a` the additive inverse, `1/a` the multiplicative inverse, `a·0 = 0`, `0/a = 0`, and `a/0` **undefined**). §7.5 applies the identity property of multiplication to **[[SystemsOfMeasurement|measurement]]**: converting units (US customary and metric) by multiplying by conversion factors equal to 1, and converting temperature between Fahrenheit and Celsius. The chapter is where prealgebra arithmetic graduates into the *structure* algebra relies on.

## Sections
- **7.1 Rational and Irrational Numbers** — defines a [[RealNumbers|rational number]] as `p/q`, `p, q` integers, `q ≠ 0` (so every [[Integer|integer]], [[Fraction|fraction]], and terminating/repeating [[Decimal|decimal]] is rational); defines an **irrational number** as a [[Decimal|decimal]] that neither terminates nor repeats; the **[[RealNumbers|real numbers]]** = rational ∪ irrational; the nested set hierarchy counting ⊂ whole ⊂ integer ⊂ rational ⊂ real with irrationals alongside; classifying [[SquareRoot|square roots]] (root of a perfect square is rational, otherwise irrational). See [[RealNumbers]].
- **7.2 Commutative and Associative Properties** — the [[CommutativeProperty|commutative]] (order) and [[AssociativeProperty|associative]] (grouping) properties of addition and multiplication; subtraction and division are neither; using them to reorder/regroup so opposites (sum 0), reciprocals (product 1), common denominators, or round decimals combine first. See [[CommutativeProperty]], [[AssociativeProperty]].
- **7.3 Distributive Property** — `a(b+c) = ab+ac` and `a(b−c) = ab−ac`; distributing variables, fractions, decimals, and **negatives** (`−(y+5) = −y−5`); removing parentheses that can't be combined; the basis for combining like terms. See [[DistributiveProperty]].
- **7.4 Properties of Identity, Inverses, and Zero** — additive identity `a+0 = a` (0); multiplicative identity `a·1 = a` (1); additive inverse `a+(−a) = 0` (opposite `−a`); multiplicative inverse `a·(1/a) = 1`, `a ≠ 0` ([[Reciprocal|reciprocal]]); properties of zero — `a·0 = 0`, `0/a = 0` (`a ≠ 0`), and `a/0` **undefined**; a closing table summarizing all real-number properties. See [[IdentityInverseZeroProperties]].
- **7.5 Systems of Measurement** — US customary units (length, weight, volume, time), [[SystemsOfMeasurement|unit conversion]] by multiplying by a conversion factor equal to 1 (the identity property of multiplication) so units divide out, the base-10 metric system and its prefixes (kilo/centi/milli), US↔metric equivalences, and Fahrenheit↔Celsius temperature conversion. See [[SystemsOfMeasurement]], [[UnitConversion]].
- **7 Key Concepts** — chapter summary listing all classifications, properties, and formulas.

## Key Concepts & Definitions
- **[[RealNumbers|Rational number]]** — a number that can be written `p/q` where `p` and `q` are integers and `q ≠ 0`; equivalently, any [[Decimal|decimal]] that **terminates** (e.g. `7.3`, `−1.2684`) or **repeats** (e.g. `0.583…` with a bar). Every [[Integer|integer]] is rational (`n = n/1`), as is every [[Fraction|fraction]] and [[MixedNumber|mixed number]].
- **Irrational number** — a real number that is **not** rational; its decimal form never terminates and never repeats (e.g. `π ≈ 3.14159…`, `√5 ≈ 2.2360679…`).
- **[[RealNumbers|Real numbers]]** — the union of the rational and irrational numbers; the set on which all the chapter's properties hold.
- **Number-set hierarchy** — counting/natural (1, 2, 3, …) ⊂ [[WholeNumbers|whole]] (0, 1, 2, …) ⊂ [[Integer|integer]] (…, −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, …) ⊂ rational; the irrationals sit beside the rationals, and together they fill out the reals.
- **[[CommutativeProperty|Commutative property]]** — order does not change the result: `a+b = b+a` and `a·b = b·a`. Holds for addition and multiplication only.
- **[[AssociativeProperty|Associative property]]** — grouping does not change the result: `(a+b)+c = a+(b+c)` and `(a·b)·c = a·(b·c)`. Holds for addition and multiplication only.
- **[[DistributiveProperty|Distributive property]]** — `a(b+c) = ab+ac` and `a(b−c) = ab−ac` (also `(b+c)a = ba+ca`); the bridge between multiplication and addition.
- **[[IdentityInverseZeroProperties|Additive identity]]** — `0`; `a+0 = a`. **[[IdentityInverseZeroProperties|Multiplicative identity]]** — `1`; `a·1 = a`.
- **[[IdentityInverseZeroProperties|Additive inverse]]** — `−a` (the opposite); `a+(−a) = 0`. **[[IdentityInverseZeroProperties|Multiplicative inverse]]** — `1/a` (the [[Reciprocal|reciprocal]]), for `a ≠ 0`; `a·(1/a) = 1`.
- **[[IdentityInverseZeroProperties|Properties of zero]]** — `a·0 = 0`; `0/a = 0` for `a ≠ 0`; `a/0` is **undefined** (division by zero has no answer).
- **[[SystemsOfMeasurement|US customary units]]** — length (1 ft = 12 in, 1 yd = 3 ft, 1 mi = 5,280 ft), weight (1 lb = 16 oz, 1 ton = 2,000 lb), volume (3 tsp = 1 tbsp, 16 tbsp = 1 cup, 1 cup = 8 fl oz, 1 pt = 2 cups, 1 qt = 2 pt, 1 gal = 4 qt), time (1 min = 60 s, 1 hr = 60 min, 1 day = 24 hr, 1 wk = 7 days).
- **[[SystemsOfMeasurement|Metric units]]** — base-10 system with prefixes kilo- (1,000), centi- (1/100), milli- (1/1,000); length (mm, cm, m, km), mass (mg, g, kg), volume (mL, L, kL).
- **[[SystemsOfMeasurement|US↔metric equivalences]]** — 1 in = 2.54 cm, 1 ft = 0.305 m, 1 lb = 0.45 kg, 1 qt = 0.95 L, 1 fl oz = 30 mL.
- **Temperature conversion** — `C = (5/9)(F − 32)` (Fahrenheit → Celsius) and `F = (9/5)C + 32` (Celsius → Fahrenheit).

## Key Procedures / Rules
- **Classify a number** ([[RealNumbers]]) — decide which sets it belongs to: write it as `p/q` to confirm rational; for a [[Decimal|decimal]], check whether it **stops or repeats** (rational) or neither (irrational); for a [[SquareRoot|square root]], a perfect-square radicand gives a rational (whole-number) root, otherwise it is irrational.
- **Use the commutative property** ([[CommutativeProperty]]) — reorder addends or factors freely (`a+b = b+a`, `ab = ba`); do **not** reorder a subtraction or division, which change value.
- **Use the associative property** ([[AssociativeProperty]]) — regroup three or more addends or factors freely; combine a coefficient with a variable's coefficient (`6(3x) = (6·3)x = 18x`).
- **Simplify by reordering/regrouping** — apply commutative + associative together so that opposites (sum to 0), reciprocals (multiply to 1), common-denominator fractions, or round decimals are combined first, then finish.
- **Apply the distributive property** ([[DistributiveProperty]]) — multiply the outside factor by **each** term inside the parentheses, keeping signs: `3(x+4) = 3x+12`, `2(x−3) = 2x−6`, `−2(4y+1) = −8y−2`, `−(y+5) = −y−5`; then combine like terms (`4(x−8) − (x+3) = 3x−35`).
- **Find inverses** ([[IdentityInverseZeroProperties]]) — the additive inverse of a number is its opposite (`−a`); the multiplicative inverse is its [[Reciprocal|reciprocal]] (`1/a`, `a ≠ 0`).
- **Simplify with zero** ([[IdentityInverseZeroProperties]]) — any product with `0` is `0`; `0` divided by a nonzero number is `0`; flag any division **by** `0` as undefined.
- **Convert units** ([[SystemsOfMeasurement]], [[UnitConversion]]) — multiply the measurement by a conversion factor written as a fraction equal to 1, arranged so the **old unit cancels** and the **new unit remains**; chain several factors for multi-step conversions (e.g. weeks → minutes); within the metric system, convert by shifting the decimal point (×1000 → right 3 places, ×0.001 → left 3 places).
- **Convert temperature** ([[SystemsOfMeasurement]]) — substitute into `C = (5/9)(F − 32)` or `F = (9/5)C + 32`.
