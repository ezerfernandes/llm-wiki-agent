## Summary
Implement the sliding-block puzzle game 2048 on a 2D grid, where the player picks a direction each turn and all numbered tiles slide that way, with adjacent equal tiles merging into one tile bearing their sum. The key subtlety is "non-greedy" merging: a tile produced by a merge cannot merge again in the same turn, and merge resolution follows the move direction's priority. After each valid move a new tile (a 2 90% of the time, a 4 the other 10%) spawns on a random empty square.

## Task Requirements
- Support four move directions (up, down, left, right); tiles slide maximally in that direction.
- Merge two adjacent matching tiles into their sum, but never re-merge a freshly created tile in the same move.
- Resolve ambiguous merges by move-direction priority (e.g. `[2][2][2]` right becomes `[2][4]`, not `[4][2]`).
- Treat a move as valid only if it changes the board; reject no-op moves.
- Spawn a new tile (2 at 90%, 4 at 10%) on a random empty cell after each valid move.
- Detect the win condition (a 2048 tile is created) and the lose condition (no valid moves remain).
