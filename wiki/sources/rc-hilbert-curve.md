## Summary
This task asks the programmer to produce a graphical or ASCII-art rendering of a Hilbert curve of at least order 3. The Hilbert curve is a continuous space-filling fractal that maps a one-dimensional line onto a two-dimensional area while preserving locality. The key insight is its recursive self-similar construction: each higher order is built from four rotated/reflected copies of the previous order, joined by connecting segments.

## Task Requirements
- Generate a Hilbert curve of order 3 or higher.
- Render it either as graphics (e.g. drawing line segments) or as ASCII art.
