## Summary
The task asks the programmer to render a cuboid (rectangular box) with relative dimensions of 2 × 3 × 4. The output may be true graphics or ASCII art depending on the language's capabilities, and either a static or rotating projection is acceptable. The key constraint is that three faces must be visible so the result reads as a 3D solid rather than a flat rectangle.

## Task Requirements
- Draw a cuboid with relative edge dimensions 2 × 3 × 4.
- Represent it graphically or as ASCII art, depending on language capabilities.
- Ensure three faces are visible (so it appears three-dimensional).
- Static or rotational projection are both acceptable.
