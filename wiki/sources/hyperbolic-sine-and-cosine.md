## Key Claims
- **Introduction to hyperbolic sine and cosine** — We have seen that the [[sine-and-cosine|sine]] of an angle can be introduced geometrically by looking at how a point moves along the [[unit-circle|unit circle]].
- **Fundamental hyperbolic identity** — The hyperbolic sine and hyperbolic cosine satisfy a relationship that plays a role analogous to the [[pythagorean-identity|Pythagorean identity]].
- **Hyperbolic identities**
- **Analytical expression of the hyperbolic sine** — A first derivation comes directly from the [[ExponentialFunction|exponential function]].
- **Analytical expression of the hyperbolic cosine** — Compared to the derivation of the hyperbolic sine, there is another way to obtain the analytical expression of the hyperbolic cosine, and it emerges directly from the classical geometric construction.
- **Analytical hyperbolic definitions**
- **Hyperbolic sine and cosine function** — The hyperbolic sine function \\(f(x) = \sinh(x)\\) associates each [[types-of-numbers|real number]] \\(x\\) with a value derived from the exponential function.
- **Relation to the circular sine and cosine** — Hyperbolic sine and cosine originate from the geometry of the equilateral hyperbola: \\[ x^{2} - y^{2} = 1 \\] where a hyperbolic sector determines a parameter \\(x\\).
