## Key Claims
- **Order of operations** — Before discussing the algebraic properties of [[real-numbers|real numbers]], it is essential to clarify how operations are performed in an expression.
- **Density and completeness in \\(\mathbb{R}\\)** — The real numbers are said to be dense: between any two distinct real numbers, there always exists another.
- **Closure property** — Among all the properties that characterize the real numbers, closure is in some sense the most fundamental: it guarantees that the set \\(\mathbb{R}\\) is self-contained with respect to its basic operations.
- **Commutative property** — The commutative property expresses a structural symmetry of the real number system.
- **Associative property** — The associative property concerns the way in which operands are grouped when more than two numbers are combined under the same operation.
- **Distributive property** — The distributive property describes the interaction between multiplication and addition.
- **Identity properties** — The identity properties describe the existence of special elements in the real numbers that leave other elements unchanged under a given operation.
- **Inverse property** — The inverse property complements the identity property.
