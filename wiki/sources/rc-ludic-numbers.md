## Summary
Ludic numbers are generated by a sieve closely resembling the Sieve of Eratosthenes, but the removal step uses positional (index) counting rather than value-based multiples. Starting with 1, then an array of integers from 2 upward, each iteration takes the surviving first element as the next ludic number and deletes every Nth element by position (where N is that just-taken number, counting the first). The key insight is that the survivors thin out by *index spacing* instead of by arithmetic multiples, producing the OEIS sequence A003309.

## Task Requirements
- Generate and display the first 25 ludic numbers.
- Count how many ludic numbers are less than or equal to 1000.
- Show the 2000th through 2005th ludic numbers.
- Stretch goal: show all triplets of ludic numbers below 250, where a triplet is three ludic numbers of the form x, x+2, x+6 that are all themselves ludic.
