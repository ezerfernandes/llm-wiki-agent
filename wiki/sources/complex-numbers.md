## Summary
Complex numbers arise to overcome the limitations of the set of [[types-of-numbers|real numbers]] \\(\mathbb{R}\\), particularly the impossibility of taking even-indexed roots of negative numbers. One major consequence of this restriction is the inability to determine the solutions of a [[quadratic-equations|quadratic equation]] with a negative [[quadratic-formula|discriminant]].
