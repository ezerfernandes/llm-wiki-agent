# BERTopic: Neural topic modeling with a class-based TF-IDF procedure

> **Stub page** — created as part of the [[hands-on-llm-ch05-text-clustering-topic-modeling|*Hands-On LLMs* Ch 5]] ingest so wikilinks to the BERTopic paper resolve. Full paper ingest is **not yet done**; this page captures the abstract-level summary from Ch 5's framing of the paper.

## Summary

[[MaartenGrootendorst|Maarten Grootendorst]] (2022, [arXiv:2203.05794](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.05794)) introduces **BERTopic**, a modular [[TopicModeling|topic-modeling]] framework that combines:

1. **Embedding-based document clustering** — embed documents with a sentence-transformer, reduce dimensionality with [[UMAP]], cluster with [[HDBSCAN]].
2. **[[ClassBasedTFIDF|Class-based TF-IDF (c-TF-IDF)]]** — a bag-of-words variant that computes term frequency **per cluster** (not per document) and reweights by an IDF computed across clusters, producing interpretable topic representations.

The framework's central claim is **modularity**: each stage can be swapped independently (embedding model, dimensionality reducer, clusterer, vectorizer, representation reranker), enabling many topic-modeling variants (guided, hierarchical, dynamic, semi-supervised, multimodal, online, zero-shot) on a single architectural base.

## Key Claims (per [[hands-on-llm-ch05-text-clustering-topic-modeling|Ch 5]] coverage)

- BERTopic preserves bag-of-words at the topic-representation stage but replaces the topic-discovery stage with embedding-based density clustering — combining semantic context (via Transformer attention) with interpretable term-based labels.
- The two stages are **largely independent**, enabling component swapping without retraining the rest of the pipeline.
- c-TF-IDF reweights cluster-level term frequency by an IDF across clusters; cluster-spanning words (stopwords, generic domain vocab) get downweighted.
- Representation models can rerank the c-TF-IDF keyword distribution per topic, including [[KeyBERTInspired]], [[MaximalMarginalRelevance|MMR]], and LLM-based ([[GenerativeTopicLabeling|generative]]) labeling.
