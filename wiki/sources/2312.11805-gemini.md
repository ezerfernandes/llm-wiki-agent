# Gemini: A Family of Highly Capable Multimodal Models

Gemini Team, [[GoogleDeepMind]] (arxiv 2312.11805 v5, May 2025; v1 December 2023). Technical report introducing **[[Gemini]] 1.0**, a family of natively multimodal foundation models in three sizes — **Ultra**, **Pro**, and **Nano** (1.8B / 3.25B). Gemini Ultra reaches new state-of-the-art on 30 of 32 benchmarks reported and is the first model to exceed human-expert performance on [[MMLU]] (90.04% vs 89.8% expert baseline).

## Summary

Gemini is built on [[Transformer]] decoders ([[1706.03762-attention-is-all-you-need]]) augmented with [[MultiQueryAttention]] for efficient inference at 32K context. The models are **natively multimodal** ([[NativeMultimodality]]) — pre-trained jointly on text, image, audio, and video rather than bolted together post-hoc — and can output interleaved text and discrete image tokens. Visual encoding is inspired by Flamingo, CoCa, and PaLI; audio is fed at 16kHz from Universal Speech Model (USM) features. Post-training applies [[RLHF]] over Reward-Modelled human preferences plus capability-specific recipes for instruction following, tool use, multilinguality, multimodal vision, and coding. Two product variants exist: **Gemini Apps** (consumer chat — Gemini and [[Bard|Gemini Advanced]]) and **Gemini APIs** (developer-focused via Google AI Studio and Cloud Vertex AI).

## Key Claims

- **Native multimodality beats late fusion.** Gemini is jointly trained from scratch across text/image/audio/video; this yields a uniform performance lift across modality benchmarks rather than a single-modality specialist + adapters.
- **Three-size product strategy.** One pre-training pipeline, three deployment targets: Ultra (frontier reasoning), Pro (cost/latency-optimized), Nano (1.8B / 3.25B, on-device, 4-bit quantized, distilled from larger Geminis).
- **State-of-the-art on 30/32 benchmarks.** 10/12 text+reasoning, 9/9 image, 6/6 video, 5/5 speech.
- **MMLU 90.04%** (CoT@32 with uncertainty-routed selection — fall back to greedy if no consensus). First model to exceed the 89.8% human-expert threshold.
- **MMMU 62.4%** (Maj1@32) — +5 points over the prior best, college-level multi-discipline multimodal reasoning.
- **GSM8K 94.4%, MATH 53.2%, HumanEval 74.4%, Natural2Code 74.9%, BIG-Bench-Hard 83.6%, DROP 82.4%, HellaSwag (decontaminated 1-shot) 87.8%.**
- **AlphaCode 2** (Gemini-Pro-tuned coding agent + search/rerank) solves 43% of 77 Codeforces div-1/2 problems → ~85th percentile, vs original AlphaCode at 50th percentile.
- **Long context retrieval @ 32K is reliable.** Synthetic key-value retrieval yields 98% accuracy across the full window; held-out NLL decreases monotonically with sequence position up to 32K.
- **Tool use as code generation.** Tool calls are emitted as code blocks; the system loops sample → if-tool-block → execute → re-sample. Tool-use SFT lifts GSM8K +10.4 pp, MATH +11.1 pp, NQ +9.0 pp, RealtimeQA +31.6 pp.
- **Factuality post-training works.** Halves the inaccuracy rate on factuality prompts (6.7% → 3.8%), boosts attribution AIS (40.2% → 60.0%), and lifts hedge-correctness from 0% → 69.3%.
- **Goodput jumped 85% → 97%** at Ultra scale by replacing periodic disk checkpointing with redundant in-memory replicas. Silent Data Corruption (SDC) became routine at this scale and required deterministic-replay fault localization.
- **Safety mitigation is mostly post-training.** Harm-inducing query enumeration → SFT (loosely Constitutional-AI-inspired with policy "constitutions") → RLHF on a safety-aware reward model. Multimodal queries needed a separate SFT pass since text-only safety did not transfer.
- **Dangerous-capabilities evals** ([[DangerousCapabilities]]) cover offensive cybersecurity, persuasion/deception, self-proliferation, situational awareness, and CBRN. December-2023 finding: Ultra solves entry-level CTF tactical challenges but not long-range exploration; CBRN evaluations suggest the model is unlikely to provide catastrophically harmful uplift.

## Architecture & Infrastructure

- **Backbone.** Transformer decoder; [[MultiQueryAttention]] (Shazeer 2019) for inference efficiency; sequence length 32,768.
- **Modality I/O.** Interleaved text + image + audio + video input; text + discrete-image-token output (in the spirit of DALL·E / Parti tokenizers). Variable image resolution to spend compute where needed. Video → sampled frames in the long context window. Audio → 16kHz USM features.
- **Tokenizer.** SentencePiece trained on a sample of the entire pre-training corpus; particularly improves non-Latin scripts.
- **Scaling.** Hoffmann et al. (Chinchilla) scaling for the largest model; smaller models trained for **more** tokens than Chinchilla-optimal to amortize inference cost (Touvron-style).
- **Hardware.** TPUv4 for Ultra, TPUv5e for smaller sizes. Ultra training combined multiple TPUv4 SuperPods (4096 chips each, optical-switch reconfigurable 4×4×4 cube topology) across multiple datacenters via Google's intra/inter-cluster network.
- **Software stack.** Jax + Pathways single-controller programming model; GSPMD partitioner; MegaScale XLA compiler statically schedules collectives to overlap with compute.
- **Reliability.** Redundant in-memory model state replaces persistent checkpointing → 85% → 97% goodput. SDC events expected weekly; mitigation is deterministic replay + proactive SDC scanners on idle/standby nodes.

## Post-Training Pipeline

A four-stage **data flywheel** applied to both product variants:

1. **Prompt curation** — diverse, real-world-shaped prompts (vendor + third-party + synthetic).
2. **SFT on demonstrations** — written by humans / model-generated then human-revised; balanced for capability/use-case diversity.
3. **Reward Model training** on relative-preference feedback over candidate responses.
4. **RLHF** ([[RLHF]]) — iterative; RM and policy co-improve through repeated evaluation/data refresh.

Capability-specific overlays: instruction following with verifiable synthetic data; tool use as code generation; multilingual SFT with translatability filtering; multimodal vision SFT; coding SFT/RLHF on top of the strong pre-trained baseline.

## Safety & Responsibility

- **Pipeline.** Assessment → Policy → Evaluation → Mitigation → Deployment, governed by Google DeepMind's Responsibility & Safety Council.
- **Safety SFT** uses ~20 enumerated harm types and a [[ConstitutionalAI]]-loosely-inspired recipe where Google's content policies are injected as "constitutions" and the model is asked to revise/rank candidate responses.
- **Multimodal safety is not transferable.** Text-only safety SFT did not generalize to image-grounded harm-inducing queries; a separate multimodal safety SFT dataset was required.
- **Red teaming** has two flavors: adversary simulations (security/availability/integrity/confidentiality) and structured sociotechnical red-teaming for representational / disparate-impact harms. Early checkpoints were vulnerable to simple jailbreaks and prompt injection — fixed in later versions.
- **External evaluations** under the White House Commitments, U.S. EO on Safe AI, and Bletchley Declaration: black-box access to a December 2023 Gemini API Ultra checkpoint, with safety filters mostly left in place.

## Position in the wiki

Gemini joins [[1706.03762-attention-is-all-you-need]] as a second **substrate-defining** entry in the AI/LLM corpus: where the 2017 paper defines the architecture, the Gemini 1.0 report defines the **frontier-multimodal-model deployment template** that subsequent papers (Anthropic Claude family, OpenAI GPT-4V/4o, Llama 3 / 4) all converge on — native multimodality, three-size strategy, RLHF flywheel, structured responsible-deployment review, dangerous-capabilities evaluation.
