## Summary
Chapter 5 of OpenStax [[Prealgebra]] 2e ([[OpenStax]]) introduces the **[[Decimal|decimal]]** as the extension of [[PlaceValue|place value]] to the right of the decimal point — a positional way to write [[Fraction|fractions]] whose denominators are powers of ten. It develops the full arithmetic of decimals ([[DecimalArithmetic]]), the two-way bridge between decimals and fractions including repeating decimals ([[DecimalFractionConversion]]), and applies the [[Equation|equality properties]] to solve equations containing decimals. The chapter then branches into three application areas that ride on decimal computation: **averages** ([[Mean|mean]], [[Median|median]], [[Mode|mode]]) and basic **[[probability|probability]]**; **[[Ratio|ratios, rates, unit rates, and unit price]]**; and **[[SquareRoot|square roots]]** (squares, perfect squares, the radical sign, estimation, and applications). Throughout, signs follow the [[SignedNumberArithmetic|integer rules]] from Chapter 3 and computations use the [[OrderOfOperations|order of operations]].

## Sections
- **5.1 Decimals** — decimal notation as parts of a whole over a power of ten; decimal [[PlaceValue|place values]] (tenths, hundredths, thousandths, …); naming decimals (whole part, "and", fractional digits, place of last digit) and writing them from words with zero placeholders; **equivalent decimals** (trailing zeros do not change value); converting a [[Decimal|decimal]] to a [[Fraction|fraction]]/[[MixedNumber|mixed number]]; locating and **ordering** decimals on the [[NumberLine|number line]]; [[Rounding|rounding]] decimals to a chosen place.
- **5.2 Decimal Operations** — [[DecimalArithmetic|adding and subtracting]] decimals by lining up the decimal points; **multiplying** decimals (decimal places in the product = sum of decimal places in the factors); **multiplying by a power of 10** (move the point right); **dividing** a decimal by a whole number and **dividing decimal numbers** (shift the divisor to a whole number, shift the dividend the same amount); money applications.
- **5.3 Decimals and Fractions** — converting a [[Fraction|fraction]] to a [[Decimal|decimal]] by dividing numerator by denominator; **repeating decimals** and overline (bar) notation; comparing/ordering fractions and decimals by converting to decimals first; [[OrderOfOperations|order of operations]] on mixed decimal/fraction expressions; circle **circumference** `C = 2πr` and **area** `A = πr²` (with `d = 2r`). See [[DecimalFractionConversion]].
- **5.4 Solve Equations with Decimals** — checking a decimal solution by substitution; the Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, and Division **[[Equation|Properties of Equality]]** applied to one-step equations whose constants/coefficients are decimals; translating "sum/difference/product/quotient … is …" sentences into equations and solving.
- **5.5 Averages and Probability** — the **[[Mean|mean]]** (`sum ÷ n`), the **[[Median|median]]** (middle of the ordered list; average of the two middle values when `n` is even), and the **[[Mode|mode]]** (most frequent value); basic **[[probability|probability]]** `P(event) = favorable outcomes / total outcomes`, expressible as a fraction, decimal, or percent, always in `[0, 1]`.
- **5.6 Ratios and Rate** — the **[[Ratio|ratio]]** (compare two quantities in the same unit; written `a to b`, `a/b`, or `a:b`; kept as an improper fraction); the **rate** (different units, units retained); the **unit rate** (denominator 1) and **unit price** (cost of one item; compare to find the better buy); simplifying ratios of whole numbers, decimals, and mixed numbers.
- **5.7 Simplify and Use Square Roots** — **square** of a number, **perfect square**, **[[SquareRoot|square root]]** and **principal (positive) square root**, the **radical sign** `√` and **radicand**; no real square root of a negative; the radical as a grouping symbol; simplifying roots of perfect squares and variable expressions; estimating roots between consecutive whole numbers; calculator approximations; applications (area, falling object, skid-mark speed).
- **5 Key Concepts** — chapter summary of all definitions, properties, and procedures.

## Key Concepts & Definitions
- **[[Decimal]]** — a way to write parts of a whole when the whole is a power of ten; positional notation extending [[PlaceValue|place value]] rightward into tenths, hundredths, thousandths, ten-thousandths, hundred-thousandths, …. The **decimal point** separates whole part from fractional part; **decimal places** = digits to its right.
- **Equivalent decimals** — decimals naming the same value; appending **trailing zeros** does not change the value (`0.31 = 0.310`).
- **Repeating decimal** — a decimal in which a digit or block repeats endlessly, marked with an **overline/bar** (`1.333… = 1.3̄`; `4.161616… = 4.1̄6̄`). A division that does not terminate produces one. See [[DecimalFractionConversion]].
- **[[Ratio]]** — comparison of two quantities in the **same** unit (`a to b`, `a/b`, `a:b`); simplified as a fraction but **kept improper** (not converted to a mixed number) so both quantities stay visible.
- **Rate** — comparison of two quantities in **different** units, written as a fraction with units retained. **Unit rate** — a rate with denominator 1 unit (e.g. 68 mph, 36 mpg, $12.50/hour). **Unit price** — the unit rate giving the cost of one item; the lower unit price is the **better buy**.
- **[[Mean]]** — the average, `sum of values ÷ n`; lies between the min and max. **[[Median]]** — the middle value of the ordered list (mean of the two middle values when `n` is even). **[[Mode]]** — the most frequently occurring value.
- **[[probability|Probability]]** (basic) — `P(event) = favorable outcomes / total outcomes`; a number in `[0, 1]` (0 = impossible, 1 = certain), expressible as fraction, decimal, or percent.
- **Square / perfect square** — `n²`; a perfect square is the square of a whole number. **[[SquareRoot|Square root]]** of `m` — a number whose square is `m`; every positive `m` has two (one positive, one negative). **Principal square root** — the positive one, written `√m` (the **radical sign** over the **radicand**); `√m = n` for `n ≥ 0` when `m = n²`. There is **no real** `√(negative)`.

## Key Procedures / Rules
- **Name a decimal** — name the whole part, say "and" at the point, name the fractional digits as a whole number, then name the place value of the last digit (`15.68` → "fifteen and sixty-eight hundredths"). **Write from words** — "and" locates the point; the last place-value word sets the number of decimal places; fill empty interior places with zeros.
- **Decimal → fraction** — digits after the point over the matching power-of-ten denominator (zeros in denominator = decimal places), then simplify; a nonzero whole part gives a [[MixedNumber|mixed number]]. **Fraction → decimal** — divide numerator by denominator (terminating or repeating). See [[DecimalFractionConversion]].
- **Order decimals** — pad with trailing zeros to equal place counts, compare as whole numbers; for negatives, farther right on the [[NumberLine|number line]] is greater. **Round decimals** — pick the target place, look at the digit just right of it, round up if `≥ 5`, drop the rest (same rule as [[Rounding]]).
- **Add / subtract decimals** — line up the decimal points (zeros as placeholders), compute as whole numbers, bring the point straight down. ([[DecimalArithmetic]].)
- **Multiply decimals** — fix the sign; multiply ignoring the points; place the point so the product has as many decimal places as the **sum** of the factors' decimal places; add leading zeros if needed. **× power of 10** — move the point **right** by the number of zeros.
- **Divide decimals** — fix the sign; move the divisor's point all the way right and the dividend's point the same number of places; long-divide with the quotient's point above the dividend's point. **÷ power of 10** — move the point **left**. Money answers round to the nearest cent.
- **Solve equations with decimals** — undo addition/subtraction with the Subtraction/Addition Property of Equality; undo multiplication/division with the Division/Multiplication Property; check by substitution ([[Equation]], [[DivisionPropertyOfEquality]]).
- **Mean / median / mode** — mean = `sum ÷ n` (must fall between min and max); median = middle of ordered list (or average of two middle values if `n` even); mode = value of highest frequency ([[Mean]] / [[Median]] / [[Mode]]).
- **Probability** — `favorable ÷ total` for equally likely outcomes; report as fraction, decimal, or percent in `[0, 1]` ([[probability]]).
- **Write/simplify a ratio** — first over second as a fraction, divide out common factors, keep improper; clear **decimals** by multiplying top and bottom by a power of ten; convert **mixed numbers** to improper fractions and divide as fractions. **Unit rate** = numerator ÷ denominator (denominator 1). **Unit price** = total price ÷ quantity, rounded to the cent ([[Ratio]]).
- **Square roots** — `√m` is the principal (positive) root; the radical is a **grouping symbol** (simplify the radicand first: `√(25+144)=√169=13 ≠ √25+√144`); `−√m` (opposite of the root) ≠ `√(−m)` (no real value); estimate by trapping between consecutive perfect squares; calculator gives a rounded approximation (`√5 ≈ 2.24`, use `≈`) ([[SquareRoot]]).
