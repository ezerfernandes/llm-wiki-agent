## Summary
Radicals emerge from the problem of solving [[equations|equations]] of the form \\( x^n = a \\), where \\( n \in \mathbb{N} \\), \\( n \ge 2 \\), and \\( a \in \mathbb{R} \\). In this context, the \\( n \\)-th root of a number is defined as a value whose \\( n \\)-th [[powers|power]] yields the original number.
