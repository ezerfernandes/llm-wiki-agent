## Summary
Chapter 1 of OpenStax [[Prealgebra]] 2e (published by [[OpenStax]]) builds the foundation of arithmetic on the **whole numbers** — the counting numbers together with zero. It develops the decimal **place-value** system (digits, periods, naming and writing numbers, the number line), the procedure for **rounding** whole numbers, and then the four operations — addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division — each with its named properties, a vertical column algorithm (carrying, borrowing, partial products, long division), and a vocabulary of result/operand terms. A recurring theme is **inverse operations**: subtraction is checked by addition and division by multiplication. The chapter closes with the **divisibility** tests for 2, 3, 5, 6, and 10 and with the standard "translate the word phrase, simplify, answer in a sentence" strategy for application problems.

## Key Concepts & Definitions
- **Counting (natural) numbers**: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, … (start at 1).
- **Whole numbers**: 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, … (the counting numbers plus 0).
- **Number line**: whole numbers placed left-to-right in increasing order; the point labeled 0 is the **origin**, and a number paired with a point is the **coordinate** of that point.
- **Digit**: one of the symbols 0–9. **Place value**: a digit's value depends on its position; each place is ten times the place to its right (the base-10 instance of a positional system — see [[NumberBase]]).
- **Periods**: groups of three places (ones, thousands, millions, billions, trillions, …) separated by commas in written numbers.
- **Addition**: combining addends to get a **sum** (a + b). **Identity Property of Addition**: a + 0 = a. **Commutative Property of Addition**: a + b = b + a.
- **Subtraction**: taking one number from another to get the **difference**; in a − b, a is the **minuend** and b the **subtrahend**. Subtraction is the inverse of addition.
- **Multiplication**: combining **factors** to get a **product**; written a × b, a · b, or with parentheses a(b). **Multiplication Property of Zero**: a · 0 = 0. **Identity Property of Multiplication**: a · 1 = a. **Commutative Property of Multiplication**: a · b = b · a.
- **Division**: splitting the **dividend** by the **divisor** to get a **quotient** (and possibly a **remainder**); written a ÷ b, a/b, or b)a. Division is the inverse of multiplication. **Properties of One**: a ÷ a = 1, a ÷ 1 = a. **Properties of Zero**: 0 ÷ a = 0, but a ÷ 0 is **undefined**.
- **Divisibility**: one number divides another with no remainder; tests exist for 2, 3, 5, 6, and 10.
- **Perimeter**: the distance around a figure (sum of its side lengths). **Area**: for a rectangle, length × width.

## Key Procedures / Rules
- **Name a whole number in words**: starting from the leftmost period, name the number in each period followed by its period name; separate periods with commas; omit the word "ones"; do not use "and."
- **Write a whole number from words using place value**: identify the period words, lay out three blanks per period (except the first), place the digits, and fill empty positions with zeros.
- **Round a whole number to a given place**: locate the target place, look at the digit immediately to its right; if it is ≥ 5 add 1 to the target digit (carrying left if the target is 9), otherwise leave it; replace every digit to the right with zeros.
- **Add whole numbers (column algorithm)**: align by place value, add right-to-left from the ones place, carry/regroup when a column sum exceeds 9.
- **Subtract whole numbers (column algorithm)**: align by place value, subtract right-to-left, borrow/regroup from the next-left place when the top digit is smaller; check by adding the difference to the subtrahend.
- **Multiply whole numbers (partial products)**: align by place value, multiply by each digit of the bottom factor (carrying when needed), shift each partial product left by one place (zero placeholder) per digit position, then add the partial products.
- **Divide whole numbers (long division)**: divide the leading digits of the dividend by the divisor, write each quotient digit above, multiply and subtract, bring down the next digit, repeat to the end; check with (quotient × divisor) + remainder = dividend.
- **Translate word phrases**: plus / sum of / increased by / more than / total → addition; minus / difference of / decreased by / less than / subtracted from → subtraction; product of / times / twice → multiplication; divided by / quotient of / divided into → division.
- **Application strategy**: identify what is asked, write a word phrase, translate to an expression, simplify, and answer in a complete sentence.
