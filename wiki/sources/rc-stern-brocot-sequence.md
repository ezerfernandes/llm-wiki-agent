## Summary
The task asks the programmer to generate the Stern-Brocot sequence (OEIS A002487) using a Fibonacci-like growth rule: starting from `1, 1`, repeatedly take the "considered" member, append the sum of it and its predecessor, then append the considered member itself, before advancing to the next member. The key insight is that this simple two-append loop produces a sequence whose consecutive pairs enumerate every positive rational exactly once, so any two adjacent members are always coprime.

## Task Requirements
- Write a function/method to generate the Stern-Brocot sequence via the append-sum-then-append-self rule.
- Show the first fifteen members: 1, 1, 2, 1, 3, 2, 3, 1, 4, 3, 5, 2, 5, 3, 4.
- Show the 1-based index where each of the numbers 1 through 10 first appears.
- Show the 1-based index where the number 100 first appears.
- Verify that the greatest common divisor of every pair of consecutive members, up to the 1000th member, is always 1.
