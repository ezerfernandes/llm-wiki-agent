## Summary
This Rosetta Code task asks the programmer to find the greatest common divisor (GCD) of two integers — the largest positive integer that divides both without a remainder. The GCD, also called the greatest common factor (GCF) or greatest common measure, is most commonly computed via the Euclidean algorithm, which repeatedly replaces the larger number by its remainder when divided by the smaller until one becomes zero.

## Task Requirements
- Compute the greatest common divisor of two integers.
- The result is the largest integer that evenly divides both inputs.
