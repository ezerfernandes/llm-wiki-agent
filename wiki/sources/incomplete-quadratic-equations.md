## Summary
A [[quadratic-equations|quadratic equation]] is considered incomplete when one or both of the terms \\(bx\\) and \\(c\\) are absent from the standard form \\(ax^2 + bx + c = 0\\), provided the term \\(ax^2\\) is present. These equations admit direct solution methods that do not require the [[quadratic-formula|quadratic formula]] or [[factoring-quadratic-equations|factorization]].
