## Summary
A module is the algebraic structure obtained by replacing the field of scalars in the definition of a [[vector-spaces|vector space]] with a [[rings|ring]]. The motivation is that several familiar constructions, such as ideals inside a ring, [[groups|abelian groups]] regarded with their canonical \\(\mathbb{Z}\\)-action, and rings of polynomials viewed over their coefficient ring, all fit a single pattern in which the scalars need not be invertible.
