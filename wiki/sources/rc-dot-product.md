## Summary
The task asks the programmer to write (or use a built-in) function that computes the dot product — also called the scalar product — of two equal-length vectors. The core operation is to multiply corresponding components of the two vectors and sum the resulting products into a single scalar. The reference example computes the dot product of [1, 3, -5] and [4, -2, -1], which evaluates to 3.

## Task Requirements
- Provide a function (or use a built-in) that computes the dot product of two vectors.
- Ideally support vectors of arbitrary length.
- Both input vectors must have the same length.
- Multiply corresponding terms from each vector, then sum the products to produce the scalar result.
- Demonstrate with the vectors [1, 3, -5] and [4, -2, -1].
