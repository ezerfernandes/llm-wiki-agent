## Summary
The task asks the programmer to produce a graphical or ASCII-art rendering of a Sierpinski square curve of at least order 3. This is a space-filling fractal curve closely related to the Sierpinski curve, typically generated by an L-system (Lindenmayer system) of rewrite rules or by direct recursion, then traced with turtle-style drawing commands. The key insight is that the curve at each higher order is built by string-substitution of the previous order's path.

## Task Requirements
- Generate a Sierpinski square curve of at least order 3.
- Render it as either a graphical image or ASCII art.
