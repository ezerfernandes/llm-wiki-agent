## Summary
A Jordan-Pólya number is any positive integer expressible as a product of one or more (not necessarily distinct) factorials, e.g. 480 = 2! × 2! × 5!. The task is to generate this sequence (OEIS A001013) in order and answer queries about it. The key insight is that the values are sparse, so they can be generated by repeatedly multiplying known J-P numbers by factorials (2! and up) and collecting the results into a sorted, deduplicated set rather than testing every integer.

## Task Requirements
- Find and display the first 50 Jordan-Pólya numbers.
- Report the largest J-P number below 100,000,000.
- Bonus: show the 800th, 1,800th, 2,800th, and 3,800th J-P numbers (optionally also the 1,050th), each with its decomposition into factorials from highest to lowest.
- When multiple decompositions exist, choose the one using the largest factorials.
- All required J-P numbers fall below 2^53, which bounds the search.
