## Summary
The task asks the programmer to produce a graphical or ASCII-art rendering of a Peano curve of at least order 3. A Peano curve is a continuous space-filling fractal that maps a one-dimensional line so that it passes through every point of a two-dimensional square. The natural construction is recursive: each segment is replaced by a scaled, rotated copy of the base pattern, often expressed through an L-system or a recursive subdivision routine.

## Task Requirements
- Generate a representation of a Peano curve (the original space-filling curve described by Giuseppe Peano).
- Use at least order 3 (three levels of recursive refinement).
- Output may be graphical (drawn lines) or ASCII art.
