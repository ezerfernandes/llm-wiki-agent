## Summary
The unit circle (or the trigonometric circle) is a circle of radius one centered at the origin of the Cartesian plane. It serves as the geometric reference for representing angles and their positions, providing a precise way to describe rotation, orientation, and the relationship between points on the circle as an angle varies.
