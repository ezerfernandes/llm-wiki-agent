## Summary
If \\(a\\) and \\(b\\) are positive [real numbers](../../properties-of-real-numbers/), where \\(a \neq 1\\), the logarithm of \\(b\\) to the base \\(a\\), denoted as \\(\log_a(b)\\), is defined as the real number \\(c\\) such that \\(a^c = b\\).
