## Summary
Vaswani et al. (Google Brain + Google Research + University of Toronto) propose the **Transformer**, a sequence transduction architecture built entirely on attention — eliminating recurrence and convolution. By replacing the recurrent encoder/decoder of prior NMT systems with stacked **multi-head self-attention** plus position-wise feed-forward layers, the model becomes massively parallelizable, reduces the path length between any two positions to O(1), and trains in a fraction of the time. The Transformer (big) sets new state-of-the-art BLEU scores on WMT 2014 (28.4 EN-DE, 41.8 EN-FR) at ~1/4 the training cost of the prior best, and generalizes to English constituency parsing. This paper is the architectural foundation of every subsequent large language model.

## Key Claims
- **Recurrence is eliminable.** A model based "solely on attention mechanisms, dispensing with recurrence and convolutions entirely" can outperform the best recurrent and convolutional sequence transduction models on machine translation while training significantly faster.
- **Self-attention has constant maximum path length.** A self-attention layer connects all positions with O(1) sequential operations, vs. O(n) for recurrent layers and O(log_k n) for ByteNet-style dilated convolutions — making long-range dependencies easier to learn.
- **Scaled dot-product attention.** `Attention(Q,K,V) = softmax(QKᵀ/√d_k)·V`. The 1/√d_k scaling counteracts the variance growth of dot products at large d_k that would otherwise push softmax into low-gradient regions.
- **Multi-head attention beats single-head.** With h=8 heads of dimension d_k=d_v=64, the model jointly attends to information from different representation subspaces; "averaging inhibits this" with a single head. Ablation row (A) shows both too few and too many heads hurt BLEU.
- **Three uses of attention in the Transformer.** (1) Encoder-decoder attention — decoder queries attend over encoder outputs. (2) Encoder self-attention — each position attends to all positions in the previous layer. (3) Decoder self-attention — masked to forbid attending to future positions, preserving auto-regressive property.
- **Sinusoidal positional encoding.** With no recurrence/convolution, position is injected via additive sine/cosine encodings of geometrically progressing wavelengths (2π → 10000·2π). Learned positional embeddings perform "nearly identical"; sinusoidal version is preferred because it may extrapolate to lengths unseen in training.
- **Translation results.** Transformer (big): 28.4 BLEU on WMT 2014 EN-DE (>2.0 BLEU above prior best, including ensembles); 41.8 BLEU on WMT 2014 EN-FR (single-model SOTA, ~1/4 the training FLOPs of the previous best). Trained 3.5 days on 8 NVIDIA P100 GPUs.
- **Generalization to parsing.** A 4-layer Transformer reaches 91.3 F1 (WSJ-only) and 92.7 F1 (semi-supervised) on Penn Treebank constituency parsing, beating most prior task-specific models without task-specific tuning.
- **Interpretable heads.** Attention visualizations (appendix) show individual heads tracking long-range dependencies, performing anaphora resolution, and exhibiting structures aligned with syntax.

## Key Quotes
> "We propose a new simple network architecture, the Transformer, based solely on attention mechanisms, dispensing with recurrence and convolutions entirely."

> "Multi-head attention allows the model to jointly attend to information from different representation subspaces at different positions. With a single attention head, averaging inhibits this."

> "We suspect that for large values of d_k, the dot products grow large in magnitude, pushing the softmax function into regions where it has extremely small gradients. To counteract this effect, we scale the dot products by 1/√d_k."

> "We are excited about the future of attention-based models and plan to apply them to other tasks. We plan to extend the Transformer to problems involving input and output modalities other than text and to investigate local, restricted attention mechanisms to efficiently handle large inputs and outputs such as images, audio and video."

## Architecture Reference (constants)
- Base model: N=6 encoder/decoder layers, d_model=512, d_ff=2048, h=8 heads, d_k=d_v=64, P_drop=0.1, ε_ls=0.1, ~65M params.
- Big model: N=6, d_model=1024, d_ff=4096, h=16, P_drop=0.3, ~213M params.
- Optimizer: Adam (β₁=0.9, β₂=0.98, ε=10⁻⁹), inverse-sqrt LR schedule with warmup_steps=4000.
- Training: WMT 2014 EN-DE (~4.5M sentence pairs, 37K BPE shared vocab); EN-FR (36M pairs, 32K word-piece vocab); ~25K source/target tokens per batch.
