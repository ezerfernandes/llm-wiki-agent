## Summary
Chapter 9 of OpenStax [[Prealgebra]] 2e turns the algebra of [[SolvingLinearEquations|solving linear equations]] toward applications. It opens with a reusable seven-step **problem-solving strategy** for translating word problems into equations (number, consecutive-integer, and percent problems), specializes it to **money applications** (coins, tickets, stamps, mixtures) via the relationship *number × value = total value*, then develops plane and solid **geometry**: angle relationships, the triangle angle-sum property, similar triangles, the Pythagorean Theorem, perimeter/area of rectangles, triangles, trapezoids, and circles, areas of irregular figures by decomposition, and volume/surface area of rectangular solids, cubes, spheres, cylinders, and cones. It closes by **solving a formula for a specific variable** (literal equations).

## Sections
- **9.1 Use a Problem-Solving Strategy** — the seven steps (Read, Identify, Name, Translate, Solve, Check, Answer); translating phrases into algebra; number, multi-number, consecutive-integer, and percent problems.
- **9.2 Solve Money Applications** — coin/ticket/stamp/mixture problems organized in a *Type | Number | Value | Total Value* table; the equation *number × value = total value*.
- **9.3 Use Properties of Angles, Triangles, and the Pythagorean Theorem** — complementary/supplementary angles; triangle angle sum = 180°; right triangles; similar triangles (equal angles, proportional sides); Pythagorean Theorem a² + b² = c².
- **9.4 Use Properties of Rectangles, Triangles, and Trapezoids** — perimeter vs. area; rectangle, triangle, and trapezoid perimeter/area formulas.
- **9.5 Solve Geometry Applications: Circles and Irregular Figures** — radius/diameter/circumference, π; circle circumference and area; area of irregular figures by decomposition.
- **9.6 Solve Geometry Applications: Volume and Surface Area** — rectangular solid, cube, sphere, cylinder, cone volume and surface area.
- **9.7 Solve a Formula for a Specific Variable** — isolating one variable in a formula (literal equations), e.g. d = rt for r or t.

## Key Concepts & Definitions
- **Problem-solving strategy** — a fixed seven-step procedure (Read → Identify → Name → Translate → Solve → Check → Answer) for any word problem.
- **Consecutive integers** — integers that follow one another: if the first is `n`, the next are `n+1`, `n+2`, …
- **number × value = total value** — the organizing relationship for money/coin/mixture problems.
- **Angle** — formed by two rays sharing a common endpoint (the **vertex**); measured in degrees. **Complementary**: two angles summing to 90°. **Supplementary**: two angles summing to 180°.
- **Triangle** — a three-sided polygon; angle measures sum to 180°. **Right triangle**: contains a 90° angle; the side opposite it is the **hypotenuse**, the other two are **legs**.
- **Similar figures** — same shape, different size: corresponding angles equal, corresponding sides in the same ratio.
- **Perimeter** — distance around a figure (linear measure). **Area** — surface covered (square measure). **Volume** — space inside a solid (cubic measure). **Surface area** — total area of all faces of a solid (square measure).
- **Rectangle, trapezoid, circle, rectangular solid, cube, sphere, cylinder, cone** — the standard figures and solids whose formulas are tabulated below.
- **π (pi)** — circumference/diameter ratio, ≈ 3.14 (or 22/7 for fraction work).
- **Formula / literal equation** — an equation among several variables; "solving for a variable" isolates one variable in terms of the rest.

## Key Procedures / Rules
**Problem-solving strategy (7 steps):** Read · Identify what you're looking for · Name the unknown with a variable · Translate to an equation · Solve · Check in the original problem · Answer with a complete sentence.

**Angle relationships:**
- Complementary: m∠A + m∠B = 90°
- Supplementary: m∠A + m∠B = 180°

**Triangle:**
- Angle sum: m∠A + m∠B + m∠C = 180°
- Similar triangles ΔABC ~ ΔXYZ: AB/XY = BC/YZ = AC/XZ
- Perimeter: P = a + b + c
- Area: A = ½bh

**Pythagorean Theorem** (right triangle, legs a, b, hypotenuse c): **a² + b² = c²**. Find hypotenuse: c = √(a² + b²). Find a leg: a = √(c² − b²). (Uses the [[SquareRoot|principal square root]].)

**Rectangle:** P = 2L + 2W; A = L·W

**Trapezoid:** A = ½h(b + B) (bases b and B, height h)

**Circle:** d = 2r; C = 2πr = πd; A = πr²

**Irregular figures:** split into known shapes, compute each area/perimeter, and add.

**Solids (Volume V, Surface area S):**
- Rectangular solid: V = LWH; S = 2LH + 2LW + 2WH
- Cube: V = s³; S = 6s²
- Sphere: V = (4/3)πr³; S = 4πr²
- Cylinder: V = πr²h; S = 2πr² + 2πrh
- Cone: V = (1/3)πr²h

**Distance:** d = rt (and solved forms r = d/t, t = d/r).

**Solving a formula for a variable:** treat the target variable as the unknown and apply the [[PropertiesOfEquality|Properties of Equality]] / [[SolvingLinearEquations|general solving strategy]] to isolate it; clear fractions by multiplying by the denominator. E.g. A = ½bh ⇒ h = 2A/b; I = Prt ⇒ P = I/(rt).
