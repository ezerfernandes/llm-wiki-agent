## Summary
SuperCollider is a dynamically typed, object-oriented programming language and runtime environment for real-time audio synthesis and algorithmic composition, originally released by James McCartney in 1996 and made open source in 2002. Its language layer, sclang, is a Smalltalk-inspired, garbage-collected interpreted language with closures and pattern abstractions, while a separate audio server (scsynth) handles low-latency signal processing communicated over OSC. It is used predominantly in electronic and computer music, live coding performance, interactive sound installations, and acoustic research, where its blend of expressive scripting and a high-performance synthesis engine is valued.
