## Summary
Tailspin is a dataflow-oriented programming language created by Torbjörn Gannholm in the late 2010s, centered on the idea of passing values through chains of transforms rather than mutating shared state. Its defining paradigm is pipeline programming: data streams left-to-right through composable transforms and templates, with pattern matching dispatching values to the appropriate processing logic. The language is dynamically typed but supports rich structural and range-based type definitions used in matchers, and it emphasizes immutable values flowing through the pipeline. Tailspin is primarily an experimental and educational language, well suited to data transformation, stream processing, and the kind of small algorithmic tasks showcased on Rosetta Code.
