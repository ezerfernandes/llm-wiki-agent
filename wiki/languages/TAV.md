## Summary
TAV is a small, dynamically typed scripting and stack-oriented language designed primarily for solving short programming puzzles and demonstrative tasks, in the same niche as other esoteric and minimalist languages catalogued on Rosetta Code. It favors a compact, expression-driven style with built-in operations for string and list manipulation rather than a large standard library or static type system. Emerging from the hobbyist and code-golf community of the 2010s, it is most often used for concise algorithmic exercises and exploratory scripting rather than production software.
