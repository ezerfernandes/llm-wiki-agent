## Summary
OpenEdge ABL (Advanced Business Language), historically known as Progress 4GL, is a statically typed, business-oriented fourth-generation language created by Progress Software in the early 1980s and still actively developed today. It is procedural at its core but has acquired object-oriented features in modern releases, and is most distinctive for its tight, built-in integration with a database: data-access verbs (FOR EACH, FIND, CREATE) and transaction handling are part of the language itself rather than a separate API. The language ships with its own integrated database, application runtime, and UI tooling, and is used primarily to build and maintain data-intensive enterprise and line-of-business applications, especially ERP and vertical-market systems.
