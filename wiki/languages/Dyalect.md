## Summary
Dyalect is a dynamically typed, multi-paradigm scripting language created in the late 2010s by Vasily Voronkov, blending object-oriented, functional, and imperative styles with a clean, expression-oriented syntax reminiscent of Swift and Rust. It runs on its own portable virtual machine (DyVM) implemented in C#/.NET, requires no installation beyond the runtime, and supports first-class functions, tuples, iterators, pattern-like matching, and user-defined types with operator overloading. Being a lightweight embeddable interpreter, it is aimed primarily at scripting, experimentation, and embedding into host applications rather than large-scale systems work.
