## Summary
Scilab is a free and open-source, high-level interpreted language and numerical computing environment created in the early 1990s at France's INRIA and ENPC, and now developed by the Scilab Enterprises/Dassault Systèmes ecosystem; it is positioned as an open alternative to MATLAB. The language is dynamically typed with matrices as its fundamental first-class data type, and it supports an imperative, array-oriented programming style augmented by functions, scripting, and some object-oriented features. It ships with extensive built-in libraries for linear algebra, signal processing, statistics, optimization, and ODE solving, plus the Xcos graphical block-diagram tool for modeling dynamical systems. Scilab is used primarily in engineering, applied mathematics, control systems, and scientific research for numerical simulation, data analysis, and rapid prototyping of algorithms.
