## Summary
Stata is a commercial statistical computing environment first released in 1985 by StataCorp, combining an interactive command-driven interface with a dynamically typed, imperative macro and ado-file scripting language for data manipulation, regression, and reproducible analysis. Its modern incarnation embeds Mata, a compiled, statically typed matrix programming language with C-like syntax that supports both functional and object-oriented styles for high-performance numerical work. Stata is used predominantly in economics, epidemiology, sociology, and the biomedical and social sciences for econometric modeling, survey analysis, and panel/longitudinal data work. Programs are typically organized as do-files (batch scripts) and ado-files (user-defined commands), making the language well suited to extending the platform with custom estimators and reusable analysis pipelines.
