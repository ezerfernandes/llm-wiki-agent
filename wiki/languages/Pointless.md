## Summary
Pointless is a small, dynamically typed, purely functional scripting language created by Christian Gill (with contributions from Jacob Bourne and others) in the late 2010s. It emphasizes immutability and lazy evaluation, builds programs from expressions and pattern matching rather than statements, and treats functions as first-class values, deliberately avoiding mutable state and side effects in the manner of its name. Pointless is intended primarily as an educational and exploratory language for learning functional programming and for writing concise, declarative scripts, and it ships with a tree-walking interpreter rather than targeting production or systems-level use.
