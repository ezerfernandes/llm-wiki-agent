## Summary
Peri is a small, dynamically typed scripting language built around an arbitrary-precision rational arithmetic core, designed so that all numeric computation is exact rather than relying on floating-point approximation. It follows an imperative, expression-oriented style with a compact C-influenced syntax, and is primarily used for numeric experimentation, mathematical scripting, and exploratory computation where precise fractional results matter. As a niche, hobbyist-era language it is best known through its Rosetta Code task solutions rather than mainstream production use.
