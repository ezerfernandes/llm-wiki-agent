## Summary
Panda is a declarative, dataflow-oriented programming language designed for transforming and querying structured data, where computation is expressed as the flow of values through pipes rather than as imperative statements. It is dynamically typed and stream-based: expressions naturally produce sequences of values, and operators compose to filter, map, and aggregate those streams, giving it a flavor reminiscent of pattern-matching and list-comprehension languages. Emerging in the 2010s as a niche research and hobbyist language, it is used primarily for concise data manipulation, generating structured output (such as XML/HTML), and exploring relational-style transformations.
