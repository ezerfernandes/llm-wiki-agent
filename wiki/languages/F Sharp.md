## Summary
F# is a strongly and statically typed, functional-first multi-paradigm language that also supports object-oriented and imperative styles, running on the .NET platform with pervasive Hindley-Milner type inference. It was created by Don Syme at Microsoft Research, with its first major release around 2005 and broad adoption following its inclusion in Visual Studio in 2010, drawing heavily on OCaml and the wider ML family. The language emphasizes immutability by default, algebraic data types, pattern matching, discriminated unions, and concise expression-oriented syntax with significant whitespace. It is commonly used for data science and analytics, financial and quantitative modeling, scripting, domain modeling, and general .NET application development where correctness and conciseness are valued.
