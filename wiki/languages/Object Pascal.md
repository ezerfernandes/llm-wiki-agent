## Summary
Object Pascal is an object-oriented extension of the Pascal language, originating at Apple in the mid-1980s and later popularized by Borland in Turbo Pascal and especially Delphi from 1995 onward. It is an imperative, multi-paradigm language with static, strong typing, supporting classes, inheritance, interfaces, properties, and exception handling layered atop Pascal's structured-programming foundations. Compiled to native code, it favors readability and rapid application development, and remains widely used through Embarcadero Delphi and the open-source Free Pascal/Lazarus toolchain for desktop, database, and cross-platform GUI applications.
