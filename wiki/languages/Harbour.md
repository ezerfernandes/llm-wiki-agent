## Summary
Harbour is a free, open-source compiler for the xBase language family, designed in the late 1990s as a modern, cross-platform superset of Clipper, the classic dBase-derived language used for DOS database applications. It is a multi-paradigm language that is primarily procedural and imperative but also supports object-oriented programming, and it is dynamically and weakly typed in the traditional xBase style where variables need not be declared with explicit types. Harbour compiles xBase source to portable C and then to native machine code, runs on Windows, Linux, macOS, and other platforms, and ships with the DBF/DBT database engine and a large standard library. It is used chiefly to maintain and modernize legacy Clipper business and database applications and to write new data-oriented and console business software.
