## Summary
PARI/GP is a specialized computer algebra system for number theory, combining the PARI C library with GP, an interactive interpreted scripting language and calculator front end. Originally developed by Henri Cohen and collaborators at the University of Bordeaux in the mid-1980s, it offers a dynamically typed, multi-paradigm imperative style with first-class support for exact and arbitrary-precision arithmetic over integers, rationals, reals, complex numbers, p-adics, polynomials, and algebraic structures. It is prized for fast, high-precision computation and is typically used by mathematicians and researchers for tasks such as factorization, elliptic curves, modular forms, and other analytic and algebraic number-theory work.
