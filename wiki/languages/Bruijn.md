## Summary
Bruijn is a purely functional, minimalist programming language created by Marvin Borner around 2022 that is based directly on the untyped lambda calculus and uses De Bruijn indices instead of named variables, which is the origin of its name. Programs are written as anonymous lambda terms composed from a standard library of combinators, with no built-in primitive types; data such as numbers, booleans, and lists are Church- or other encoding-based constructs rather than native machine values. As an experimental, esoteric language emphasizing theoretical purity and term-rewriting reduction, it is used mainly for exploring lambda-calculus programming, teaching, and recreational coding rather than for production software.
