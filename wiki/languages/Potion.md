## Summary
Potion is a dynamically typed, object- and prototype-oriented scripting language created by Sam Stephenson around 2008-2009, drawing heavy inspiration from Ruby, Lua, and Io. It is a small, expression-centric language that blends object-oriented and functional styles, treating everything as a first-class object and using a compact syntax with closures, message passing, and prototype-based objects. Potion compiles to its own bytecode and ships with a JIT-style virtual machine, making it primarily an experimental, research-oriented language for exploring language design and fast lightweight scripting rather than a mainstream production tool.
