## Summary
LabVIEW (Laboratory Virtual Instrument Engineering Workbench) is a graphical, dataflow programming environment developed by National Instruments and first released in 1986. Programs, called virtual instruments (VIs), are written by wiring together nodes on a block diagram rather than typing text, with execution driven by data availability and inherent support for parallelism; the language is statically typed, with wire colors and styles encoding data types. It is most commonly used for data acquisition, instrument control, test and measurement automation, and embedded/real-time and FPGA applications in engineering and scientific settings.
