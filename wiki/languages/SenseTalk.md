## Summary
SenseTalk is a high-level, dynamically typed scripting language with an English-like, natural-language syntax descended from Apple's HyperTalk and the broader xTalk family of the late 1980s and 1990s. It is primarily imperative and procedural, supporting first-class functions, handlers, and flexible containers (lists and property lists) while emphasizing readable, near-prose code through extensive use of synonyms and ordinal references. The language is best known today as the scripting engine behind Eggplant Functional, a model-based GUI test-automation tool, where it drives cross-platform application testing through image and OCR-based interaction. Its loose typing and forgiving, verbose syntax make it especially approachable for non-programmers and quality-assurance engineers automating user-interface workflows.
