## Summary
SAS is a proprietary statistical software suite and programming language developed at North Carolina State University in the 1970s and commercialized by SAS Institute. Its core is an imperative, procedural language organized around the DATA step (row-by-row data manipulation) and PROC steps (predefined procedures for statistics, reporting, and graphics), supplemented by a macro preprocessor for code generation and the matrix-oriented SAS/IML language. Typing is dynamic and weak, with values stored as either numeric (floating point) or character. It remains widely used in enterprise data management, business analytics, clinical trials, pharmaceutical research, and other regulated industries that demand validated, reproducible statistical processing of large datasets.
