## Summary
Langur is a general-purpose, dynamically typed scripting language designed to be expression-oriented and immutable by default, with a strong functional flavor alongside imperative and structured constructs. Emerging in the 2010s as an independent open-source project, it emphasizes clean, regular syntax, first-class functions, pattern matching, and rich built-in operations over collections (lists, hashes, strings) with extensive use of regular expressions. Values are predominantly immutable, encouraging a declarative, transformation-based style rather than mutation. It is most often used for text processing, data manipulation, and small scripting tasks where concise functional pipelines are convenient.
