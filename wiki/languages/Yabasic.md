## Summary
Yabasic (Yet Another BASIC) is a free, lightweight interpreted dialect of BASIC created by Marc-Oliver Ihm in 1995, with later contributions continuing into the 2010s and beyond. It follows an imperative, procedural paradigm augmented with subroutines and structured control flow, and uses dynamic, weak typing centered on just two value kinds: numbers (double-precision floats) and strings, distinguished by the trailing $ on string variable names. Designed for simplicity and portability across Unix and Windows, it ships with built-in graphics, printer, and basic system facilities. It is typically used for small scripts, teaching, quick numeric or text utilities, and hobbyist programs rather than large-scale application development.
