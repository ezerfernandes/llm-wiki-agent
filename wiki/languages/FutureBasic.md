## Summary
FutureBasic is a procedural, statically typed BASIC dialect for Apple's macOS, descending from the early-1990s ZBasic lineage and later distributed commercially by Staz Software before becoming free and open source. It compiles native Cocoa applications by translating BASIC source into C/Objective-C and feeding it to Apple's compiler toolchain, giving programs full access to the macOS frameworks alongside familiar BASIC syntax. Its supports both classic line-oriented BASIC constructs and structured programming with user-defined functions, and it remains in active use primarily for building lightweight native Mac GUI utilities and learning Mac development without the verbosity of raw Objective-C or Swift.
