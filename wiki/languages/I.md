## Summary
I is a small, experimental array-oriented programming language created by Marcin Wojnarski in the early 2010s, drawing inspiration from APL and J while aiming for a cleaner, more approachable syntax. It is a dynamically typed, interpreted language whose primitives operate uniformly on whole arrays, favoring concise function composition and point-free style over explicit loops. Like its APL-family relatives it is primarily used for terse mathematical, numeric, and data-manipulation tasks, and remains mostly a niche language explored for its expressive array semantics rather than for production work.
