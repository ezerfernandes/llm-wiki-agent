## Summary
Dt is a concatenative, stack-based programming language in the Forth and Factor lineage, created in the early 2020s by Justin Meiners as a small, scriptable shell-and-glue language. It is dynamically typed and combinator-oriented: programs are composed by juxtaposing words (functions) that consume and produce values on an implicit data stack, with quotations enabling higher-order and point-free style. Distributed as a compact interpreter, Dt is aimed at command-line scripting, text-processing pipelines, and lightweight automation where a terse, composable notation is preferred over conventional infix syntax.
