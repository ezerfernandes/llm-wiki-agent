## Summary
Apex is a strongly and statically typed, object-oriented programming language developed by Salesforce and introduced in 2007. Its Java-like syntax runs on the multitenant Force.com platform, executing in a managed cloud environment with built-in governor limits to enforce fair resource usage across tenants. The language is tightly integrated with the Salesforce data model and SOQL/SOSL query languages, and it supports procedural and database-bound constructs alongside its class-based OOP features. It is used almost exclusively to add custom business logic, triggers, batch jobs, and web service endpoints to Salesforce CRM applications.
