## Summary
Jakt is a statically typed, memory-safe systems programming language created by Andreas Kling (of SerenityOS) starting in 2022, and is implemented as a compiler that transpiles to C++. It is multi-paradigm, blending imperative, object-oriented, and functional styles, and emphasizes memory safety through reference-counting, automatic array-bounds checking, and explicit handling of nullability and integer overflow, while still permitting unsafe blocks for low-level control. Drawing influence from Rust, Swift, and C++, it targets systems-level programming where safety guarantees are desired without sacrificing close-to-the-metal performance. As a young, experimental language it is used primarily within the SerenityOS ecosystem and by enthusiasts exploring safe systems programming.
