## Summary
EchoLisp is a Scheme-like dialect of Lisp created by Christophe Devulder in the mid-2010s that runs entirely inside the web browser using JavaScript and HTML5, requiring no installation. It is dynamically typed and primarily functional in style while supporting imperative and list-oriented programming, and it ships with built-in support for bignums, rational and complex numbers, sets, and an interactive REPL with extensive native libraries. Designed largely for learning, mathematical experimentation, and recreational programming, EchoLisp emphasizes immediate feedback through its browser console and rich graphics, plotting, and data-structure facilities.
