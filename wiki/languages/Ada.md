## Summary
Ada is a statically and strongly typed, structured, imperative programming language with extensive support for object-oriented, concurrent, and generic programming. Designed in the late 1970s under a U.S. Department of Defense initiative and named after Ada Lovelace, it was first standardized as Ada 83 and substantially revised in Ada 95, 2005, 2012, and 2022. Its hallmark features include a rich type system with subtypes, ranges, and contracts, built-in tasking for concurrency, and a strong emphasis on readability, reliability, and compile-time safety. Ada is most commonly used in high-integrity, safety-critical, and real-time domains such as avionics, aerospace, defense, railway signaling, and medical systems.
