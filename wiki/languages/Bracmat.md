## Summary
Bracmat is a dynamically typed, multi-paradigm language created by Bart Jongejan in the late 1980s that blends symbolic computation, term rewriting, and pattern matching as its core mechanisms. Everything in Bracmat is an expression built from a small set of binary operators, and computation proceeds by evaluating and rewriting these tree-structured expressions, which makes it well suited to symbolic algebra and structured pattern manipulation. Its pattern-matching engine is unusually powerful, supporting associative and commutative matching, backtracking, and matching directly against nested data and strings. Bracmat is typically used for computer algebra, text and natural-language processing, parsing, and other tasks involving the transformation of symbolic or hierarchical data.
