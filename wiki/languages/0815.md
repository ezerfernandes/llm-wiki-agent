## Summary
0815 is an esoteric programming language whose source code reads like a sequence of cryptic, often palindrome-like tokens, deriving its name from the German idiom "Nullachtfünfzehn" meaning bland or ordinary. It is an imperative, low-level language that operates on two registers (X and Y) plus a queue, manipulating untyped machine-word integer values through arithmetic, bitwise, conditional, and looping constructs delimited by special bracket characters. Created in the late 2000s as a recreational obfuscation challenge rather than a practical tool, it has no abstractions like named variables, functions, or data structures. Its typical use is purely for puzzle-solving and code-golf-style exercises such as the Rosetta Code tasks, where the appeal lies in coaxing meaningful behavior out of a deliberately opaque syntax.
