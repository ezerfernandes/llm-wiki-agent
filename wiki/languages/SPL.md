## Summary
SPL (Simple Programming Language) is a general-purpose, imperative scripting language created in the 2010s, designed to be easy to learn and read with a minimal, English-like syntax. It is dynamically typed and interpreted, running on a lightweight runtime, and supports structured constructs such as loops, conditionals, functions, and basic data structures. SPL is typically used for small utilities, automation, teaching introductory programming, and rapid prototyping rather than large-scale or performance-critical systems.
