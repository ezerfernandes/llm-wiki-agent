## Summary
GNU Octave is a high-level, dynamically and weakly typed scripting language first released by John W. Eaton in 1993, designed primarily for numerical computation and largely compatible with the syntax of MATLAB. It follows an imperative, array-oriented paradigm with matrices and vectors as first-class data types, and interprets code interactively through a read-eval-print loop while also supporting batch scripts and user-defined functions. Octave is commonly used for linear algebra, signal processing, statistics, optimization, and rapid prototyping of scientific and engineering algorithms, and it serves as a free, open-source alternative to MATLAB.
