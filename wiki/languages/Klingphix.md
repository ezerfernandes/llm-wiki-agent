## Summary
Klingphix is a small concatenative, stack-based programming language created by Jan van Lent (Nascom) in the mid-2010s, conceived as a minimalist interpreted language in the same family as Forth and Joy. It is dynamically typed and manipulates values on an operand stack via postfix word definitions, with built-in support for strings, numbers, lists, and higher-order quotations. Programs are compact and composed by chaining words, making the language well suited to teaching and exploring concatenative programming rather than large-scale production use, and it appears chiefly in recreational and Rosetta Code-style task implementations.
