## Summary
Slope is a small, dynamically typed Lisp dialect built on a Scheme-like, parenthesized s-expression syntax, designed primarily for scripting and for producing vector graphics output (notably SVG). It follows a functional and procedural paradigm with first-class functions, list-based data structures, and a compact standard library, in the tradition of minimalist embeddable Lisps that emerged in the 2010s. The language is typically interpreted and aimed at lightweight scripting, generative and turtle-style graphics, and educational use rather than large-scale application development.
