## Summary
GAP (Groups, Algorithms, Programming) is an open-source computer algebra system and interpreted programming language for computational discrete algebra, with particular emphasis on computational group theory. First developed in 1986 at RWTH Aachen and now maintained by an international collaboration, it pairs a high-level, dynamically typed, imperative language with extensive built-in libraries of algebraic algorithms and data on groups, rings, fields, and combinatorial structures. The language supports functional and procedural styles, automatic arbitrary-precision integers, and exact arithmetic, making it a staple tool for mathematicians and researchers exploring permutation groups, representation theory, and other areas of abstract algebra.
