## Summary
TUSCRIPT is an interpreted, imperative scripting language with roots in the IBM mainframe and VM/CMS world, originally developed in the 1980s as a macro and text-processing language and still used through interpreters available on contemporary platforms. It is dynamically typed and string-oriented, treating most data as text and offering rich built-in operators for string manipulation, list and table handling, and pattern matching. Programs are written as sequences of commands with a distinctive verbose, keyword-driven syntax, making the language particularly suited to text transformation, report generation, and lightweight automation tasks rather than systems programming.
