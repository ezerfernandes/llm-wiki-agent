## Summary
REBOL (Relative Expression Based Object Language) is a multi-paradigm scripting language designed by Carl Sassenrath, first released in 1997, blending functional, prototype-based object-oriented, and metaprogramming styles. It is dynamically typed and homoiconic, with a rich set of built-in datatypes (including URLs, emails, dates, tuples, and money) and the distinctive concept of dialects—domain-specific sublanguages embedded directly in the code—built on the language's deep "code as data" foundation. Lightweight and interpreted, REBOL was created for distributed network communication, data exchange, and cross-platform GUI and scripting tasks, emphasizing extreme conciseness and a tiny runtime footprint. Its open-source successor, Red, carries the same design philosophy forward.
