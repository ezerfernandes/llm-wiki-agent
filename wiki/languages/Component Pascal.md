## Summary
Component Pascal is a statically and strongly typed, object-oriented programming language developed by Oberon microsystems in the late 1990s as a refinement of Niklaus Wirth's Oberon-2, itself a descendant of Pascal and Modula-2. It is an imperative, modular, component-oriented language featuring type-bound procedures (methods), garbage collection, single inheritance, and safe references, designed to support the development of robust software components that can be dynamically loaded and extended. The language is most closely associated with the BlackBox Component Builder IDE and framework, where it is used for building modular desktop applications, and it has seen continued use in education and in scientific and financial component-based systems.
