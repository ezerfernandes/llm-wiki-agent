## Summary
DBL (now usually marketed as Synergy DBL or Synergy/DE) is a statically typed, imperative business programming language descended from DEC's DIBOL (Digital Interactive Business Oriented Language), which originated on PDP-11 minicomputers in the 1970s. Like its COBOL-influenced ancestor, it was designed for data-processing and record-oriented business applications, pairing fixed-format declarations and ISAM file handling with strong support for decimal arithmetic. Modern DBL, developed by Synergex, has evolved into a procedural and object-oriented language that compiles to native code or runs on .NET and the JVM, while remaining largely backward compatible with legacy DIBOL/DBL code. It is used chiefly to maintain and modernize long-lived enterprise and ERP systems originally written for DEC and OpenVMS platforms.
