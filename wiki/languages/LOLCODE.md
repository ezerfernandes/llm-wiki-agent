## Summary
LOLCODE is an esoteric, imperative programming language created by Adam Lindsay in 2007, with a syntax modeled on the "lolcats" internet meme and its associated broken-English slang. It is dynamically and weakly typed, using keywords such as HAI and KTHXBYE to delimit programs, I HAS A to declare variables, and VISIBLE for output, with implicit coercion between its core types (numbers, strings, booleans, and the untyped NOOB). Though primarily a humorous novelty and teaching curiosity rather than a production language, it is fully Turing-complete and has spawned multiple interpreters and compilers, making it a common subject for parser experiments and playful programming exercises.
