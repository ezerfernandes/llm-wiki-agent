## Summary
Relation is a small interpreted scripting language built around the relational model, treating relations (sets of tuples, as found in relational databases) as a first-class data type alongside more conventional scalar values. It is dynamically typed and imperative in flavor, layering relational-algebra operations such as projection, selection, and join directly into the language's expression syntax. Originating in the late 2000s as a niche language showcased through Rosetta Code, it is used chiefly for exploratory data manipulation and teaching relational concepts rather than for general-purpose application development.
