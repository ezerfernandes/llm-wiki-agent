## Summary
Objeck is a general-purpose, object-oriented programming language created by Randy Hollines in the mid-2000s, drawing influence from Java, C#, and Scheme. It is statically and strongly typed, compiles to bytecode that runs on its own register-based virtual machine, and supports object orientation alongside functional features such as closures, higher-order functions, and first-class lambdas. The language ships with garbage collection, a sizable standard library covering collections, regular expressions, networking, and databases, and was designed primarily as a practical teaching and general application language rather than for a single specialized niche.
