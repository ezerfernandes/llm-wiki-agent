## Summary
Pure Data (Pd) is a visual, dataflow programming language created by Miller Puckette in the mid-1990s as an open-source successor to his earlier Max environment. Programs (called "patches") are built by placing objects on a canvas and wiring them together with patch cords, so control and signal flow rather than textual statements drive execution; data is dynamically typed, flowing as messages, numbers, symbols, and audio signal blocks. It is primarily used for real-time audio synthesis and processing, computer music, and interactive multimedia and installation art, where its signal-graph model maps naturally onto digital signal processing. Extensions such as GEM add video and graphics, broadening its reach beyond sound into general live media performance.
