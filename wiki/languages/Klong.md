## Summary
Klong is an array programming language in the APL/K lineage, created by Nils M. Holm around 2015 and designed to be simpler and more rigorously specified than Arthur Whitney's K. It is dynamically typed and follows a terse, function-level (point-free, combinatory) style in which programs are built by composing primitive functions and adverbs that operate uniformly over scalars, lists, and nested arrays, evaluated strictly right-to-left with no operator precedence. The language emphasizes a small, fully documented core and ships as a free reference interpreter, making it well suited to interactive data manipulation, mathematical and vector-oriented computation, and as a teaching vehicle for array-programming concepts.
