## Summary
HSL (HALON Scripting Language) is a domain-specific, imperative scripting language embedded in the Halon email security and SMTP platform, which emerged in the late 2000s alongside the company's mail transport software. It is a dynamically typed language with a C- and PHP-influenced syntax, offering first-class functions, associative arrays, and a large standard library of built-in functions tailored to message inspection and manipulation. Its primary use is filtering, routing, rewriting, and policy enforcement on email as it passes through the MTA, where scripts hook into SMTP transaction stages to make per-message decisions. Outside of mail processing it is rarely used as a general-purpose language, though its Rosetta Code entries demonstrate its capacity for ordinary computation.
