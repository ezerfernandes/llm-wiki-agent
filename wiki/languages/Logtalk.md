## Summary
Logtalk is an object-oriented logic programming language created by Paulo Moura in 1998 that extends Prolog with a layer of code-encapsulation and code-reuse constructs while remaining backward compatible with standard Prolog. It adds objects, protocols (interfaces), categories (composable units of behavior), and both class-based and prototype-based inheritance, along with support for event-driven programming and high-level multi-threading, all on top of Prolog's declarative, dynamically typed, relational foundation. The language is implemented as a portable layer that runs on top of several standard-compliant Prolog systems rather than as a standalone compiler. It is used primarily for programming-in-the-large in logic-based and symbolic-AI applications, knowledge representation, and teaching modular logic programming.
