## Definition
A gravitational wave is a propagating distortion (ripple) in the fabric of spacetime, traveling at the speed of light, produced by the acceleration of massive objects. Gravitational waves are a key prediction of [[GeneralRelativity]].

## Key Points
- Generated by violent, asymmetric mass motions such as merging compact objects (black holes, neutron stars).
- Predicted by Einstein but extraordinarily weak and hard to detect, requiring exquisitely sensitive interferometers.
- **LIGO's first direct detection** on September 14, 2015 observed waves from two merging [[BlackHole]]s, confirming both Einstein's century-old prediction and the reality of black-hole coalescence.
- Opened the field of gravitational-wave astronomy, a new observational window complementing electromagnetic observations.
