## Definition
Visible light is the narrow [[ElectromagneticSpectrum]] band (~400–750 nm wavelength) that the human eye detects. It is produced by atomic and molecular vibrations and by electronic transitions within atoms and molecules.

## Key Points
- Red has the lowest frequency / longest wavelength; violet the highest frequency / shortest wavelength.
- Detected through electronic transitions in retinal molecules in the eye.
- A tiny slice of the full spectrum, yet the band most of physics-of-vision and optics concerns.

## Equations
- `c = f λ` with c = 3.00×10⁸ m/s; e.g., λ = 400–750 nm corresponds to f ≈ 4.0–7.5×10¹⁴ Hz.

## Applications
Human vision and perception, photosynthesis in plants, and optics (ray and wave optics depending on interaction scale).
