# Statistical Learning

A vast set of tools for *understanding* data — modeling the relation between inputs $X$ and an output $Y$ when supervised, or finding structure in $X$ alone when unsupervised. Coined as a field in the 1990s; blends [[Statistics|statistics]] with [[MachineLearning]] and computer science. The umbrella term framing [[islr-seventh-printing|ISLR]] and its technical companion [[ElementsOfStatisticalLearning|ESL]]. See [[SupervisedLearning]], [[UnsupervisedLearning]], [[BiasVarianceTradeoff]].
