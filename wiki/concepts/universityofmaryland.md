# University of Maryland (UMD)

Primary author affiliation on [[2605.08083-autotts]] (Tong Zheng, Ruibo Chen, Chenxi Liu, Tianyi Xiong, Heng Huang) — the [[AutoTTS]] paper on agentic discovery for test-time scaling. Also a contributor affiliation on [[2604.20987-cos-play|COS-PLAY]].

UMD's College Park CS department hosts [[HengHuang]]'s group, which has authored the [[2602.03845-parallel-probe|Parallel-Probe]] line that AutoTTS extends.
