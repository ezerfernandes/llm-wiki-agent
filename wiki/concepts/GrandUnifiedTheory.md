## Definition
A **Grand Unified Theory (GUT)** proposes that the [[FundamentalForces|fundamental forces]] are different manifestations of a single force at extreme energy. Having unified the electromagnetic and weak forces as the [[ElectroweakForce|electroweak]] force, GUTs aim to merge the **strong** force with the electroweak force; a **Theory of Everything (TOE)** would add gravity.

## Key Points
- **Force-strength convergence**: as energy rises (distances shrink), the relative strengths of the forces approach one another. The electroweak merger happens at ~100 GeV; the GUT scale (electroweak + strong) is ~10¹⁵ GeV; the TOE scale (adding gravity) is ~10¹⁹ GeV — far beyond any accelerator.
- **Symmetry breaking**: forces appear distinct at ordinary energy because an underlying symmetry is broken; at high energy the symmetry is restored and carriers behave identically. The Higgs mechanism is the electroweak example.
- **Testable prediction**: GUTs allow rare **[[ConservationLawsParticlePhysics|baryon-number-violating]]** processes such as proton decay, p → π⁰ + e⁺. Predicted proton lifetime ~10³¹ yr; experiments push the lower bound past 5×10³² yr without observing it.
- **Cosmological link**: the early universe reached GUT-scale energies, so the cosmic microwave background and large-scale structure offer indirect tests inaccessible to accelerators. Superstring theory (scale ~10⁻³⁵ m) is one TOE candidate.

## Equations / scales
- p → π⁰ + e⁺ (violates baryon and lepton-family number)
- GUT scale ≈ 10¹⁵ GeV; TOE scale ≈ 10¹⁹ GeV; predicted proton lifetime ~10³¹ yr
