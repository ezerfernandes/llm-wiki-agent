# Feature Quality

The fitness of engineered features for modeling — relevance, coverage, freshness, consistency between training and serving. Poor quality manifests as training/serving skew, [[DataDrift]], or [[DataLeakage]]; monitored via [[DataObservability]] and audited inside a [[FeatureStore]].
