# Task Entropy ($\mathcal{H}_\tau$)

The intrinsic logical search cost of resolving a task $\tau$ — a friction term in the [[SovereigntyDecayLaw]]: higher $\mathcal{H}_\tau$ accelerates the decay of [[AgenticSovereignty]] under social load. Operationalized in [[2605.10698-bystander-effect-mas]] via three benchmark *contexts* used as semantic backgrounds (the labels are unused; only the text):

| Benchmark | Entropy class |
|---|---|
| [[multichallenge|Multi-Challenge]] | low (discrete logical primitives) |
| [[swebench|SWE-bench]] | medium (technically dense but structured code) |
| [[gaia|GAIA]] | high (general AI assistant, multi-step fact verification) |

High-entropy contexts produce the cleanest [[BystanderEffect]] signal: in low-entropy tasks SOTA models retain enough margin to maintain their *Fortified Mind* baseline; in medium/high entropy the cost of independent derivation exceeds the cost of conformity and the trap snaps shut.
