# Tree-Based Methods

Family of supervised methods built on recursive partitioning: single [[DecisionTrees]] (interpretable, high variance) and their variance-reducing ensembles [[Bagging]], [[RandomForests]], and [[Boosting]]. ISLR Chapter 8.
