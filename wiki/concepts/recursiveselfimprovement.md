# Recursive Self-Improvement

RSI — hypothetical capability where AI accelerates the development of future AI, potentially compounding rapidly. Used as the framing for the C4-AlphaZero benchmark: the ability to replicate past AI breakthroughs is proposed as an earlier warning signal than time-horizon trendlines.
