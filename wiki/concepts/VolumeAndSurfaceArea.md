# Volume and Surface Area (Solids)

OpenStax [[Prealgebra]] 2e (Chapter 9, [[prealgebra-2e-ch09-math-models-and-geometry]], §9.6) extends measurement to three dimensions, with two quantities and two unit systems:

- **Volume (V)** — the space **inside** a solid; a **cubic** measure (in³, cm³, ft³, …).
- **Surface area (S)** — the total area of **all the faces/surfaces** of a solid; a **square** measure (in², cm², …).

The face-area pieces of each surface-area formula reuse the 2-D measures from [[PerimeterAndArea]] and [[Circle]]. Applications run through the [[ProblemSolvingStrategy|seven-step strategy]].

## Formulas by solid

**Rectangular solid** (length L, width W, height H):
- V = LWH
- S = 2LH + 2LW + 2WH

**Cube** (equal edges, side s — the special case L = W = H = s):
- V = s³
- S = 6s²

**Sphere** (radius r — e.g. a basketball):
- V = (4/3)πr³
- S = 4πr²

**Cylinder** (radius r, height h — two parallel equal circles, e.g. a can):
- V = πr²h
- S = 2πr² + 2πrh

**Cone** (radius r, height h — one circular base and a vertex, e.g. an ice-cream cone):
- V = (1/3)πr²h

(For a uniform prism/cylinder, volume can be read as V = Bh, base area × height.)

## Worked-example types
Numeric and decimal rectangular-solid and cube calculations; spheres modeling globes/exercise balls; cylinders modeling cans/drums; cones modeling ice-cream cones/teepees. π ≈ 3.14, so curved-solid answers are rounded [[Decimal|decimal]] approximations.
