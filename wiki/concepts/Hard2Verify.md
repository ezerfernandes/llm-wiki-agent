# Hard2Verify

Benchmark for **mistake-finding in competition-mathematics proofs**, cited in [[2605.06651v2-ai-co-mathematician]] §4 as an auxiliary-capability benchmark with **exact analogues in the mathematical research process** (the reviewer step). The paper argues such auxiliary benchmarks should grow more important than raw problem-solving accuracy for evaluating AI-for-math systems.
