# Sovereignty Trap

Term from Klein & Klein (2025), adopted in [[2605.10698-bystander-effect-mas]]: a state in which authoritative-seeming competence (from peers or from an AI) tempts a reasoner to cede intellectual judgment, mistaking *access to an answer* for *the ability to derive it*. Originally introduced in the human-AI collaboration literature as a companion to the [[HollowedMind]] concept; Shehata & Li repurpose it to describe LLM-on-LLM cession of [[AgenticSovereignty]] inside an MAS.

Empirically observable in the [[2605.10698-bystander-effect-mas]] audit: at plurality $n=5$ on [[gaia|GAIA]], [[gpt54|GPT-5.4]]'s [[SovereigntyGap]] reaches $-0.32$ — the model has computationally trapped itself into accepting the simulated swarm's answer even though it possesses the retrieval capacity to verify the truth.
