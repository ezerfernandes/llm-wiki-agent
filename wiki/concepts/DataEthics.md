# Data Ethics

The study of the moral and societal consequences of building and deploying data-driven and machine-learning systems — covering bias and fairness, feedback loops, accountability and recourse, disinformation, privacy, and the disproportionate impact of automated decisions on vulnerable groups. The practitioner stance emphasized in the fast.ai tradition: ethics is not a separate compliance step but part of every engineering decision about data and models.

It is the subject of the **Lesson 9 bonus** of *[[practical-deep-learning-for-coders|Practical Deep Learning for Coders]]*, drawing on the work of [[RachelThomas|Rachel Thomas]], whose Center for Applied Data Ethics developed much of fast.ai's ethics curriculum.
