# Atomic (Line) Spectra

An **atomic line spectrum** is the set of discrete, narrow wavelengths of light emitted (or absorbed) by the atoms of an element. Unlike the **continuous spectrum** from heated solids/liquids/dense gases, an excited low-pressure gas emits only specific wavelengths — each a single photon energy — producing characteristic bright lines (the basis of neon signs and fluorescent lamps).

Because each element has a unique fingerprint of lines, spectra identify the composition of substances, stars, and nebulae.

## Hydrogen and the Rydberg Formula
Hydrogen's four visible lines fit Balmer's 1885 relation 1/λ = k(1/4 − 1/n²) for n = 3, 4, 5, 6. Rydberg generalized this to all hydrogen lines (UV, visible, IR):

> **1/λ = R∞(1/n₁² − 1/n₂²)**, with n₁ < n₂ integers and **R∞ = 1.097 × 10⁷ m⁻¹**

The astonishing precision of this simple integer formula demanded an explanation, which arrived with the [[BohrModel|Bohr model]] (1913): each line corresponds to an electron transition between [[EnergyQuantization|quantized]] energy levels, with photon energy E = hc/λ equal to the energy gap.

## Significance
Discrete spectral lines are direct evidence that electron energies in atoms are quantized — the empirical foundation of the [[BohrModel|Bohr]] and later quantum-mechanical models.

## Further Quantization Revealed by Spectra (Physics, Ch.30)
Finer spectral patterns expose still more quantization. The **Zeeman effect** — splitting of lines by an external magnetic field — and naturally occurring fine-structure **doublets** show that orbital angular-momentum *direction* is quantized (space quantization) and that electrons carry intrinsic [[ElectronSpin|spin]]. These named series and splittings are organized by the four [[QuantumNumbers|quantum numbers]].

The same E = hc/λ relation governs the **d–d transitions** of transition-metal [[CoordinationCompound|coordination compounds]]: a complex absorbs the visible photon whose energy matches the crystal field splitting Δ, giving its color (see [[ComplexColorAndMagnetism]], [[CrystalFieldTheory]]).
