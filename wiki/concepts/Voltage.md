## Definition
**Voltage** is the everyday name for [[PotentialDifference|potential difference]] — the change in [[ElectricPotential|electric potential]] (energy per unit [[ElectricCharge|charge]]) between two points: ΔV = ΔPE/q. It is always defined **between two locations**, never at a single point, and is measured in volts (1 V = 1 J/C).

## Key Points
- A battery's voltage rating does not by itself determine its stored energy — total charge moved also matters (a motorcycle and a car battery can both be 12 V).
- In a uniform [[ElectricField]] between parallel plates, voltage and field are simply related: V = Ed.
- Air breaks down near 3.0 × 10⁶ V/m, which caps the voltage sustainable across a given gap (relevant to arcing in transmission lines and [[Capacitor|capacitors]]).
- Powers everyday systems: 120 V household outlets, hundreds of kV on transmission lines, ~70 mV across cell membranes.

## Equations
- ΔV = ΔPE / q
- V = E d  (uniform field, d = separation)
- 1 V = 1 J/C
- 1 N/C = 1 V/m
