## Definition
A velocity selector is a region with mutually perpendicular (crossed) [[ElectricField]] and [[MagneticField]] that passes only charged particles moving at one specific speed. It is a common front-end for a [[MassSpectrometer]].

## Key Points
- The electric force qE and the magnetic force qvB act in opposite directions; they balance only at one velocity.
- Particles at that velocity travel straight through; faster or slower particles are deflected and removed.
- The selected speed is independent of charge and mass — it depends only on the field strengths.

## Equations
- Force balance: qE = qvB
- Selected velocity: v = E / B
