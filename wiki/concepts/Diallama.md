# Dia-LLaMA

*Stub — referenced by ECG-Chat as a radiology precedent for diagnosis-driven prompting on the LLaMA family.*

**Chen, Luo, Bie, Hu (2024) — *"Dia-llama: Towards large language model-driven CT report generation."*** A LLaMA-based MLLM for CT report generation that uses diagnosis-driven prompts in the same family as [[Promptmrg]]. Cited by [[2408.08849-ecg-chat|ECG-Chat]] alongside Promptmrg as a precursor for [[DiagnosisDrivenPrompt|DDP]] adapted from radiology to ECG.
