# Behavioral Testing

Testing an ML model against curated input/output expectations grouped by capability (invariance, directional expectation, minimum functionality) rather than aggregate accuracy. Popularized by the CheckList framework; complements [[IntegrationTesting]] and surfaces failure modes hidden by single-number metrics like [[F1Score]].
