# Neutron

A **neutron** is an uncharged (electrically neutral) subatomic particle found in the [[AtomicNucleus|nucleus]] of an [[Atom]]. Its mass (≈1.00866 amu, 1.67493 × 10⁻²⁴ g) is approximately equal to that of a [[Proton]] and far greater than that of an [[Electron]].

[[JamesChadwick]] discovered the neutron in 1932 (see [[EvolutionOfAtomicTheory]]). Detecting it had been difficult precisely because it carries no charge. Its discovery resolved a puzzle: protons account for only about half or less of the nuclear mass, and neutrons supply the remainder.

## Explaining Isotopes

Neutrons explain the existence of [[Isotope|isotopes]]: atoms of the same element have the same [[AtomicNumberAndMassNumber|atomic number]] (proton count) and thus identical chemistry, but can have different numbers of neutrons and therefore different masses. The number of neutrons equals the mass number minus the atomic number (A − Z).
