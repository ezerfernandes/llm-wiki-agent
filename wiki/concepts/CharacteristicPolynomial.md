# Characteristic Polynomial

$p_\mathbf{A}(\lambda) = \det(\mathbf{A}-\lambda\mathbf{I})$ ([[mml-book]] §4.2) — roots are eigenvalues. The algebraic route to [[Eigendecomposition]]; coefficients include $\text{tr}(\mathbf{A})$ and $\det(\mathbf{A})$.
