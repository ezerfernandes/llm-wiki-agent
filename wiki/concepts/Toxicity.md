Toxicity refers to context-dependent offensive, rude, or harmful language that an LM may generate. It is measured with tools such as the [[PerspectiveAPI]] over benchmarks like [[RealToxicityPrompts]], and mitigated through data-based and decoding-based detoxification methods.
