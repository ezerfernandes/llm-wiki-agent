## Definition
The period (T) is the time required to complete one full cycle of a periodic motion or wave, measured in seconds. It is the reciprocal of [[Frequency]].

## Key Points
- In [[SimpleHarmonicMotion]] the period depends only on mass and stiffness, not amplitude.
- For a mass-spring oscillator, T = 2π √(m/k); for a simple [[Pendulum]], T = 2π √(L/g).
- For a [[Wave]], the period is the time for one full oscillation of a medium particle and relates wavelength to speed: v_w = λ/T.

## Equations
- T = 1/f
- Mass-spring: T = 2π √(m/k)
- Pendulum: T = 2π √(L/g)
