# Proportion (and the Cross Products Property)

A **proportion** is an [[Equation|equation]] stating that two [[Ratio|ratios]] (or rates) are equal:

> **a/b = c/d**,  where `b ≠ 0` and `d ≠ 0`

read aloud as "`a` is to `b` as `c` is to `d`." OpenStax [[Prealgebra]] 2e (Chapter 6, [[prealgebra-2e-ch06-percents]], §6.5) introduces proportions and uses them as a second route through every percent problem. As an equation between two [[Fraction|fractions]], a proportion is solved with ordinary algebra ([[Equation|Properties of Equality]]).

## Cross Products Property

For any proportion `a/b = c/d`, the **cross products** (each denominator times the *opposite* numerator) are equal:

> **a · d = b · c**

This is the key test and tool:
- **Is an equation a true proportion?** Compute the two cross products; the equation is a proportion **iff** they are equal.
- **Solving a proportion** — two cases:
  - **Variable in a numerator** — multiply both sides by the LCD of the denominators (i.e. clear fractions), simplify, divide out common factors, then check by substitution.
  - **Variable in a denominator** — set the cross products equal, simplify the resulting [[Equation|equation]], divide to isolate the variable, then check.

## Applications
Proportions model "this rate holds at a different scale" problems: medication dosing, calories/nutrition, currency exchange, map scales, unit pricing (see [[Ratio|rates and unit rates]]). Set up two equal ratios with **like quantities in matching positions** (same units in each numerator, same units in each denominator), put the unknown where it belongs, and solve by cross products.

## Percent proportion
A [[Percent|percent]] problem can be written as a proportion instead of as the percent equation `amount = percent × base`:

> **amount / base = percent / 100**

read "the amount is to the base as the percent is to 100." Identify the **base** (the word **"of"** points to it), the **amount**, and the **percent**, place the unknown, and solve by cross products. This handles all three percent problem types — finding the amount, the base, or the percent — including percents greater than 100 and decimal/monetary percents.

- [[Percent]] — the percent proportion `amount/base = percent/100` is the proportion form of the percent equation.
