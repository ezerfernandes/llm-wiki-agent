# Kubeflow

An open-source ML platform on Kubernetes providing pipelines, [[HyperparameterTuning]] (Katib), distributed training operators (PyTorchJob, MPIJob), and serving. Direct alternative to managed platforms like [[AnyscaleJobs]]/[[AnyscaleServices]] for teams that want to run on their own cluster.
