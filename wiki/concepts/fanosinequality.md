# Fano's Inequality

Information-theoretic lower bound (Cover & Thomas 2006). Xu et al. use it to prove that under bounded-context composition with log|H| > K log|Y|, retrieval-based ¯α < 1 holds for any operator class — not merely as an empirical claim.
