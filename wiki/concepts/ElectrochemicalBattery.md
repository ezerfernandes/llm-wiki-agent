# Battery (Electrochemical)

> Disambiguation: this is the *chemistry* concept — a [[GalvanicCell|galvanic cell]] engineered as a power source. (The physics electricity chapters use "battery" loosely as an emf source; this page covers the electrochemical cell chemistry.)

A **battery** is a galvanic cell (or set of cells) specially designed and constructed to serve as a portable source of electrical power for a specific application. It converts the chemical energy of a spontaneous [[OxidationReduction|redox]] reaction into electrical work.

## Primary vs Secondary
- **Primary (single-use):** the cell reaction is not conveniently reversible; cannot be recharged.
- **Secondary (rechargeable):** the cell reaction is reversible, so an external power source can recharge it.

## Common Batteries
### Zinc-Carbon Dry Cell — primary, ~1.5 V
Zinc can (anode), graphite rod (cathode), paste of MnO₂/ZnCl₂/NH₄Cl/water.
- Anode: Zn(s) → Zn²⁺(aq) + 2e⁻
- Cathode: 2MnO₂(s) + 2NH₄Cl(aq) + 2e⁻ → Mn₂O₃(s) + 2NH₃(aq) + H₂O(l) + 2Cl⁻
- Sizes D/C/AA/AAA share voltage; larger cells just hold more reactant (more charge).

### Alkaline — primary, +1.43 V
KOH electrolyte; ~3–5× the energy of a same-size zinc-carbon cell. Prone to KOH leakage; not safely rechargeable.
- Anode: Zn(s) + 2OH⁻(aq) → ZnO(s) + H₂O(l) + 2e⁻
- Cathode: 2MnO₂(s) + H₂O(l) + 2e⁻ → Mn₂O₃(s) + 2OH⁻(aq)

### Nickel-Cadmium (NiCd) — secondary, ~1.2 V
KOH electrolyte, "jelly-roll" design; ~1000 recharge cycles. Cadmium is toxic — dispose as toxic waste.
- Anode: Cd(s) + 2OH⁻(aq) → Cd(OH)₂(s) + 2e⁻
- Cathode: NiO₂(s) + 2H₂O(l) + 2e⁻ → Ni(OH)₂(s) + 2OH⁻(aq)

### [[LithiumIonBattery|Lithium-Ion]] — secondary, ~3.7 V
Dominant in portable electronics: high current, light, near-constant discharge voltage.

### [[LeadAcidBattery|Lead-Acid]] — secondary, ~2 V/cell
Automotive starter batteries (12 V from six cells in series).

## Fuel Cells
A [[FuelCell|fuel cell]] is a related galvanic device, but fuel and oxidant are fed in **continuously** rather than sealed inside.
