# Agentic Harness

Software wrapping an LLM with memory, skills, tools, and an orchestrator loop. Examples cited: Claude Code, Codex, OpenClaw, Hermes-Agent. HEAVYSKILL argues the apparent gains often come from an internalizable parallel-reason → deliberate pattern, not the harness itself.
