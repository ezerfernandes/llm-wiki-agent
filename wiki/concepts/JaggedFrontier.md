# Jagged Frontier

Term (Dell'Acqua et al.) referenced in [[2605.06651v2-ai-co-mathematician]] §4 for the **uneven capability surface** of frontier AI: tasks of seemingly comparable human difficulty can fall on opposite sides of the line of AI competence. The paper invokes the term to motivate **broader evaluation** of interactive mathematics agents — relying on a single static benchmark can mask large swings just past the benchmark's frontier.
