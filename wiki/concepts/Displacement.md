## Definition
Displacement is the change in position of an object, measured relative to a [[ReferenceFrame]]. It is a [[VectorQuantity]] with both magnitude and direction, and depends only on the initial and final positions — not the path taken.

## Key Points
- Position is an object's location relative to a chosen reference frame.
- [[Distance]] is the magnitude of displacement (a scalar); displacement also carries direction.
- "Distance traveled" is the total path length and can exceed the magnitude of displacement when the object reverses direction.
- In 1D, direction is shown with a positive or negative sign; the choice of positive direction is arbitrary but must remain fixed within a problem.
- The symbol Δ ("delta") means "change in."

## Equations
- Δx = x_f − x₀  (1D displacement; x₀ = initial position, x_f = final position)
