# T5

**Text-to-Text Transfer Transformer** (Raffel et al., 2020) — an encoder-decoder Transformer trained with a span-corruption denoising objective on the [[c4]] corpus, then fine-tuned on downstream tasks cast in a unified [[texttotextframework]]: every task, including classification and regression, becomes string→string with a task-prefix prompt.

Introduced in [[1910.10683-t5]]. The model family spans five sizes — **Small** (60M), **Base** (220M), **Large** (770M), **3B**, **11B** — all sharing the same recipe; the headline state-of-the-art results (GLUE 90.3, SuperGLUE 88.9, SQuAD F1 96.22, 18/24 task SOTA) come from T5-11B.

## What is novel

T5's primary contribution is *not* a new architecture or objective — both are minor variations on the standard [[transformer]] and on BERT-style [[maskedlanguagemodel]] denoising — but a controlled, large-scale ablation that fixes the recipe for transfer learning in NLP:

1. **Encoder-decoder beats decoder-only and prefix-LM** at matched compute on text-to-text tasks.
2. **Denoising beats causal LM** for pre-training regardless of architecture.
3. **Among denoising variants, all behave similarly** — pick the cheapest. T5 chose [[spancorruption]] (replace mean-length-3 spans at 15% rate) for shorter target sequences.
4. **C4 + heuristic filtering > Wikipedia / RealNews / unfiltered C4** for general transfer.
5. **Full fine-tuning > adapter / gradual unfreezing** on this scale.
6. **Examples-proportional or T=2 temperature-scaled multi-task pre-training + per-task fine-tuning** ≈ unsupervised pre-train then fine-tune.
7. **Scale dominates** — every careful method tweak yielded ≤1 point; doubling compute yielded several.

## Architectural details

- Standard encoder-decoder [[transformer]] with two minor deviations: LayerNorm bias removed and placed outside the residual path; relative-position embedding replaced with a single shared scalar added to attention logits, bucketed log-scale up to offset 128.
- [[sentencepiece]] WordPiece tokenizer, 32k vocab, trained on a 10:1:1:1 EN:DE:FR:RO mixture of C4.
- AdaFactor optimizer; inverse-sqrt LR schedule for pre-training; constant LR 0.001 for fine-tuning.
- Sentinel tokens (extra vocabulary entries) mark the spans corrupted by [[spancorruption]].

## From [[hands-on-llm-ch04-text-classification|*Hands-On LLMs* Ch 4]]

Ch 4 introduces T5 as the chapter's **encoder-decoder generative-model archetype** and uses [[FLANT5|Flan-T5]]-small for [[GenerativeClassification|generative classification]] (F1 = 0.84 on [[RottenTomatoes|Rotten Tomatoes]]).

The chapter's two-paragraph T5 sketch surfaces:

- **Encoder-decoder structure** — *"its architecture is similar to the original Transformer where 12 decoders and 12 encoders are stacked together."* (T5-Base size.)
- **[[spancorruption|Span-corruption]] pretraining** — *"instead of masking individual tokens, sets of tokens (or token spans) were masked during pretraining."*
- **Multi-task fine-tuning via [[texttotextframework|text-to-text framing]]** — *"each task is converted to a sequence-to-sequence task and trained simultaneously."* This generalizes naturally to Flan-T5's 1,800+ task instruction tuning (Chung et al., 2022).

In Hugging Face Transformers, T5 / Flan-T5 are loaded via the **`text2text-generation`** pipeline (distinct from `text-generation` used for decoder-only models) — a visible architectural marker.
