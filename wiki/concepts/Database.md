# Database

A structured persistent store optimized for transactional or analytical access — relational (Postgres, MySQL), columnar (ClickHouse), document (MongoDB), or vector. Often sits behind a [[DataPipeline]] alongside [[DataLake]] and [[DataWarehouse]] tiers.
