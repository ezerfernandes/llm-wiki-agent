## Definition
Beats are the periodic amplitude fluctuations (loud–quiet cycles) heard or measured when two [[Wave]]s of slightly different frequencies undergo [[Superposition]].

## Key Points
- The combined wave is a carrier at the average frequency modulated by a slow envelope; the envelope produces alternating constructive and destructive [[WaveInterference]].
- The beat frequency equals the absolute difference of the two source frequencies.
- Listening for beats is a sensitive way to compare frequencies — used to tune pianos and instruments against a reference (no beats means matched frequencies).
- Some instruments (12-string guitars, mandolins) are deliberately slightly detuned to create pleasant slow beats.

## Equations
- Resultant: x = 2X cos(π f_B t) cos(2π f_ave t)
- Beat frequency: f_B = |f₁ - f₂|
- Average frequency: f_ave = (f₁ + f₂)/2
