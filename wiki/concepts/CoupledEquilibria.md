# Coupled Equilibria

**Coupled (simultaneous) equilibria** are "two or more separate chemical reactions that share one or more reactants or products." When the reactions are added, their equilibrium constants **multiply** (and reversing a reaction inverts its constant) — the same K-manipulation rules introduced for the [[EquilibriumConstant|equilibrium constant]] in Ch 13. Coupling a [[SolubilityProduct|solubility]] equilibrium with an acid–base or complex-formation reaction can change a solid's [[MolarSolubility|molar solubility]] by many orders of magnitude, because the second equilibrium removes one of the dissolution products and (via [[LeChatelierPrinciple|Le Châtelier's principle]]) pulls more solid into solution.

## Solubility coupled to acid–base (ocean acidification)

$$\mathrm{CaCO_3(s) \rightleftharpoons Ca^{2+} + CO_3^{2-}} \quad K_{sp} = 8.7 \times 10^{-9}$$

Dissolved CO₂ acidifies seawater (CO₂ → H₂CO₃ → HCO₃⁻ → CO₃²⁻, with Ka2 = 4.7 × 10⁻¹¹). Adding the dissolution to the reverse of the second [[AcidIonizationConstant|ionization]] gives:

$$\mathrm{CaCO_3(s) + H_3O^+ \rightleftharpoons Ca^{2+} + HCO_3^- + H_2O} \quad K = \frac{K_{sp}}{K_{a2}} = 180$$

K ≫ Ksp, so CaCO₃ (coral, shells) is much more soluble in acidic water. The same acid-driven dissolution attacks tooth-enamel **hydroxyapatite** Ca₅(PO₄)₃OH; substituting fluoride to form fluorapatite (with the weaker base F⁻) resists it.

## Solubility coupled to complex formation

$$\mathrm{Al(OH)_3(s) \rightleftharpoons Al^{3+} + 3\,OH^-} \quad K_{sp} = 2 \times 10^{-32}$$
$$\mathrm{Al^{3+} + 4\,OH^- \rightleftharpoons Al(OH)_4^-} \quad K_f = 1.1 \times 10^{33}$$
$$\mathrm{Al(OH)_3(s) + OH^- \rightleftharpoons Al(OH)_4^-} \quad K = K_{sp} \times K_f = 22$$

A [[FormationConstant|formation constant]] coupled to a [[SolubilityProduct|Ksp]] greatly increases solubility. In a pH-4.74 acetate buffer, Al(OH)₃ solubility rises from 5 × 10⁻⁹ M (pure water) to 1.2 × 10⁻⁴ M — roughly ten-million-fold. The same logic dissolves silver halides: photographic fixing uses thiosulfate, AgBr(s) + 2 S₂O₃²⁻ ⇌ Ag(S₂O₃)₂³⁻ + Br⁻ with K = Ksp × Kf = 24.
