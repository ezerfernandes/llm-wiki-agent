## Definition
**Gravitational potential energy** (PE_g) is energy stored in the Earth–object system because of the object's vertical position. Near Earth's surface its change is ΔPE_g = mgh. It is a property of the system, not of a single object, and only *differences* in it are physically meaningful — the zero reference level is chosen arbitrarily.

## Key Points
- Lifting an object does [[Work]] equal to the gravitational PE gained.
- **Path-independent:** ΔPE_g depends only on the change in height, not the route taken — a hallmark of a [[ConservativeForce]].
- As an object falls, PE_g converts to [[KineticEnergy]] (−ΔPE_g = ΔKE), letting one find speeds without force calculations.
- Combines with kinetic energy to form [[MechanicalEnergy]]; relies on a near-constant [[GravitationalField]] g.

## Equations
- ΔPE_g = m g h  (h positive upward, negative downward)
- W = F d = m g h  (work to lift)
- −ΔPE_g = ΔKE
- v = √(2 g |h|)  (from rest)
- v = √(2 g |h| + v₀²)  (with initial speed)
