# ECG-ExpertQA

A **123-pair domain-specific question-answer evaluation benchmark** for ECG-language models, released by [[2408.08849-ecg-chat|ECG-Chat]]. Each pair is automatically generated by ChatGPT-4o guided by *"real medical cases and expert knowledge,"* with **expert evaluation confirming effective assessment of large-model performance in domain-specific knowledge."*

Used in the paper's **GraphRAG × DSPy ablation** (Table VII), evaluated under [[RAGAS]] across seven metrics: Faithfulness (F), Answer Relevancy (AR), Context Recall (CR), Context Precision (CP), Context Utilization (CU), Context Entity Recall (CER), Summarization Score (SS).

## Headline finding from the ablation

| GraphRAG | DSPy | F | AR | CR | CP | CU | CER | SS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ✗ | ✗ | 39.87 | 32.56 | 9.03 | 29.82 | 29.13 | 24.67 | 18.94 |
| ✓ | ✗ | 76.60 | 68.29 | 32.67 | 67.53 | 64.39 | 57.00 | 54.40 |
| ✗ | ✓ | 71.57 | 63.93 | 27.57 | 62.81 | 59.54 | 53.35 | 47.72 |
| ✓ | ✓ | **82.12** | **74.29** | **39.44** | **73.18** | **88.92** | **73.10** | **81.83** |

GraphRAG alone dominates Faithfulness and Answer Relevancy; DSPy alone wins on Context Recall and Summarization Score. Together they reach 80+ on five of seven metrics.
