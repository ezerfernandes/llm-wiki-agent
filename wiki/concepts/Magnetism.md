## Definition
Magnetism is the set of physical phenomena arising from moving electric charge and the intrinsic magnetic moments of particles. It is one aspect of the unified electromagnetic interaction (see [[FundamentalForces]]), inseparable from electricity: all magnetic effects ultimately trace back to [[ElectricCurrent]] (moving [[ElectricCharge]]).

## Key Points
- Magnetic effects range from compass needles and refrigerator magnets to auroras, electric motors, generators, and MRI.
- Magnetic poles always occur in north-south pairs; isolated monopoles have never been observed (see [[MagneticFieldLines]]).
- Magnetic and electric forces are two manifestations of a single electromagnetic interaction, coupled through moving charges.
- Magnetism was known in antiquity (Magnesia) long before systematic study; the modern quantitative framework comes from [[AmperesLaw]] and the [[LorentzForce]].
