# Reproducibility in ML

The challenge that ML adds to plain [[Reproducibility]]: nondeterministic GPUs, data drift, randomness in [[WeightInitialization]] and shuffling. Mitigated by seeds, [[Versioning]], [[ModelRegistry]] artifacts, and [[Versioning]].
