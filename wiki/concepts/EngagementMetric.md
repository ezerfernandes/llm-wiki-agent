# Engagement Metric

A **depth-of-interaction metric**. Per [[ai-engineering-ch04-evaluate-ai-systems|*AI Engineering* Ch 4]]:

> "Others prioritize engagement metrics, like the number of conversations a user initiates per month or the duration of each visit — the longer a user stays on the app, the less likely they are to leave."

## Examples

- Conversations per month per user.
- Session duration.
- Messages per session.
- Features used per session.

## Trade-off with quality

Engagement can be gamed at the cost of user value. Ch 4: *"While an emphasis on stickiness and engagement metrics can lead to higher revenues, it may also cause a product to prioritize addictive features or extreme content."*
