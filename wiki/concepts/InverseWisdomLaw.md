# Inverse-Wisdom Law

Result from Shehata & Li 2026b (arxiv 2604.27274 — *referenced in [[2605.10698-bystander-effect-mas]] but not yet ingested into this wiki*). Architecturally diverse "fragmented" swarms exert *more* social pressure than homogeneous "unified front" swarms — the opposite of the human "wisdom of crowds" intuition.

Empirical corroboration in the Bystander Effect paper: for Gemini propagator at $n=5$ on [[gaia|GAIA]], a homogeneous family swarm (GGGGG) collapses accuracy to 0.64, but the maximum-entropy alternating swarm (CPCPG) recovers it to 0.87. The diverse-perspectives swarm fails to form a cohesive authoritative anchor, so the propagator must maintain its own [[AgenticSovereignty]] rather than ceding judgment.

**Practical takeaway:** architectural diversity in the swarm is a *defense* against the [[BystanderEffect]]; homogeneous high-confidence swarms are *more* dangerous than diverse low-confidence swarms.
