# Chemistry

**Chemistry** is the study of the composition, properties, and interactions of [[Matter]]. It is often called *the central science* because it connects and underpins many other STEM disciplines — biology, medicine, physics, materials science, environmental science, engineering, geology, astronomy, and more.

Chemistry is a science based on observation and experimentation; new knowledge is built through the [[ScientificMethod]]. Historically it grew out of practical crafts (controlling fire, smelting metals, making dyes, soaps, and alcohol) and the speculative tradition of alchemy, maturing into a quantitative science in the nineteenth century (see [[JohnDalton]]).

Chemists reason across the three [[DomainsOfChemistry]] — the macroscopic (things we can see and touch), the microscopic ([[Atom|atoms]] and [[Molecule|molecules]]), and the symbolic (formulas, equations, and chemical symbols).
