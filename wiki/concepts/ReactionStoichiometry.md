# Reaction Stoichiometry

**Stoichiometry** is the study of the quantitative relationships between the amounts of substances consumed and produced by a reaction (Greek *stoicheion*, element + *metron*, measure). A balanced [[ChemicalEquation|chemical equation]] carries both qualitative information (what reacts) and quantitative information (the relative amounts, via coefficients).

## Stoichiometric Factors (Mole Ratios)

The coefficients of a balanced equation give exact ratios between any two species — the **stoichiometric factors** (mole ratios) used as conversion factors. For N₂(g) + 3H₂(g) → 2NH₃(g), the NH₃-to-H₂ factor is **2 mol NH₃ / 3 mol H₂** (it holds for molecules, dozens, or moles alike).

## The Master Conversion Pathway

Most stoichiometry problems follow **mass A → moles A → moles B → mass B**:

1. Mass A ↔ moles A, using the [[MolarMass|molar mass]] of A.
2. Moles A ↔ moles B, using the **stoichiometric factor** from the balanced equation.
3. Moles B ↔ mass B, using the molar mass of B.

Side branches use other conversion factors: mass ↔ volume (density), moles ↔ solution volume ([[Molarity|molarity]]), moles ↔ particles ([[AvogadrosNumber|Avogadro's number]], 6.022 × 10²³), and — for gases — moles ↔ gas volume via the [[IdealGasLawChemistry|ideal gas law]] (see [[GasStoichiometry]]). Whatever the units, the indispensable step is the stoichiometric factor from the balanced equation — all of this is [[DimensionalAnalysis|dimensional analysis]].

For gaseous reactants and products measured at the same temperature and pressure, [[AvogadrosLaw|Avogadro's law]] makes the volume ratios equal to the coefficient mole ratios directly — e.g., in N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃, one volume of N₂ reacts with three volumes of H₂ to give two volumes of NH₃ (see [[GasStoichiometry]]).

## Worked Examples

- **Mole→mole** (2Al + 3I₂ → 2AlI₃): 0.429 mol Al × (3 mol I₂ / 2 mol Al) = **0.644 mol I₂**.
- **Mole→molecules** (C₃H₈ + 5O₂ → 3CO₂ + 4H₂O): 0.75 mol C₃H₈ × (3 mol CO₂ / 1 mol C₃H₈) × (6.022 × 10²³ / mol) = **1.4 × 10²⁴ CO₂ molecules**.
- **Mass→mass** (MgCl₂ + 2NaOH → Mg(OH)₂ + 2NaCl): 16 g Mg(OH)₂ × (1 mol / 58.3 g) × (2 mol NaOH / 1 mol Mg(OH)₂) × (40.0 g/mol) = **22 g NaOH**.
- **Mass→mass** (2C₈H₁₈ + 25O₂ → 16CO₂ + 18H₂O): 702 g C₈H₁₈ × (1 mol / 114.23 g) × (25 mol O₂ / 2 mol C₈H₁₈) × (32.00 g/mol) = **2.46 × 10³ g O₂**.
