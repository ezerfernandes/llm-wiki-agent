## Definition
Engineering practice unifying development and operations through automation, CI/CD, monitoring, and shared responsibility.

## In LLM Engineer's Handbook
DevOps is the discipline that unifies software development (Dev) and IT operations (Ops) through automation, continuous integration / continuous delivery, monitoring, and shared responsibility for deployed services. [[leh-ch11-mlops-and-llmops]] defines [[MLOps]] as 'the extension of the DevOps field that makes data and models its first-class citizen' — so DevOps is the operational parent discipline.
