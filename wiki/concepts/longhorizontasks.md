# Long-Horizon Tasks

Tasks requiring sequential decision-making over many turns. Defined precisely in [[2605.02572-long-horizon-llm-training]] as decoupling reasoning complexity from horizon length; instantiated at scale by the Synthetic Computers methodology (>2,000 turns / >8h runtime per simulation).
