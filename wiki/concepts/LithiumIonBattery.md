# Lithium-Ion Battery

The **lithium-ion battery** is a rechargeable (**secondary**) [[ElectrochemicalBattery|battery]] and one of the most popular for portable electronics. Charge/discharge shuttle Li⁺ ions between a lithium cobalt oxide cathode and a graphite (LiC₆) anode.

## Reactions (~3.7 V)
- **Cathode:** Li₁₋ₓCoO₂ + xLi⁺ + xe⁻ ⇌ LiCoO₂
- **Anode:** xLiC₆ ⇌ xLi⁺ + xe⁻ + xC₆
- **Overall:** Li₁₋ₓCoO₂ + xLiC₆ ⇌ LiCoO₂ + xC₆

The variable stoichiometry (typically x ≤ 0.5) makes the voltage vary somewhat with state of charge.

## Why It's Popular
- Delivers large current
- Lighter than comparable batteries
- Nearly constant voltage during discharge
- Slow self-discharge when stored
