## Definition
Quantum gravity is the sought-after theory that reconciles quantum mechanics with [[GeneralRelativity]], describing gravity as the exchange of a force-carrier particle (the **graviton**) and applying in regimes where both quantum and gravitational effects are essential.

## Key Points
- Needed where gravity and quantum effects coincide — for example near [[BlackHole]] event horizons (Hawking radiation arises from quantum pair production there) and in the earliest moments of the [[BigBang]].
- Suggests spacetime may be discrete at the smallest scales: there may be a minimum meaningful time interval, perhaps ~10⁻⁴³ s (possibly as short as 10⁻⁹⁵ s), implying time itself could be quantized.
- The relevant length scale is the **Planck scale** (~10⁻³⁵ m), where quantum gravity is expected to dominate.
- [[Superstring]] theory is the leading candidate framework for quantum gravity and a possible Theory of Everything, since it naturally includes the graviton.
- Unifying gravity with the other forces ([[StandardModel]], [[ElectroweakForce]], [[GrandUnifiedTheory]]) remains a central open problem of physics.

## Equations
- No standalone equation in this section; the minimum time scale ~10⁻⁴³ s is the Planck time order of magnitude.
