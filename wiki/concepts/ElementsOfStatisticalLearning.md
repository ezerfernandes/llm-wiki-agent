# The Elements of Statistical Learning (ESL)

Hastie, Tibshirani & Friedman (Springer, 2001; 2nd ed. 2009). Comprehensive technical reference for the field. Intended for readers with advanced mathematical training. The book that [[islr-seventh-printing|ISLR]] re-presents at a lower mathematical bar — same topical coverage, but with R labs and minimal matrix algebra.
