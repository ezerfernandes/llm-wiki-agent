## Definition
Frequency (f) is the number of complete cycles or events per unit time. For oscillations and waves it is the reciprocal of the [[Period]]; its SI unit is the hertz (Hz), one cycle per second.

## Key Points
- In [[SimpleHarmonicMotion]], frequency is set by mass and stiffness and is independent of amplitude.
- Musical pitch and ultrasound imaging are everyday frequency applications (e.g., middle C ≈ 264 Hz; diagnostic ultrasound ≈ 2.5 MHz).
- For a [[Wave]], frequency together with wavelength fixes propagation speed: v_w = f λ.
- The natural frequency f₀ is what a system oscillates at when undriven and undamped; driving at f₀ causes [[Resonance]].

## Equations
- f = 1/T
- Mass-spring: f = (1/2π) √(k/m)
- Wave relation: v_w = f λ
