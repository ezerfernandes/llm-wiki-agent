# Complementary Learning Systems

CLS theory (McClelland et al. 1995; O'Reilly et al. 2014): biological intelligence pairs fast hippocampal episodic storage with slow neocortical weight consolidation during sleep. The Xu/Dai/Zhang paper argues current AI agents implement only the hippocampal half — episodes accumulate, but no consolidation path to the neocortical half exists in any deployed system.
