# Slope

The **slope** of a line, denoted `m`, measures its steepness and direction as the **ratio of rise to run** — vertical change over horizontal change. It is the key quantitative feature of a line in the [[RectangularCoordinateSystem|coordinate plane]]. From OpenStax [[Prealgebra]] 2e Chapter 11 ([[prealgebra-2e-ch11-graphs]]). As a [[Ratio|ratio]] of changes, slope ties the chapter's geometry back to arithmetic with [[Integer|integers]] and [[Fraction|fractions]].

## Definition and formula
$$m = \frac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}} = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$$

for any two points `(x₁, y₁)` and `(x₂, y₂)` on the line — "y of the second point minus y of the first, over x of the second minus x of the first." Subtract the coordinates in the **same order** top and bottom.

## Finding slope from a graph
1. Pick two points with integer (lattice) coordinates.
2. Draw the right triangle whose hypotenuse joins them.
3. Count the **rise** (signed vertical leg) and **run** (signed horizontal leg).
4. Compute `m = rise/run`.

## Four cases
| Line | Slope |
|---|---|
| Slants **up** left-to-right | **positive** |
| Slants **down** left-to-right | **negative** |
| **Horizontal** `y = b` | **zero** (rise = 0) |
| **Vertical** `x = a` | **undefined** (run = 0 → division by zero) |

## Graphing from a point and the slope
Plot the given point, read `m` as rise/run, count off the rise and run to locate a second point, then draw the line. (See [[GraphingLinearEquations]], Method 3.)
