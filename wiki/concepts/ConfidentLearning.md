# Confident Learning

A framework (cleanlab) that estimates label noise by comparing predicted class probabilities to given labels, surfacing likely-mislabeled examples. Improves [[DataQuality]] and [[FeatureQuality]] without retraining and is complementary to active-learning loops with [[humanintheloop]] verification.
