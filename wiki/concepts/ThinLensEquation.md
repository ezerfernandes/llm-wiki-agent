## Definition
The thin lens equation relates the object distance `d_o`, image distance `d_i`, and focal length `f` of a thin lens: `1/d_o + 1/d_i = 1/f`. The same form applies to spherical mirrors ([[OpticalMirror]]).

## Key Points
- Sign conventions: `f > 0` for converging lenses, `f < 0` for diverging; `d_i > 0` for real images, `d_i < 0` for virtual images.
- **Magnification** `m = h_i/h_o = −d_i/d_o`: positive `m` is upright, negative is inverted; `|m| > 1` magnified, `|m| < 1` diminished.
- **Optical power** `P = 1/f` measured in diopters.
- Solving the equation predicts image position, size, orientation, and type, matching ray-tracing diagrams.
- Used for cameras, magnifiers, eyeglasses, and the human eye.

## Equations
- Thin lens / mirror equation: `1/d_o + 1/d_i = 1/f`
- Magnification: `m = h_i / h_o = − d_i / d_o`
- Optical power: `P = 1 / f` (diopters)
