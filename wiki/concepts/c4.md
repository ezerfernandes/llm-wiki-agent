# C4

The **Colossal Clean Crawled Corpus** — a 745 GB English pre-training corpus distilled from a single April 2019 Common Crawl dump via aggressive heuristic filtering, released alongside [[t5]] in [[1910.10683-t5]] and distributed via TensorFlow Datasets.

## Construction recipe

Starting from ~20 TB/month of raw Common Crawl HTML-extracted text, C4 applies the following filters in order:

- Retain only lines ending in terminal punctuation (`.`, `!`, `?`, closing quotation).
- Discard pages with fewer than 3 sentences; retain only lines with ≥5 words.
- Drop pages containing any word from the [LDNOOBW List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene or Otherwise Bad Words](https://github.com/LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words).
- Drop lines containing the literal "Javascript" (JS-required warnings).
- Drop pages containing "lorem ipsum".
- Drop pages containing a curly bracket `{` (proxies for source code).
- Strip citation markers (`[1]`, `[citation needed]`, …) — Wikipedia residue.
- Drop lines containing "terms of use", "privacy policy", "cookie policy", "uses cookies", "use of cookies", "use cookies".
- Three-sentence dedup: retain only one of any 3-sentence span appearing >1× across the corpus.
- Filter to English via `langdetect` with p ≥ 0.99.

The resulting corpus is **~745 GB**, ~7 × 10¹¹ tokens, orders of magnitude larger than prior NLP pre-training corpora (BERT used ~16 GB of Wikipedia + Toronto Books).

## Empirical findings about C4

From the ablation in [[1910.10683-t5]] §3.4:
- **Filtering matters.** Unfiltered C4 (6.1 TB) underperformed filtered C4 on every downstream task.
- **In-domain corpora can beat C4.** RealNews-like (35 GB), WebText-like (17 GB), and Wikipedia+TBC (20 GB) each beat C4 on matched downstream tasks (ReCoRD, several GLUE tasks, MultiRC respectively) — but at the cost of being substantially smaller and not generalizing as broadly.
- **Repetition is costly.** Training the baseline on a truncated 2²⁹-token slice of C4 (repeated 64× over pre-training) was nearly indistinguishable from full C4; repeating 2²³ tokens (4,096×) collapsed downstream performance, visible as memorization in the pre-training loss curve.
