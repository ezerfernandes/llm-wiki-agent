# Canary Deployment

Rolling out a new model version to a small slice of production traffic, monitoring health metrics, and gradually expanding if signals stay green. Often paired with [[ABTesting]] for statistical confirmation; native primitive in [[AnyscaleServices]] and [[KNative]].
