# Atomic Structure

The modern model of **atomic structure** describes an [[Atom]] as a tiny, dense, positively charged [[AtomicNucleus|nucleus]] — containing [[Proton|protons]] and [[Neutron|neutrons]] — surrounded by negatively charged [[Electron|electrons]]. The atom's diameter (~10⁻¹⁰ m) is about **100,000 times** larger than its nucleus (~10⁻¹⁵ m), so the atom is mostly empty space. This picture emerged from the [[EvolutionOfAtomicTheory|evolution of atomic theory]].

## Subatomic Particles

| Particle | Charge (C) | Unit charge | Mass (amu) | Mass (g) | Location |
|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------|---------|----------|
| [[Proton]] | +1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ | 1+ | 1.00727 | 1.67262 × 10⁻²⁴ | nucleus |
| [[Neutron]] | 0 | 0 | 1.00866 | 1.67493 × 10⁻²⁴ | nucleus |
| [[Electron]] | −1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ | 1− | 0.00055 | 0.00091 × 10⁻²⁴ | outside nucleus |

The **fundamental (elementary) charge** is e = 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C, the magnitude of an electron's charge. Masses are expressed in the **unified atomic mass unit (amu / dalton, Da)**, defined as exactly 1/12 of the mass of one carbon-12 atom = 1.6605 × 10⁻²⁴ g.

## Identity and Charge

- The [[AtomicNumberAndMassNumber|atomic number Z]] (proton count) defines the element; mass number A = protons + neutrons.
- A neutral atom has equal protons and electrons. If they differ, the atom is a charged [[ChemicalIon|ion]].
- Atoms of the same element with different neutron counts are [[Isotope|isotopes]].
