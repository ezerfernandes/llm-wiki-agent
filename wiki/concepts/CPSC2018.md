# CPSC2018

**Liu, Liu, Zhao, Zhang, Wu, Xu, Liu, Ma, Wei, He et al. (J. of Medical Imaging and Health Informatics, 2018) — *"An open access database for evaluating the algorithms of electrocardiogram rhythm and morphology abnormality detection."*** **6,877 standard 12-lead ECG records** annotated with 9 distinct labels (sinus rhythm + 8 abnormalities). Recording durations 6–60 s; sampling rate 500 Hz.

In [[2408.08849-ecg-chat|ECG-Chat]]:
- Pre-processing: records longer than 60 s truncated to 10 s; records shorter than 10 s zero-padded.
- Evaluation set for **ECG classification** alongside [[PTB-XL]] — paper reports F1 80.1 (CPSC2018) vs 52.8 (PTB-XL). The gap is informative: CPSC2018's 9-label space is much narrower than PTB-XL's 40+ SCP-Codes, so headline accuracy is higher but the discrimination task is easier.
