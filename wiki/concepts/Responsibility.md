# Responsibility

In a [[MixtureModel|mixture model]] with parameters $\boldsymbol\theta$, the responsibility of component $k$ for data point $\mathbf{x}_n$ is the **posterior probability** that $\mathbf{x}_n$ was generated by component $k$ ([[mml-book]] §11.2.1):

$$r_{nk} := \frac{\pi_k\,p_k(\mathbf{x}_n)}{\sum_{j=1}^K \pi_j\,p_j(\mathbf{x}_n)}.$$

For a [[GaussianMixtureModel|GMM]], $p_k = \mathcal{N}(\boldsymbol\mu_k,\boldsymbol\Sigma_k)$.

## Properties

- **Normalized**: $\sum_k r_{nk} = 1$ — $\mathbf{r}_n$ is a probability vector over components.
- **Boltzmann/Gibbs form**: $r_{nk}\propto \exp(\log\pi_k + \log p_k(\mathbf{x}_n))$ — the "softmax of energies."
- **Effective count**: $N_k := \sum_n r_{nk}$ is the (fractional) number of data points assigned to component $k$.

## Why responsibilities are the central quantity of EM

[[mml-book]] §11.2: every M-step update of the GMM parameters is a $r_{nk}$-weighted average:

$$\boldsymbol\mu_k^{\text{new}} = \frac{\sum_n r_{nk}\mathbf{x}_n}{N_k}, \quad \pi_k^{\text{new}} = \frac{N_k}{N}.$$

The mean update is interpretable as an **importance-weighted Monte Carlo estimate** ([[mml-book]] p. 354): each $\mathbf{x}_n$ contributes to $\boldsymbol\mu_k$ in proportion to how likely it was generated by component $k$.

## Soft vs hard assignment

- **Soft** (GMM-EM): $r_{nk}\in[0,1]$, a point belongs partially to multiple clusters.
- **Hard** ($k$-means): $r_{nk}\in\{0,1\}$, each point belongs to exactly one cluster.

The hard limit is the $\sigma\to 0$ limit of equal-covariance isotropic GMM. So $k$-means is GMM-EM with degenerate responsibilities — the source of its sharper but less expressive cluster boundaries.
