## Definition
Wave speed (v_w) is the rate at which a [[Wave]] disturbance propagates through a medium. Wavelength (λ) is the distance between successive identical points along the direction of propagation.

## Key Points
- Wave speed equals wavelength divided by [[Period]], equivalently wavelength times [[Frequency]].
- The propagation speed is generally set by the medium, not by the source; changing the driving frequency changes the wavelength to keep v_w fixed.
- Wave speed differs from the speed of an individual medium particle's oscillation.
- In a string fixed at both ends, higher tension raises v_w and thus all resonant frequencies (see [[StandingWave]]).

## Equations
- v_w = λ / T
- v_w = f λ
