# KNative

A Kubernetes-based platform for serverless workloads — scale-to-zero, [[Autoscaling]], and revision-based [[CanaryDeployment]] for HTTP services. Foundation for KServe model serving and an alternative deployment target alongside [[AnyscaleServices]] and [[Kubeflow]].
