# Organic Functional Group

An **(organic) functional group** is *the part or moiety of a molecule that imparts a specific chemical reactivity*. Functional groups are the **major determinants of an organic compound's chemical properties** and the basis on which organic compounds are **classified**: molecules sharing a functional group tend to share characteristic reactions, regardless of the rest of their carbon skeleton. (Named *OrganicFunctionalGroup* to avoid collision with the unrelated abstract-algebra / functional-programming senses of "functional group"; this page is strictly the organic-chemistry concept.)

For example, the **C–Cl** portion of chloroethane (CH₃CH₂Cl) is a functional group; so is the reactive **C=C** of an [[Alkene|alkene]] or the **C=O** carbonyl of an [[AldehydesAndKetones|aldehyde/ketone]].

## The main functional groups in Chapter 20

| Group | Name | Class | Suffix / form |
|---|---|---|---|
| C–C single bonds only | (saturated) | [[Alkane|alkane]] | -ane |
| C=C | alkene/double bond | [[Alkene|alkene]] | -ene |
| C≡C | alkyne/triple bond | [[Alkyne|alkyne]] | -yne |
| aromatic ring | aromatic | [[AromaticHydrocarbon|aromatic]] | (benzene derivatives) |
| –OH | hydroxyl | [[AlcoholsAndEthers|alcohol]] | -ol |
| –O– | ether | [[AlcoholsAndEthers|ether]] | (alkoxy) |
| –CHO | carbonyl (terminal) | [[AldehydesAndKetones|aldehyde]] | -al |
| –CO– | carbonyl (internal) | [[AldehydesAndKetones|ketone]] | -one |
| –COOH / –CO₂H | carboxyl | [[CarboxylicAcidsAndEsters|carboxylic acid]] | -oic acid |
| –CO₂R | ester | [[CarboxylicAcidsAndEsters|ester]] | -oate |
| C–N (lone pair) | amino | [[AminesAndAmides|amine]] | -ine |
| N on a carbonyl | amide | [[AminesAndAmides|amide]] | -amide |

A **substituent** (a branch or group replacing an H on a chain) may itself be a functional group; electronegative substituents take the "-o" suffix (chloro, fluoro) in nomenclature.
