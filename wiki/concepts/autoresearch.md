# Autoresearch

Loop where a coding agent iteratively proposes, evaluates, and refines code/models against a verifiable outcome. Examples: AlphaEvolve and related works. AGENTIC-IMODELS applies autoresearch to discover scikit-learn-compatible regressors that are interpretable to LLM agents.
