# Reinforcement Learning

Training paradigm where a policy is optimized against a scalar reward signal. In the corpus, RL is the unifying theme for LLM training advances: end-to-end coordination ([[2512.04388-conductor]]), pretraining-stage optimization ([[2601.21343-self-improving-pretraining]]), heavy-thinking inner skill ([[2605.02396-heavyskill]] via RLVR), and long-horizon agent training ([[2605.02572-long-horizon-llm-training]]).
