# Facebook Hate Speech Detection Model

A **specialized hate-speech classifier** from [[meta|Meta]]/Facebook. Cited by [[ai-engineering-ch04-evaluate-ai-systems|*AI Engineering* Ch 4]] as a canonical example of a cheap specialized [[Safety|safety]] classifier — *"much smaller, faster, and cheaper than general-purpose AI judges."*

Originally developed for moderating Facebook content, repurposable for AI-generated text. Sibling to [[PerspectiveAPI]] (Jigsaw) and [[SkolkovoToxicityClassifier]] (Skolkovo Institute).
