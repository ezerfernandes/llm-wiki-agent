# Matter Classification

All [[Matter]] can be classified by composition into two broad categories:

**Pure substances** have a constant composition; every specimen has the same makeup and properties regardless of source (e.g., sucrose is always 42.1% C, 6.5% H, 51.4% O by mass). Pure substances are either:
- **[[ChemicalElement|Elements]]** — cannot be broken into simpler substances by chemical change.
- **[[ChemicalCompound|Compounds]]** — two or more elements chemically combined; can be decomposed by chemical change.

**Mixtures** combine two or more types of matter in varying amounts and can be separated by physical means (e.g., evaporation):
- **Homogeneous mixtures (solutions)** — uniform composition throughout (air, salt water, sports drinks, gasoline).
- **Heterogeneous mixtures** — composition varies from point to point (Italian dressing, granite, chocolate-chip cookies).

**Decision flow:** Does it have constant properties/composition? *No* → mixture (uniform? *No* → heterogeneous; *Yes* → homogeneous). *Yes* → pure substance (chemically simplifiable? *No* → element; *Yes* → compound).
