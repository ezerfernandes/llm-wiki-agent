# Proactiveness

**Proactiveness** is the agent characteristic of **initiating actions toward its goals** rather than waiting to be told what to do at each step. One of the defining features of an [[AgenticAI|agentic system]] in [[AgenticDesignPatterns|*Agentic Design Patterns*]] (Gulli): a proactive agent can anticipate future needs and act on them — e.g., a customer-inquiry agent that proactively resolves an issue and anticipates follow-up needs rather than answering a single scripted question.

## How it works
Proactiveness builds on [[Autonomy|autonomy]] and [[GoalOriented|goal-orientation]]: given a goal, the agent decides on its own when to take an action that advances it. In the book's future hypotheses, this matures into **proactive goal discovery** — agents that learn a user's latent goals and "take initiative on your behalf when highly confident that the action will be helpful" (e.g., suggesting relevant courses or summarizing research for an emerging interest).

## Why it matters
Proactive behavior is what turns an agent from a passive tool into a partner/ally. It also raises the quality bar: unprompted actions are intrusive if low-quality — the same tension Chip Huyen frames on the [[ReactiveOrProactive|reactive/proactive product axis]], where proactive features carry a higher quality bar and higher failure cost.
