# Supply-Chain Security

Discipline borrowed from package-manager / OS history: typed artifacts, signed manifests, capability sandboxing, hash-chained audit, least-privilege gates. Metere argues skills are a new supply-chain surface and that signature plus clearance is not a substitute for behavioral verification.
