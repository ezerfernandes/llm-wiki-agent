# MuSR

**Multi-step Soft Reasoning** benchmark (Sprague et al. 2023). Per [[ai-engineering-ch04-evaluate-ai-systems|*AI Engineering* Ch 4]]:

> "MuSR (Sprague et al., 2023): a chain-of-thought, multistep reasoning benchmark."

## Position

Added to HuggingFace's [[OpenLLMLeaderboard|Open LLM Leaderboard]] in the **June 2024 refresh** alongside [[MATHLevel5]], [[MMLUPro]], [[GPQA]], [[BigBenchHard]], and [[IFEval]] — *"more challenging and focus on more practical capabilities."*
