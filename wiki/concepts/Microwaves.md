## Definition
Microwaves are the [[ElectromagneticSpectrum]] band of roughly 10⁹ to 10¹² Hz — shorter-wavelength than [[RadioWaves]] (hence the name). They are produced both by macroscopic circuit resonance and by thermal agitation of atoms and molecules.

## Key Points
- Require clear line-of-sight propagation.
- Polar molecules (notably water) absorb microwaves strongly via dielectric heating — microwave ovens operate near 2.45 GHz.
- Radar uses microwaves: the Doppler shift measures target velocity, and wavelength limits resolution.
- Penetrate atmospheres opaque to visible light (used to map Venus's surface).

## Equations
- `c = f λ`: e.g., a 1.90 GHz cell-phone signal → λ ≈ 0.158 m.

## Applications
Satellite and terrestrial communications, radar, microwave ovens, microwave diathermy (deep tissue heating), and planetary surface mapping.
