# CSV Format

Comma-Separated Values — a plain-text tabular format ubiquitous in data interchange. Easy to inspect but lacks types, schema, and compression; commonly replaced by [[Parquet]] or [[HDF5]] for serious analytics workloads loaded via [[pandas]].
