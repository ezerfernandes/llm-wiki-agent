# Anyscale Jobs

Managed batch job primitive on the Anyscale platform for running Ray workloads (training, [[BatchInference]], ETL) on autoscaling clusters. Complements [[AnyscaleServices]] (online serving) and [[AnyscaleWorkspaces]] (interactive dev) as the production execution surface in [[MLOps]] pipelines.
