# Distributed Computing

Coordinating work across many machines (or processes) to overcome single-node limits in compute, memory, or storage. Frameworks include Ray, Spark, Dask, and PyTorch DDP; foundation for [[DistributedTraining]], [[BatchInference]], and large-scale [[DataPipeline]] execution.
