# Spectrochemical Series

The **spectrochemical series** ranks [[Ligand|ligands]] by the size of the [[CrystalFieldTheory|crystal field splitting]] (**Δ_oct**) they induce in a [[CoordinationCompound|coordination compound]] — from **weak-field** (small Δ) to **strong-field** (large Δ):

> **I⁻ < Br⁻ < Cl⁻ < F⁻ < H₂O < NH₃ < en < NO₂⁻ < CN⁻**

- **Weak-field ligands** (left, e.g. halides, H₂O): small Δ_oct → favor **high-spin**, low-energy (long-wavelength) absorption → complexes appear blue-green/blue/indigo.
- **Strong-field ligands** (right, e.g. CN⁻, NO₂⁻): large Δ_oct → favor **low-spin**, high-energy (short-wavelength) absorption → complexes appear yellow/orange/red.

Δ_oct also increases with higher metal oxidation state and with larger d orbitals (3d < 4d < 5d), but for a fixed metal this series is the dominant control. Whether a given d⁴–d⁷ metal is high- or low-spin follows directly from where its ligands sit in the series versus the pairing energy P (Δ vs P; see [[CrystalFieldTheory]]).

Same Fe²⁺ (d⁶), opposite outcomes: weak-field **[Fe(H₂O)₆]²⁺** is high-spin/paramagnetic; strong-field **[Fe(CN)₆]⁴⁻** is low-spin/diamagnetic.
