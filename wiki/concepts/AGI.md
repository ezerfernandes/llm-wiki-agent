# AGI — Artificial General Intelligence

*Stub — referenced by other wiki pages but not yet ingested as a primary source.*

**Artificial General Intelligence** has no canonical definition; influential framings cited in [[2605.12966-agentic-ai-to-agi]] include:

- **Legg & Hutter (2007).** AGI is "an agent's ability to achieve goals in a wide range of environments."
- **Chollet (2019).** "The intelligence of a system is a measure of its skill-acquisition efficiency over a scope of tasks, with respect to priors, experience, and generalization difficulty."

These framings push toward a *cross-task generality* criterion. [[2605.12966-agentic-ai-to-agi]] formalizes this as requiring high performance across the [[StructuredRealWorldDistribution]] — a near-infinite union of low-dimensional task manifolds — and proves a monolithic learner cannot satisfy that criterion without paying the [[AverageTrap]] penalty and the [[CurseOfDimensionality]] in the ambient $D$. The paper's central claim is therefore that [[AgenticAI]] is a *foreseeable* (rather than only theoretically possible) pathway to AGI.
