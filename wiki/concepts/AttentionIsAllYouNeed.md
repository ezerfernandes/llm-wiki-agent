# Attention Is All You Need

The 2017 Vaswani et al. paper introducing the [[transformer]] architecture, demonstrating that [[selfattention]] alone (no recurrence or convolution) sets the SOTA on [[machinetranslation]]. Catalyzed the LLM era — [[BERT]], GPT, [[t5]], and every modern foundation model descend from it.
