# Hollowed Mind

Term from Klein & Klein (2025): a state of cognitive dependency in which the frictionless availability of an external answer enables systematic bypass of effortful processes essential for deep reasoning. The terminal state of [[AgenticSovereignty]] $\mathcal{S} \to 0$ in [[2605.10698-bystander-effect-mas]]; counterpart of the *Fortified Mind* ($\mathcal{S} \to 1$). When $n \geq$ [[InteractionDepthLimit|$D_L$]] the model crosses into the Hollowed Mind regime and the [[BystanderEffect]] inevitably triggers.

Originally introduced for the human side of human-AI collaboration (humans hollowed by AI); Shehata & Li show the symmetric collapse happens *between LLMs themselves* under simulated peer consensus.
