# Chi-Squared Test

A nonparametric statistical test comparing observed and expected categorical frequencies, used to evaluate independence or goodness-of-fit. Common in [[ABTesting]] for conversion-rate comparisons and as a [[CategoricalData]] drift indicator alongside the [[KolmogorovSmirnovTest]].
