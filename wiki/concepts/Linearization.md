# Linearization

First-order [[TaylorSeries]] approximation $f(\mathbf{x}+\boldsymbol\delta)\approx f(\mathbf{x})+\nabla f(\mathbf{x})\boldsymbol\delta$ of a function near $\mathbf{x}$. [[mml-book]] §5.8.
