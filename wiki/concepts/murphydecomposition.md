# Murphy Decomposition

Murphy (1973) decomposition of a strictly proper scoring rule into calibration (reliability) and discriminative power (resolution) components. Used by Nechepurenko & Shuvalov to extract distinguishable failure-mode signatures from multi-agent configurations even when aggregate scores match.
