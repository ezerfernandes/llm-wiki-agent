# LLM Fine-Tuning

Specialization of pretrained large language models via supervised fine-tuning, instruction tuning, [[rlhf]], or DPO. Often parameter-efficient via [[lora]] / [[adapterlayers]] to fit single-GPU budgets; targeted at task adaptation, style, safety, or domain knowledge.
