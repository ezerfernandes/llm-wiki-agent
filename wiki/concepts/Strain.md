## Definition
In mechanics, strain is the relative deformation of a material: the dimensionless ratio of the change in a dimension to its original value (e.g. ΔL/L₀). It is the response to applied [[Stress]]; in the elastic region the two are proportional (see [[HookesLaw]]).

## Key Points
- Dimensionless, since it divides a deformation by an original dimension.
- Forms for each [[Deformation]] mode: longitudinal strain ΔL/L₀, shear strain Δx/L₀, and volumetric strain ΔV/V₀.
- Related to stress by an elastic modulus: strain = stress / modulus.
- Distinct from statistical/ML usages of the word; this page is the physical fractional-deformation quantity.

## Equations
- Longitudinal: ΔL/L₀ = (1/Y)(F/A)
- Shear: Δx/L₀ = (1/S)(F/A)
- Volumetric: ΔV/V₀ = (1/B)(F/A)
