# Born-Haber Cycle

The **Born-Haber cycle** is a thermochemical cycle that applies [[HessLaw|Hess's law]] to calculate the [[LatticeEnergy|lattice energy]] of an ionic compound, which cannot be measured directly. It breaks the formation of an [[IonicCompound|ionic solid]] from its elements into a sequence of steps whose enthalpies are known.

## Steps

1. **ΔH_s°** — enthalpy of sublimation of the metal (solid → gaseous atoms)
2. **½D** — half the [[BondEnergy|bond dissociation energy]] of the nonmetal (to make gaseous atoms)
3. **IE** — [[IonizationEnergy|ionization energy]] of the metal (atom → cation)
4. **EA** — [[ElectronAffinity|electron affinity]] of the nonmetal (atom → anion)
5. **−ΔH_lattice** — gaseous ions combine into the solid

## Overall Relationship

$$\Delta H_f^\circ = \Delta H_s^\circ + \tfrac{1}{2}D + \text{IE} + \text{EA} - \Delta H_\text{lattice}$$

Solve for the unknown [[LatticeEnergy|lattice energy]] given the others.

## CsF Example

ΔH_s° = 76.5; ½D = 79.4; IE = 375.7; EA = −328.2; ΔHf° = −553.5 kJ/mol. Then:

$$\Delta H_\text{lattice} = 76.5 + 79.4 + 375.7 + (-328.2) - (-553.5) = 756.9 \text{ kJ/mol}$$
