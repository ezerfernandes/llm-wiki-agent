# Classification and Regression Trees (CART)

Breiman, Friedman, Olshen & Stone (1984) — the foundational treatise on [[DecisionTrees|decision trees]], introducing recursive binary partitioning with cross-validated cost-complexity pruning. Among the first practical demonstrations of [[CrossValidation]] for model selection.
