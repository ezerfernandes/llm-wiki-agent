# emrQA

Large-scale clinical question-answering dataset built from electronic medical records by [[AnusriPampari|Pampari]], Raghavan, Liang & Peng (2018, arXiv:1809.00732). Generated by **repurposing existing annotations** on EHRs into question-answer pairs at scale — useful for pretraining and benchmarking but criticized for **synthetic question phrasing** and **mapping to structured outputs** rather than free-text answers.

The predecessor reference cited by [[2025-bionlp-archehr-qa-neural|Reddy et al. (2025)]] as motivation for [[ArchEHRQA2025]]'s more naturalistic, expert-annotated, free-form-answer design.
