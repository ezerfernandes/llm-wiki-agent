# Nitrogen Group Chemistry (Pnictogens: Nitrogen and Phosphorus)

This page covers the group 15 nonmetals (**pnictogens**) **nitrogen** and **phosphorus** — their occurrence, preparation, oxides, and oxyacids. They show oxidation states from 3− to 5+.

## Nitrogen
From fractional distillation of liquid air (atmosphere is 78% N₂); essential in proteins and DNA/RNA. Colorless gas, bp 77 K (cheap coolant). **Very low reactivity** owing to the strong N≡N triple bond — at room temperature it reacts only with Li (Li₃N), some transition-metal complexes, and via biological **nitrogen fixation** (nitrogenase enzymes containing Fe and Mo, in legume root-nodule rhizobia) and lightning.

### Nitrogen Oxides
- **N₂O** (laughing gas, dental anesthetic) — from careful heating of ammonium nitrate; strong oxidizer.
- **NO** — most thermally stable nitrogen oxide; simplest stable molecule with an unpaired electron; dimerizes to N₂O₂. Lab prep: 3Cu(s) + 8HNO₃(aq) → 2NO(g) + 3Cu(NO₃)₂(aq) + 4H₂O(l).
- **N₂O₃** — blue liquid, anhydride of nitrous acid (→ HNO₂).
- **NO₂** ⇌ **N₂O₄** (2NO₂ ⇌ N₂O₄, Kₚ = 6.86); NO₂ disproportionates in water to HNO₂ + HNO₃ (cold) or HNO₃ + NO (hot).
- **N₂O₅** — white solid, anhydride of nitric acid: N₂O₅(s) + H₂O(l) → 2HNO₃(aq); made by dehydrating HNO₃ with P₄O₁₀.

Ammonia, the principal nitrogen feedstock, is made via the Haber process (covered under [[HydrogenChemistry]]).

## Phosphorus
Only group-15 element not free in nature; many [[Nonmetals|allotropes]] — **white phosphorus** (P₄ tetrahedra; bursts into flame in air; toxic; stored under water) and **red phosphorus** (polymeric, much less reactive). Industrial prep:

> 2Ca₃(PO₄)₂(s) + 6SiO₂(s) + 10C(s) → 6CaSiO₃(l) + 10CO(g) + P₄(g)

### Oxides and Oxyacids
- **P₄O₆** (P(III) oxide) — dissolves slowly in cold water to **phosphorous acid H₃PO₃**.
- **P₄O₁₀** (P(V) oxide) — white powder from burning P in excess O₂; excellent drying agent; P₄O₁₀(s) + 6H₂O(l) → 4H₃PO₄(aq) (**phosphoric acid**).

### Halides
PX₃ and PX₅ from direct reaction with halogens. **PCl₃** (colorless liquid; hydrolyzes to H₃PO₃); **PCl₅** (off-white solid from PCl₃ + excess Cl₂; hydrolyzes to H₃PO₄). Pentahalides are [[LewisAcid|Lewis acids]] forming PX₆⁻; solid PCl₅ is ionic [PCl₄⁺][PCl₆⁻], while PF₅ is molecular. Applications: fertilizers, alloys (ferrophosphorus, phosphor bronze), pesticides, matches; many P compounds start from P₄O₁₀.
