# Legendre–Fenchel Transform

*Synonym redirect.* The **Legendre–Fenchel transform** is the transformation that maps a [[ConvexFunction|convex function]] $f$ to its **convex conjugate** $f^*(\mathbf{s})=\sup_{\mathbf{x}}(\langle\mathbf{s},\mathbf{x}\rangle-f(\mathbf{x}))$ ([[mml-ch07-continuous-optimization|MML Ch 7]] §7.3.3, Def. 7.4). The terms "Legendre–Fenchel transform" and "convex conjugate" are used interchangeably in [[mml-book]]; physics calls the classical (convex-differentiable) special case the **Legendre transform** (relating the Lagrangian and Hamiltonian).

See **[[ConvexConjugate]]** for the full treatment (definition, geometry via supporting hyperplanes, the $f(x)=x^2$ derivation, Examples 7.7–7.9, and ML uses).
