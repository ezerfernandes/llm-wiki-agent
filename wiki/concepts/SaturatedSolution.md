# Saturated Solution

A **saturated solution** is one whose solute concentration equals its [[Solubility|solubility]] — the maximum that can dissolve under the given conditions at equilibrium. At saturation, dissolution and crystallization occur at equal rates (dynamic equilibrium); adding more solute leaves the excess undissolved.

## Saturation Classes
- **Saturated** — concentration = solubility (at equilibrium).
- **Unsaturated** — concentration < solubility; more solute can still dissolve.
- **Supersaturated** — concentration > solubility; a metastable nonequilibrium state (see [[SupersaturatedSolution]]).

These are distinct from the qualitative descriptors **dilute** (low concentration) and **concentrated** (high concentration), which do not reference the solubility limit.
