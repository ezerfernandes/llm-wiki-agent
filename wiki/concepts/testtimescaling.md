# Test-Time Scaling

Improving inference-time performance by spending more compute per query (extra reasoning, parallel rollouts, multi-pass self-refinement). The Conductor's recursive topologies introduce an online iterative-adaptation TTS axis; HEAVYSKILL formalizes parallel-reason + deliberation as an inner-skill TTS pattern.
