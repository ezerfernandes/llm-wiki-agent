# Precision and Recall

Two complementary classification metrics: precision = TP/(TP+FP) measures correctness of positive predictions; recall = TP/(TP+FN) measures coverage of actual positives. Their harmonic mean is F1; core to [[ModelEvaluation]] and [[OfflineEvaluation]].
