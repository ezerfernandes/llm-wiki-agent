# Capability vs Alignment

The distinction between what a model can do (capability — knowledge, reasoning, skills) and what it chooses to do under instructions (alignment — refusal, honesty, helpfulness). A core framing in safety research; mis-alignment can mask or unlock latent capability, motivating work on [[rlhf]], [[constitutionalai]], and [[Interpretability]].
