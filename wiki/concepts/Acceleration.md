## Definition
Acceleration is the rate of change of [[Velocity]] with respect to time. It is a [[VectorQuantity]] pointing in the direction of the *change* in velocity, not necessarily the direction of motion. SI unit: m/s².

## Key Points
- **Average acceleration** is the change in velocity over the elapsed time.
- **Instantaneous acceleration** is the acceleration at a specific instant (an infinitesimal interval).
- Velocity can change in magnitude, direction, or both — any of these is acceleration.
- **Sign rule:** when acceleration and velocity have the same sign, the object speeds up; opposite signs → it slows down.
- **Deceleration** means acceleration opposite to velocity (reduces speed). It is NOT the same as negative acceleration: an object moving left while accelerating right has positive acceleration yet is decelerating.
