# Biconditional Correctness

Metere's central criterion: the multiset projection of corpus delta `D = delta(s0, s1)` onto operation-and-target equals the multiset projection of approved-and-executed records `S` from the audit log. Catches gate bypass, audit forgery, silent-fail, and wrong-target execution. Doubles as a benchmark for elevating a skill's verification level under adversarial-ensemble exercise.
