## Definition
Pitch is the subjective perception that corresponds to the [[Frequency]] of a [[Sound]] wave: higher frequencies are heard as higher pitch. It is the perceptual counterpart of frequency, just as [[Loudness]] is the perceptual counterpart of intensity.

## Key Points
- Normal human hearing spans 20–20,000 Hz; below 20 Hz is **infrasound** and above 20,000 Hz is **ultrasound**, both inaudible.
- Humans can discriminate frequencies differing by as little as ~0.3%.
- Below ~1000 Hz, auditory nerves fire at the sound's frequency; above ~1000 Hz, frequency is encoded by position along the basilar membrane in the cochlea.
- Because [[SpeedOfSound]] is nearly frequency-independent in a medium, lower-pitched sounds have longer wavelengths — explaining why large instruments produce low notes.
