# Anyscale Services

Anyscale's managed online serving primitive — deploys Ray Serve applications with [[Autoscaling]], zero-downtime upgrades, and [[CanaryDeployment]] support. Sits alongside [[AnyscaleJobs]] (batch) and [[AnyscaleWorkspaces]] (dev) in the platform's three-tier execution model.
