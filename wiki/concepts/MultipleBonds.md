# Multiple Bonds

A **multiple bond** is a double or triple [[CovalentBonding|covalent bond]], which in [[ValenceBondTheory|valence bond theory]] consists of **both σ and π bonds**: a double bond is one σ + one π, a triple bond is one σ + two π. The σ bond always forms first and from [[OrbitalHybridization|hybrid orbitals]]; the π bonds form from leftover **unhybridized** *p* orbitals overlapping side by side. (Named *MultipleBonds* to keep the chemistry sense distinct.)

## Double Bonds — Ethene (C₂H₄)

Each carbon has three bonding regions (one C, two H) → trigonal planar → **sp²** (the 2*s* plus two 2*p* orbitals mix).

- **σ framework**: the sp² hybrids form one C–C σ bond (sp²–sp²) and four C–H σ bonds (sp²–s).
- **π bond**: the one **unhybridized** *p* orbital on each carbon (perpendicular to the sp² plane) overlaps side by side, above and below the axis, forming one π bond — see [[SigmaAndPiBonds]].
- **Planarity & rotation**: the molecule is planar so the *p* orbitals can overlap; rotation about the C=C bond is restricted because it would break the π overlap.

## Triple Bonds — Acetylene (C₂H₂)

Acetylene is linear; each carbon is **sp** hybridized, leaving **two** unhybridized *p* orbitals per carbon.

- **σ framework**: sp hybrids form one C–C σ bond and two C–H σ bonds.
- **π bonds**: the two unhybridized *p* orbitals on each carbon overlap side by side to form **two** π bonds → one σ + two π = a triple bond.

## Hybridization Is Set by σ Bonds Only

Hybridization involves only σ bonds, lone pairs, and single unpaired electrons; the **arrangement of π bonds does not affect it**. Because resonance only shuffles π bonds among unhybridized orbitals, it does not change the hybridization either. In benzene each carbon stays sp² regardless of resonance form, and the π electrons are **delocalized** throughout the ring. SO₂ likewise has S surrounded by two bonds and one lone pair in either resonance form → trigonal planar → sp². [[ValenceBondTheory|Valence bond theory]] handles such delocalization poorly; [[MolecularOrbitalTheory|MO theory]] does it better.
