# Matter

**Matter is anything that occupies space and has mass.** Solids, liquids, and gases are all matter (a gas occupies space, as shown by an inflating balloon).

Two related but distinct quantities describe a sample of matter:
- **Mass** — a measure of the amount of matter in an object; intrinsic and unchanging.
- **Weight** — the gravitational force exerted on that mass; proportional to mass but dependent on local gravity (an astronaut's mass is the same on the Moon, but her weight is ~1/6 of her Earth weight).

Matter exists in different physical [[StatesOfMatter]] (solid, liquid, gas, plasma) and is organized by [[MatterClassification]] into pure substances ([[ChemicalElement|elements]] and [[ChemicalCompound|compounds]]) and mixtures. Its smallest units are [[Atom|atoms]] and [[Molecule|molecules]]. The total quantity of matter is preserved through changes, per the [[ConservationOfMatter|law of conservation of matter]].
