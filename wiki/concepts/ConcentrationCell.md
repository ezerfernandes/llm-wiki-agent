# Concentration Cell

A **concentration cell** is a [[GalvanicCell|galvanic cell]] built from two **nearly identical half-cells** — same half-reaction and same electrode material — differing **only in the concentration** of one redox species. Because the two electrodes are chemically identical, the standard cell potential is **E°cell = 0 V**, and the entire driving force comes from the concentration difference, computed with the [[NernstEquation|Nernst equation]].

## How It Works
The species at **higher concentration** is reduced at the **cathode**; the species at **lower concentration** is oxidized at the **anode**. The cell spontaneously works to **equalize** the two concentrations.

## Example
Zn(s)│Zn²⁺(0.10 M)║Zn²⁺(0.50 M)│Zn(s):
- Overall: Zn²⁺(0.50 M) → Zn²⁺(0.10 M); E°cell = 0.000 V
- E_cell = 0.000 − (0.0592/2)log(0.10/0.50) = **+0.021 V** (spontaneous)

At equilibrium the concentrations meet in the middle (here 0.30 M each) and E_cell returns to 0.000 V.
