# Gemini Deep Think

[[googledeepmind|Google DeepMind]]'s **deep-reasoning variant** of [[gemini|Gemini]] — a separately-served high-inference-budget reasoning model. Used in [[2605.06651v2-ai-co-mathematician]] as the **prover sub-agent** of the [[AICoMathematician|AI co-mathematician]] (versions: Gemini 3.1 Deep Think during the main internal evaluation; Gemini 2.5 Deep Think was evaluated separately via the web UI).

On the internal 100-question research-math benchmark: Gemini 3.1 Pro 57%, Gemini 3.1 Deep Think 70%, AI co-mathematician (with both) 87%.
