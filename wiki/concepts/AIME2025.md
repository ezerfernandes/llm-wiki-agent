# AIME 2025

The **American Invitational Mathematics Examination** problem set for 2025 (Balunović et al., MathArena dataset; HuggingFace `MathArena/aime_2025`). 30 problems — competition mathematics requiring chain-of-thought reasoning over algebra, combinatorics, geometry, and number theory.

Used by [[2507.19457-gepa|GEPA]] as one of six core benchmarks for testing math-reasoning prompt optimization. Also used as the **adversarial-search target** ([[AdversarialPromptSearch]]) — GPT-5 Mini's clean-prompt pass@1 of 76% on AIME-2025 drops to 10% under GEPA's adversarial prompt.

## GEPA results on AIME-2025

| Optimizer | Qwen3 8B | GPT-4.1 Mini |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline | 27.33 | 45.33 |
| [[grpo|GRPO]] | **38.00** | — |
| [[MIPROv2]] | 20.00 | 51.33 |
| **GEPA** | 32.00 | **59.33** |
| **GEPA+Merge** | 32.00 | **59.33** |

AIME-2025 is the **only benchmark where GRPO beats GEPA** on Qwen3 8B (38.00 vs 32.00) — the math/reasoning regime where weight-space adaptation can still extract more than prompt-only optimization. On GPT-4.1 Mini, GEPA's +14 over baseline (+12 over MIPROv2) is the paper's most-cited single result.

## Tutorial receipt: `auto="light"` GEPA on GPT-4.1 Mini

The [[dspy-tutorial-gepa-aime|official `dspy.GEPA` tutorial]] re-runs the paper's GPT-4.1 Mini × AIME-2025 cell at the **smallest budget setting** (`auto="light"` ≈ 560 metric calls ≈ 6.22 full evals) with the canonical asymmetric pair ([[GPT|GPT-4.1 Mini]] student + [[GPT|GPT-5]] reflection LM):

| | Paper Table 1 (full budget) | Tutorial (`auto="light"`) |
|---|---|---|
| Test set | AIME-2025 (30 problems, single eval) | AIME-2025 × 5 = **150 evals** (oversampled for variance) |
| Baseline | 45.33% | **46.7% (70/150)** |
| **GEPA** | **59.33%** | **56.7% (85/150)** |
| Lift | +14.00 | **+10.00** |

Qualitative replication of the paper's headline result at a lower compute budget; the ~3-point gap is consistent with the budget cut + 5× oversampling. The tutorial is the **first wiki receipt of `auto="light"` quantified** as a numeric budget (~6.22 full evals on a 90-example train+val).

## Adversarial result

GEPA evolves a universal adversarial prefix that **drops GPT-5 Mini pass@1 from 76% → 10%** on AIME-2025 — the trivia-injection + strict-format-string interaction failure documented in [[AdversarialPromptSearch]].
