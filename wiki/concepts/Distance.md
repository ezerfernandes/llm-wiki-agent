## Definition
Distance is the magnitude of [[Displacement]] between two positions. It is a [[ScalarQuantity]] — it has magnitude but no direction. "Distance traveled" is a related but distinct quantity: the total length of the path covered.

## Key Points
- Distance (magnitude of displacement) considers only the straight-line separation of start and end points.
- Distance traveled is the full path length and can be larger than the magnitude of displacement when the path changes direction.
- Example: a cyclist going 3 km west then 2 km east has displacement magnitude 1 km but distance traveled 5 km.
