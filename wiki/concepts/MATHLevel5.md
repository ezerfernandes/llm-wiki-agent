# MATH Level 5

The **hardest-difficulty subset** of the MATH competitive-mathematics benchmark. Per [[ai-engineering-ch04-evaluate-ai-systems|*AI Engineering* Ch 4]]:

> "GSM-8K was replaced by MATH lvl 5, which consists of the most challenging questions from the competitive math benchmark MATH."

## Why MATH-lvl-5 specifically

[[GSM8K]] saturated quickly. The original [[MATH-benchmark|MATH]] benchmark covers high-school competition math at multiple difficulty levels; Level 5 retains only the hardest problems that remained discriminating in mid-2024.

## Position

Added to HuggingFace's [[OpenLLMLeaderboard|Open LLM Leaderboard]] in the **June 2024 refresh** to replace [[GSM8K]].
