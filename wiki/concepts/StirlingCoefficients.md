# Stirling Coefficients

Stirling coefficients arising in **symmetric power representations**. In [[2605.06651v2-ai-co-mathematician]] §5.2, G. Bérczi used the [[AICoMathematician|AI co-mathematician]] to attack conjectures hypothesizing that, in a certain binomial expansion, the coefficients are not only **strictly positive** but also form a **log-concave sequence**.

[[AlphaEvolve]] had previously failed to resolve the higher-index cases but hinted at an inductive direction. Bérczi pre-loaded that exploration history as "structured posing of questions" — rich background documents as part of the initial project formulation phase. The system produced claimed proofs of two conjectures in separate workstreams (currently under detailed human review). Bérczi observed that a **margin annotation** in the finished document alerted him to a key insight he could investigate via chat — the [[WorkingPaper]] design paid off as an audit surface.
