## Definition
**Viscosity (η)** is the internal friction of a fluid — its resistance to shear (to layers sliding past one another). Low-viscosity fluids (juice, water) flow easily; high-viscosity fluids (syrup, cold oil) flow sluggishly. SI unit: Pa·s.

## Key Points
- Defined operationally by the force needed to shear a fluid between two plates: F = ηvA/L.
- Viscosity is strongly temperature-dependent — engine oil thickens (η rises) when cold.
- It causes a pressure drop along a flowing pipe and underlies the flow [[PoiseuilleLaw|resistance]] of a tube.
- Drives [[LaminarAndTurbulentFlow|laminar vs. turbulent]] behavior together with density and speed via the [[ReynoldsNumber]].
- Sets viscous drag on objects ([[DragForce]]) through Stokes' law.

## Equations
- F = ηvA/L  (parallel-plate definition)
- η = FL/(vA)
