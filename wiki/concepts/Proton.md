# Proton

A **proton** is a positively charged subatomic particle found in the [[AtomicNucleus|nucleus]] of an [[Atom]]. It carries charge +1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C (unit charge 1+) and has a mass of about 1.00727 amu (1.67262 × 10⁻²⁴ g), roughly 1,800 times that of an [[Electron]] and nearly equal to that of a [[Neutron]].

[[ErnestRutherford]] identified the proton, finding that the nuclei of other elements contain the hydrogen nucleus as a "building block" (see [[EvolutionOfAtomicTheory]]).

## Defining the Element

The number of protons in the nucleus is the [[AtomicNumberAndMassNumber|atomic number Z]] — the defining property of a [[ChemicalElement|element]]. Changing the proton count changes the element itself. In a neutral atom the proton count equals the electron count; an imbalance makes the atom a charged [[ChemicalIon|ion]]. Atoms with the same proton count but differing neutron counts are [[Isotope|isotopes]].
