# DeepSearchQA

General-purpose fact-finding benchmark cited in [[2605.06651v2-ai-co-mathematician]] §4 as one of the **auxiliary-capability** benchmarks that better reflect the broader workflows of professional mathematicians than monolithic problem-solving benchmarks like [[FrontierMath]]. The paper argues such benchmarks (DeepSearchQA, [[Hard2Verify]]) should grow more important as AI systems improve.
