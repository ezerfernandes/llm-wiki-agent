# Bell-La Padula

Classification lattice from MAC literature. Labels are tuples ⟨ℓ, C, R⟩ — rank, compartment set, releasability caveats. Combine via join: a ⊔ b = ⟨max(ℓa,ℓb), Ca∪Cb, Ra∩Rb⟩. Reused by the Metere trust schema for `M.label`.
