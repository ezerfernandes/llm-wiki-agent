# Stickiness Metric

A **retention-or-return-rate metric**. Per [[ai-engineering-ch04-evaluate-ai-systems|*AI Engineering* Ch 4]], stickiness is one of the two dominant [[BusinessMetric|business metric]] families AI applications target (the other being [[EngagementMetric|engagement]]).

## Canonical examples

- **[[DAUWAUMAU|DAU / WAU / MAU]]** — daily / weekly / monthly active users.
- **Retention rate** — fraction of users from period N still active in period N+1.
- **Churn rate** — its complement.

## Trade-off

Like engagement, stickiness can drive revenue but can also incentivize *"addictive features or extreme content."* Ch 4 explicitly flags this trade-off.
