# Arrange-Act-Assert

A unit-test structuring pattern: arrange inputs/fixtures, act by invoking the unit under test, then assert on outcomes. Drives readable [[IntegrationTesting]] and [[BehavioralTesting]] code and underpins [[Coverage]] tooling.
