## Definition
Deformation is a change in the shape or size of an object caused by an applied force. When it fully reverses upon removing the force it is elastic deformation (see [[Elasticity]]); beyond the elastic limit it becomes permanent.

## Key Points
- Three modes, each with its own elastic modulus:
  - **Longitudinal** (tension/compression) — length change ΔL, governed by Young's modulus Y.
  - **Shear** (transverse) — sideways displacement Δx, governed by shear modulus S.
  - **Volumetric** (bulk) — volume change ΔV under uniform pressure, governed by bulk modulus B.
- In the elastic region, deformation is proportional to force ([[HookesLaw]]) and relates [[Stress]] to [[Strain]].
- Examples: suspension cables stretch under tension; femurs compress slightly under load; nails bend by shear; ocean water compresses under pressure at depth.

## Equations
- Longitudinal: ΔL = (1/Y)(F/A) L₀
- Shear: Δx = (1/S)(F/A) L₀
- Volumetric: ΔV = (1/B)(F/A) V₀
