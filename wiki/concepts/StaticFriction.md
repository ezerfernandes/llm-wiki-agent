## Definition
Static friction is the [[Friction]] force present when two surfaces in contact are not moving relative to each other. It adjusts itself to match an applied force (preventing motion) up to a maximum value, beyond which the object begins to slide.

## Key Points
- Variable, not fixed: it equals whatever force is needed to keep the object stationary, until the maximum is exceeded.
- The maximum static friction is generally larger than [[KineticFriction]] for the same pair of surfaces — this is why it takes more force to start sliding than to keep sliding.
- Depends on the [[NormalForce]] and the static [[CoefficientOfFriction]] μ_s, not on contact area.

## Equations
- f_s ≤ μ_s N
- Maximum: f_s(max) = μ_s N
