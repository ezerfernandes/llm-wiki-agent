# Electrochemical Cell Notation

**Cell notation** (a **cell schematic** or **cell diagram**) is the standardized shorthand for the composition and construction of an [[ElectrochemicalCell|electrochemical cell]], especially a [[GalvanicCell|galvanic cell]].

## Rules
1. Use chemical formulas / element symbols for the relevant components, with concentrations or pressures where known.
2. A single vertical line **│** marks an interface between phases (e.g., solid electrode | solution); a **comma** separates components within the same phase; a double line **║** marks the **salt bridge**.
3. Write left → right starting at the **anode** and ending at the **cathode**.

## Examples
- Copper-silver cell:
  Cu(s)│1 M Cu(NO₃)₂(aq)║1 M AgNO₃(aq)│Ag(s)
- Magnesium / iron(III) cell with an inert Pt cathode (both Fe species are solutes):
  Mg(s)│0.1 M MgCl₂(aq)║0.2 M FeCl₃(aq), 0.3 M FeCl₂(aq)│Pt(s)
- From net reaction Sn⁴⁺(aq) + Zn(s) → Sn²⁺(aq) + Zn²⁺(aq):
  Zn(s)│Zn²⁺(aq)║Sn⁴⁺(aq), Sn²⁺(aq)│Pt(s)

An **inert electrode** (Pt) appears when neither member of the couple is a usable solid conductor (both are dissolved species).
