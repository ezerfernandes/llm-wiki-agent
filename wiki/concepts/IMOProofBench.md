# IMO Proof Bench

Static problem-solving benchmark for AI-for-math systems, focused on International Mathematical Olympiad-style proofs. Referenced in [[2605.06651v2-ai-co-mathematician]] §4 as one of the current static benchmarks (alongside [[FrontierMath]] and [[PutnamBench]]) the field over-relies on — the paper argues these capture *raw problem-solving* rather than the broader **collaborative-research** workflow of professional mathematicians.
