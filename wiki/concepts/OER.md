# Open Educational Resource

Teaching materials — textbooks, lecture notes, lab manuals, problem sets, full courses — released under licenses (typically Creative Commons) that permit free redistribution, derivation, and reuse, usually with attribution and often non-commercially. The category covers MIT OpenCourseWare, the OpenStax textbook line, the LibreTexts wiki, and individually-authored works like [[JamesFiore]]'s electrical-engineering series.

[[embedded-controllers-fiore]] is itself a CC BY-NC-SA OER:

> "I have decided instead to release this using a Creative Commons non-commercial, share-alike license. I encourage others to make use of this manual for their own work and to build upon it. If you do add to this effort, I would appreciate a notification." — author's note (p. 5)

## License terms in this corpus

- **BY** — attribution required.
- **NC** — non-commercial use only.
- **SA** — derivative works must use the same license (the share-alike requirement that prevents enclosure).

## Why OER matters in the embedded / CS context

Two specific advantages [[embedded-controllers-fiore]] illustrates:

1. **No paywall to the canonical reference.** Students who already paid for a $25 Arduino board don't need to add another $100 for the textbook.
2. **Errata and updates flow fast.** The text is at version 2.1.11, dated 15 Oct 2024 — no publisher revision cycle gates the next edition.

The downside is that OER textbooks are often single-author works without the editorial / proofing layer commercial publishers provide.
