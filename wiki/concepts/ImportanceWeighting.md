# Importance Weighting

Reweighting training samples by the ratio of target to source density to correct for [[DataDrift]] (covariate shift). Used in [[BlackBoxShiftDetection]] follow-ups and in off-policy learning more broadly; sensitive to weight variance when distributions diverge.
