## Definition
A wave is a disturbance that propagates outward from its source, transporting energy through space or a medium. The disturbance travels while the medium's particles largely stay in place (oscillating about equilibrium rather than flowing with the wave).

## Key Points
- A wave can be visible (water, light) or invisible (sound, seismic).
- Each medium particle executes [[Oscillation]] — often [[SimpleHarmonicMotion]] — tracing a sinusoidal pattern in time.
- Classified by particle-motion direction relative to travel: [[TransverseWave]] (perpendicular) and [[LongitudinalWave]] (parallel).
- Characterized by wavelength (λ), [[Period]], [[Frequency]], amplitude, and [[WaveSpeed]].
- Energy carried scales with amplitude squared (see [[WaveIntensity]]); waves combine by [[Superposition]].

## Equations
- f = 1/T
- Wave speed: v_w = λ / T = f λ
