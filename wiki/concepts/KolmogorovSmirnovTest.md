# Kolmogorov-Smirnov Test

A nonparametric test comparing two empirical distributions by the maximum gap between their CDFs. Common signal for [[DataDrift]] on continuous features; paired with [[ChiSquaredTest]] for categorical features in [[DataObservability]] dashboards.
