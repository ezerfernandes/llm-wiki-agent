# Maximum Mean Discrepancy (MMD)

A kernel-based statistical test for comparing two distributions by their embeddings in a reproducing kernel Hilbert space. Used in [[ModelMonitoring]] to detect [[TargetDrift]] and covariate shift between training and production data.
