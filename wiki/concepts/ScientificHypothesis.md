# Scientific Hypothesis

A **scientific hypothesis** is a tentative explanation of observations that guides the gathering and checking of information. A hypothesis is tested through experimentation, calculation, and comparison with others' experiments, and is refined or rejected as evidence accumulates.

Within the [[ScientificMethod]], the hypothesis is the testable conjecture stage: it sits between an initial observation/question and the eventual formation of a [[ScientificLawAndTheory|scientific law or theory]]. A hypothesis that survives repeated testing may contribute to a broader theory; one that fails its predictions is revised or discarded.

> Note: this page is the chemistry/scientific-method sense of "hypothesis." It is distinct from the Python property-based-testing library of the same name, which has its own entity page.
