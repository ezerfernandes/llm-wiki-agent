## Definition
Kinetic (sliding) friction is the [[Friction]] force that acts when two surfaces are moving relative to one another. It opposes the direction of sliding and is approximately constant in magnitude.

## Key Points
- Roughly independent of sliding speed at low nonzero velocities.
- Usually smaller than the maximum [[StaticFriction]] for the same surfaces.
- Set by the [[NormalForce]] and the kinetic [[CoefficientOfFriction]] μ_k, independent of contact area.
- On an incline, an object sliding at constant velocity satisfies μ_k = tan θ — a practical measurement method.

## Equations
- f_k = μ_k N
- On an incline: f_k = μ_k m g cos θ
- Constant-velocity sliding on an incline: μ_k = tan θ
