# ReXTrust

**Fine-grained hallucination detection model for AI-generated radiology reports** — Hardy, Kim, Ro & Rajpurkar, arXiv:2412.15264 (2024). Ref [38] in [[2507.03152-medval]].

Specialized radiology-only error-detection model. [[2507.03152-medval]] §4 groups ReXTrust with [[ReXErr]] / [[GREEN]] / [[FineRadScore]] as radiology-only prior art that MedVAL generalizes beyond, since MedVAL covers 6 diverse medical tasks including non-radiology and bilingual translation.
