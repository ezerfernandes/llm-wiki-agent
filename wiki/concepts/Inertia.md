## Definition
Inertia is the property by which an object at rest tends to stay at rest, and an object in motion tends to continue at constant velocity, unless acted on by a net external force. It is the conceptual basis of [[NewtonsFirstLaw]] (the "law of inertia").

## Key Points
- **Mass** is the quantitative measure of inertia: more mass means greater resistance to changes in motion.
- Mass depends on the number and types of atoms/molecules in an object and is the same everywhere (Earth, orbit, Moon) — unlike weight (see [[MassVersusWeight]]).
- Inertia explains why a net external force is required to start, stop, speed up, slow down, or turn an object.

## Equations
- (Qualitative) — quantified through mass m in [[NewtonsSecondLaw]]: F_net = ma
