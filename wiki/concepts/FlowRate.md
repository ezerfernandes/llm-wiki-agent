## Definition
**Flow rate (Q)** is the volume of fluid that passes a given location per unit time. Its SI unit is m³/s. For fluid moving through a conduit it equals the cross-sectional area times the average velocity, Q = A·v̄.

## Key Points
- Distinct from mass flow rate; Q is a *volume* per time.
- Q = A·v̄ ties geometry to speed: for the same Q, a smaller area means a higher velocity.
- For an incompressible fluid, Q is conserved along a closed system — the basis of the [[ContinuityEquation]].
- Used to compute cardiac output, infusion rates, and pipe discharge.

## Equations
- Q = V/t
- Q = A·v̄
