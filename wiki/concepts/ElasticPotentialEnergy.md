## Definition
Elastic potential energy is the energy stored in a system deformed according to [[HookesLaw]] — for example a compressed or stretched spring. It is a form of [[PotentialEnergy]] recoverable as work or [[KineticEnergy]].

## Key Points
- Stored energy grows as the square of displacement, so doubling the deformation quadruples the stored energy.
- In an undamped oscillator it continuously interconverts with [[KineticEnergy]], keeping total mechanical energy constant (see [[ConservationOfEnergy]]).
- A compressed spring releasing its energy (e.g., a toy launcher) converts elastic PE fully to KE.
- Underpins the energy treatment of [[SimpleHarmonicMotion]] and the maximum-velocity relation.

## Equations
- PE_el = ½ k x²
- Energy conservation in SHM: ½ m v² + ½ k x² = constant
- Maximum velocity: v_max = X √(k/m)
