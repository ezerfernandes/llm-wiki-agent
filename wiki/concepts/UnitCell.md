# Unit Cell and Crystal Lattice

A **unit cell** is "the simplest repeating unit" of a [[CrystallineSolid|crystalline solid]]: lattice points marking atom/ion positions, repeated in three dimensions to build the whole structure. Particles pack efficiently because "the overall attractive interactions between particles are maximized, and the total intermolecular energy is minimized, when the particles pack in the most efficient manner." A unit cell is described by three edge lengths (a, b, c) and three angles (α, β, γ); there are **7 lattice systems and 14 unit-cell types** (cubic, tetragonal, orthorhombic, monoclinic, triclinic, hexagonal, rhombohedral).

## The Three Cubic Unit Cells (Metals)

| Cubic cell | Atoms/cell | Coordination # | Packing efficiency | Edge–radius relation | Examples |
|------------|-----------|----------------|--------------------|----------------------|----------|
| Simple (primitive) cubic | 1 (8 corners × 1/8) | 6 | ~52% | l = 2r (contact along edges) | Po |
| Body-centered cubic (BCC) | 2 (8 × 1/8 + 1 center) | 8 | ~68% | corners contact the center atom | K, Ba, Cr, Mo, W, Fe |
| Face-centered cubic (FCC) | 4 (8 × 1/8 + 6 × 1/2) | 12 | 74% (most efficient) | contact along face diagonals | Al, Cu, Pb, Ag, Ni, Ca, Pt |

FCC is **cubic closest packing (CCP)** with ABCABC layering; **hexagonal closest packing (HCP)** uses ABABAB layering (coordination 12; Cd, Co, Li, Mg, Na, Zn). About two-thirds of metals adopt closest-packed arrays.

**Density example (Po, simple cubic, edge 336 pm):** r = 168 pm; mass/cell = 208.998 / (6.022×10²³) = 3.47×10⁻²² g; V = (336×10⁻¹⁰ cm)³ = 3.79×10⁻²³ cm³ → density 9.16 g/cm³.

## Ionic Crystal Structures

Stable ionic structures maximize opposite-charge contact: larger anions form the close-packed array and cations sit in the **holes**.

| Hole type | Cation:anion radius ratio | Holes per anion | Max ratio if all filled |
|-----------|---------------------------|-----------------|-------------------------|
| Tetrahedral (4 anions) | ~22.5%–41.4% | 2 | 2:1 (Li₂O, Na₂S) |
| Octahedral (6 anions) | ~41.4%–73.2% | 1 | 1:1 (NaCl, NiO) |
| Cubic | ~73.2%–100% | — | 1:1 (CsCl) |

Representative cells:
- **CsCl** — simple cubic Cl⁻ with Cs⁺ at the center (1 Cs⁺ + 1 Cl⁻); contact along the body diagonal; not BCC since the ions differ (Cs⁺ 174 pm, Cl⁻ 181 pm).
- **NaCl** — FCC Cl⁻ with Na⁺ in all octahedral holes (4 Na⁺ + 4 Cl⁻); Na⁺ 102 pm, Cl⁻ 181 pm.
- **Zinc blende (ZnS)** — FCC S²⁻ with Zn²⁺ in alternating tetrahedral holes (4 + 4).
- **Fluorite (CaF₂)** — FCC Ca²⁺ with F⁻ in all tetrahedral sites (4 Ca²⁺ + 8 F⁻, ratio 1:2).

**Ionic radii from edge length:** in a LiCl-type FCC (edge 0.514 nm), anions touch along the face diagonal = 4r, so a² + a² = (4r)² → r_Cl = 0.182 nm (assuming perfect spheres).
