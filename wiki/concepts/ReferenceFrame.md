## Definition
A reference frame (or coordinate system) is the basis used to describe an object's position and motion. It may be Earth, a building, a vehicle, or any chosen system, and it defines where "zero" is and which direction is positive.

## Key Points
- Position is always specified relative to a reference frame; [[Displacement]] is the change in that position.
- In 1D, one assigns a positive direction (commonly right or up); the negative direction is its opposite.
- The choice of positive direction is arbitrary but must remain fixed throughout a problem.
- The same physical motion can look different (e.g., different signs) depending on the frame chosen.
