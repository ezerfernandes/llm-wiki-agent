## Definition
Infrared (IR) radiation is the [[ElectromagneticSpectrum]] band just below visible light in frequency ("infra" = below red), overlapping microwaves at its low end. It is produced by thermal motion and by vibrational/rotational molecular excitation plus electronic transitions.

## Key Points
- Emitted strongly by warm objects (human skin emissivity ≈ 0.97), making it the basis of thermal imaging.
- The atmosphere absorbs much incoming and outgoing IR.
- **Greenhouse effect**: atmospheric CO₂ and H₂O absorb the IR that Earth radiates and re-emit part of it downward, raising the surface temperature roughly 40°C above what it would be otherwise.

## Equations
- `c = f λ` (IR sits between visible light and microwaves).

## Applications
Thermal imaging (night vision, building heat-loss surveys, reconnaissance), infrared heating lamps, and astronomical blackbody studies.
