# BBH — BIG-bench Hard

A **reasoning-focused subset** of [[bigbench|BIG-bench]] (Suzgun et al. 2022 / Srivastava et al. 2023). Per [[ai-engineering-ch04-evaluate-ai-systems|*AI Engineering* Ch 4]]:

> "BBH (BIG-bench Hard) (Srivastava et al., 2023): another reasoning benchmark."

## Position

Added to HuggingFace's [[OpenLLMLeaderboard|Open LLM Leaderboard]] in the **June 2024 refresh** alongside [[MATHLevel5]], [[MMLUPro]], [[GPQA]], [[MuSR]], and [[IFEval]] — chosen because most original benchmarks had saturated.
