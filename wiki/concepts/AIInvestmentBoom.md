# AI Investment Boom

**The 2022–2025 surge in capital, hiring, and market attention around AI**, triggered by [[ChatGPT|ChatGPT's]] November 2022 launch. Per [[ai-engineering-ch01-intro|*AI Engineering* Ch 1]], the investment boom is **factor 2 of 3** behind the emergence of [[AIEngineering|AI engineering]] as a discipline (alongside general-purpose model capabilities and low entry barriers via [[ModelAsAService|model-as-a-service]]).

## Anchor data points

- **Goldman Sachs Research** (2023): AI investment could approach **$100B in the US** and **$200B globally by 2025**. For comparison, the *entire* US public elementary + secondary school expenditures are around $900B — only 9× the projected AI investment.
- **FactSet** (Q2 2023): **1 in 3 S&P 500 companies** mentioned AI in earnings calls — **3× the year prior**.
- **WallStreetZen**: companies that mentioned AI in earnings calls saw stock prices rise **4.6%** vs. **2.4%** for those that didn't. Causation vs. correlation is unclear.
- **[[Scribd]] / Matt Ross** (April 2022 → April 2023): the estimated AI cost for his use cases **dropped two orders of magnitude** in 12 months — a stunning per-inference price decay.
- **Gartner 2023** (2,500 executives): **7%** cited *business continuity* as their reason for embracing generative AI.

## Adjacent indicators

- **LinkedIn (August 2023)**: professionals adding terms like "Generative AI," "ChatGPT," "Prompt Engineering," "Prompt Crafting" to their profile increased on average **75% per month**.
- **ComputerWorld**: declared *"teaching AI to behave is the fastest-growing career skill."*
- **GitHub stars**: 4 open-source AI engineering tools ([[AutoGPT]], [[StableDiffusion]] Web UI, [[LangChain]], [[Ollama]]) garnered more stars than Bitcoin within 2 years; on track to surpass React and Vue.
- **AI coding fundraising** (August 2024): [[MagicAI|Magic]] $320M, [[Anysphere]] $60M.

## Cost-decay risk

The cost-collapse cuts both ways. Ch 1 warns: *"You may decide to build a model in-house because it seems cheaper than paying for model providers, only to find out after three months that model providers have dropped their prices in half, making in-house the expensive option."* See [[ModelAsAService]].
