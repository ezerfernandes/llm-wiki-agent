# Datamapplot

**Datamapplot** is a Python visualization library (`pip install datamapplot`) that produces **labeled 2D landscape plots** of clustered embeddings — point clouds with topic labels positioned over the corresponding clusters. Used in [[hands-on-llm-ch05-text-clustering-topic-modeling|*Hands-On LLMs* Ch 5]] for the final chapter figure: the **top-20 GPT-3.5-labeled BERTopic topics** rendered over the 2D [[UMAP]] projection of the 44,949-abstract [[ArXivNLP|ArXiv NLP]] dataset.

## From [[hands-on-llm-ch05-text-clustering-topic-modeling|*Hands-On LLMs* Ch 5]]

Ch 5's closing visualization: pipe BERTopic's `visualize_document_datamap(...)` with topic labels generated by [[ChatGPT|GPT-3.5-turbo]] (via the [[GenerativeTopicLabeling|generative topic labeling]] representation model) and Datamapplot draws the labels directly on the manifold landscape. This is the chapter's most legible artifact — it transforms 156 numbered BERTopic clusters into a navigable "map" of NLP research subfields (*"Advancements in Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis,"* *"Neural Machine Translation Enhancements,"* etc.).
