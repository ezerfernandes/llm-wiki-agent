# Perimeter and Area (Rectangle, Triangle, Trapezoid)

OpenStax [[Prealgebra]] 2e (Chapter 9, [[prealgebra-2e-ch09-math-models-and-geometry]], §9.4–9.5) measures plane figures with two distinct quantities. They use different units, a recurring source of confusion the chapter stresses:

- **Perimeter** — the distance **around** a figure; a **linear** measure (in, ft, cm, …).
- **Area** — the surface **covered** by a figure; a **square** measure (in², ft², cm², …).

Every measure below plugs into the [[ProblemSolvingStrategy|seven-step strategy]]; "solve for a dimension" problems isolate a variable via [[Formula|solving a formula for a variable]].

## Rectangle
A four-sided figure with four right angles; opposite sides equal, dimensions **length L** and **width W**.
- **Perimeter**: P = 2L + 2W
- **Area**: A = L·W

## Triangle
Base `b` and perpendicular **height** `h` (height is the perpendicular distance from the base to the opposite vertex).
- **Perimeter**: P = a + b + c
- **Area**: A = ½bh
- Angle-sum property and special triangles (isosceles, equilateral) are covered on [[Triangle]].

## Trapezoid
A quadrilateral with two parallel sides (the **bases**) and two non-parallel sides. Smaller base `b`, larger base `B`, height `h` (perpendicular distance between the bases).
- **Area**: A = ½h(b + B)

(The area equals the height times the **average** of the two bases.)

## Irregular figures (§9.5)
A shape that isn't a single standard figure is handled by **decomposition**: split it into figures whose formulas you know (rectangles, triangles, semicircles — see [[Circle]]), compute each piece, and **add** the areas (or add edge lengths for perimeter). Typical compositions: rectangle + triangle, rectangle with semicircular ends, trapezoid + triangle.

## Worked-example types
Finding perimeter/area from dimensions; recovering a missing length, width, base, or height from a given perimeter or area (a [[Formula|solve-for-a-variable]] task); perimeters of isosceles/equilateral triangles; decimal-measurement trapezoids; multi-shape irregular figures.
