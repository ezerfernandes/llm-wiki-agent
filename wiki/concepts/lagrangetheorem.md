# Lagrange Theorem

*Disambiguation page.* The string "Lagrange theorem" appears in three distinct contexts; pick the right one:

- **Lagrange's theorem (group theory)** — the order of any subgroup divides the order of the finite group containing it. See [[groups]] for the algebraic background.
- **Lagrange's theorem (mean value)** — for continuous $f$ on $[a,b]$, differentiable on $(a,b)$, there exists $c$ with $f'(c) = \frac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}$. Foundational to [[TaylorSeries]] and [[VectorCalculus]] error bounds.
- **[[LagrangeMultipliers]]** — the optimization technique for constrained problems used by [[SupportVectorMachine|SVMs]] (dual derivation) and [[PrincipalComponentAnalysis|PCA]] (eigenvalue derivation). Treated in [[mml-book]] §7.2.

The most-linked sense in this wiki is the *Lagrange multipliers* one. Follow the link to [[LagrangeMultipliers]] for the canonical treatment.
