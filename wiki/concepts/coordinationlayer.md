# Coordination Layer

Architectural framing in Nechepurenko & Shuvalov: agents as opaque endpoints with a separable layer specifying topology, authority distribution, synchronization, aggregation, termination, failure handling. Exposing this layer enables pre-deployment failure-mode prediction independent of agent logic and information access.
