# Data Warehouse

A schema-on-write analytical store (Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift) optimized for SQL aggregations over curated, modeled data. Sits downstream of raw [[DataLake]] zones in modern [[DataPipeline]] architectures; primary serving layer for BI and ML feature derivation.
