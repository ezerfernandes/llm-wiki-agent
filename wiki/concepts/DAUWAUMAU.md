# DAU / WAU / MAU

**Daily / Weekly / Monthly Active Users** — the canonical [[StickinessMetric|stickiness metric]] family. Cited in [[ai-engineering-ch04-evaluate-ai-systems|*AI Engineering* Ch 4]] as the dominant [[BusinessMetric|business metric]] AI applications target:

> "Many applications focus on stickiness metrics, such as daily, weekly, or monthly active users (DAU, WAU, MAU)."

## What each measures

| Metric | Unique users who used the app within the last … |
|---|---|
| **DAU** | 1 day |
| **WAU** | 7 days |
| **MAU** | 30 days |

DAU/MAU ratio is a common stickiness derivative — *"on average, what fraction of your monthly users come back every day?"*
