# ReXErr

**Synthesizing clinically meaningful errors in diagnostic radiology reports** — Rao, Zhang, Acosta, Adithan & Rajpurkar, *Biocomputing 2025: Proceedings of the Pacific Symposium*, pp. 70–81 (2024). Ref [39] in [[2507.03152-medval]].

Injects clinical errors into radiology reports to build evaluation suites for error-detection systems — a methodological inspiration for the **perturbation-injection** step in [[MedVAL]]'s Stage 1, which generalizes the same idea across 6 task families and four severity levels.
