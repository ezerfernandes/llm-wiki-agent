# Condition Number

Ratio $\kappa = \sigma_{\max}(\mathbf{A})/\sigma_{\min}(\mathbf{A})$ — how much a linear system amplifies error. Large $\kappa$ ⇒ ill-conditioned ⇒ [[GradientDescent]] zigzags ⇒ need a [[Preconditioner]]. [[mml-book]] §7.1.1.
