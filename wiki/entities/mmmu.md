# MMMU

**Massive Multi-discipline Multimodal Understanding** (Yue et al., 2023) — a college-level multimodal benchmark spanning Art & Design, Business, Science, Health & Medicine, Humanities & Social Science, and Technology & Engineering. Each question pairs an image with text and requires multi-step subject reasoning.

In [[2312.11805-gemini]], Gemini Ultra advances MMMU SOTA by more than 5 percentage points (62.4% Maj1@32 vs 56.8% prior best), beating the previous best in 5 of 6 disciplines.
