# John Hennessy

Computer architect (Stanford; pioneer of the MIPS RISC architecture) and co-author with [[DavidPatterson|David Patterson]] of the *Computer Architecture: A Quantitative Approach* tradition. In [[mlsysbook-ch16-conclusion|mlsysbook Vol 1's conclusion]], the Hennessy–Patterson 1990 framework is held up as the model the book's [[ThirteenQuantitativeInvariants|thirteen invariants]] aspire to emulate: it "gave computer architecture a shared analytical language… that transformed a craft practiced by intuition into a discipline governed by measurable principles" (before it, RISC-vs-CISC was debated with rhetoric; after it, with CPI/clock-rate/instruction-count arithmetic). Hennessy & Patterson's 2019 **"New Golden Age for Computer Architecture"** (driven by the end of Dennard scaling, the slowdown of Moore's Law, and domain-specific architectures) frames the conclusion's argument that frontier AI is a systems-engineering, not just an algorithmic, challenge.
