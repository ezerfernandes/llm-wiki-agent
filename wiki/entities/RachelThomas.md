# Rachel Thomas

Co-founder of **[[FastAI|fast.ai]]** with [[JeremyHoward|Jeremy Howard]], and a prominent voice on **data ethics** in machine learning. She founded the Center for Applied Data Ethics at the University of San Francisco, and the data-ethics material she developed anchors the Lesson 9 ethics bonus of *[[practical-deep-learning-for-coders|Practical Deep Learning for Coders]]*.

> Distinct from [[RachelHu|Rachel Hu]] (the D2L appendix co-author) — different person, same first name.
