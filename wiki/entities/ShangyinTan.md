# Shangyin Tan

[[UCBerkeley]] researcher; co-first author on **[[2312.13382-dspy-assertions|DSPy Assertions]]** (Singhvi, Shetty, Tan, Potts, Sen, Zaharia, Khattab 2024) and co-author on **[[2507.19457-gepa|GEPA]]** (Agrawal, Tan, Soylu, Ziems et al., ICLR 2026 Oral). Berkeley-side contributor to the [[DSPy]]-adjacent research line — bridges the *assertion-driven self-refinement* layer (this paper) and the *reflective prompt evolution* layer ([[2507.19457-gepa|GEPA]]).
