# Max Planck

**Max Planck** (German physicist) initiated quantum theory around **1900** by resolving the ultraviolet catastrophe in blackbody radiation. He matched the experimental curves by assuming a vibrating atom can hold only discrete energies for each frequency:

> **E = nhν**, with **Planck's constant h = 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s**

This was the first statement of [[EnergyQuantization|energy quantization]] — College Physics 2e writes the [[BlackbodyRadiation|blackbody]] oscillator levels as E = (n + ½)hf. Planck was troubled by his own result and could not explain *why* energy is quantized; [[AlbertEinstein|Einstein]] later applied the idea to light (the [[PhotonQuantum|photon]]). His name lives on in [[PlancksConstant|Planck's constant]] h.
