# Nandan Thakur

Researcher in NLP / information retrieval / embeddings. **First author of [[AugmentedSBERT]]** (Thakur, Reimers, Daxenberger & Gurevych 2020, arXiv:2010.08240 — *"Augmented SBERT: Data Augmentation Method for Improving Bi-Encoders for Pairwise Sentence Scoring Tasks"*). Co-author of [[GPL|GPL]] (Wang, Thakur, Reimers & Gurevych 2021) and BEIR (Thakur et al. 2021 — the canonical heterogeneous IR benchmark).

PhD researcher at [[IrynaGurevych|Iryna Gurevych]]'s UKP Lab (TU Darmstadt) at time of [[AugmentedSBERT|Augmented SBERT]] publication; later moved to David R. Cheriton School of Computer Science at the University of Waterloo (Jimmy Lin's group).

## From [[hands-on-llm-ch10-creating-text-embedding-models|*Hands-On LLMs* Ch 10]]

Ch 10 cites the Augmented SBERT paper directly: *"Nandan Thakur et al. 'Augmented SBERT: Data augmentation method for improving bi-encoders for pairwise sentence scoring tasks.' arXiv preprint arXiv:2010.08240 (2020)."*
