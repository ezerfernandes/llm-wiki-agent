## What it is
Galileo is an LLM observability and evaluation company that also offers **Galileo Protect**, a runtime guardrails product covering prompt injection, toxicity, privacy leaks, and hallucination detection.

## In LLM Engineer's Handbook
Ch. 2 ([[leh-ch02-tooling-and-installation]]) lists Galileo among the prompt-monitoring alternatives to [[Opik]], alongside [[Langfuse]] and [[LangSmith]]. Ch. 11 ([[leh-ch11-mlops-and-llmops]]) names **Galileo Protect** as a concrete option for input + output guardrails when discussing the LLMOps requirement to defend against prompt-injection, jailbreaks, and toxic / hallucinated outputs.
