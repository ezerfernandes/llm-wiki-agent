Stanford CS324 is the Winter 2022 graduate course "Large Language Models" at Stanford University. Its 14 lectures cover capabilities, harms, data, security, legality, modeling, training, parallelism, scaling laws, selective architectures, adaptation, and environmental impact, and form the source corpus for this wiki.
