# Mind Lab

Research lab listed as primary affiliation on [[2605.12357-delta-mem|δ-mem: Efficient Online Memory for Large Language Models]] (Lei, Zhang et al., May 2026). Github org: `Declare-lab` & `MindLab-Research`. Affiliated lab members on this paper are jointly cross-affiliated with [[nanyangtechnologicaluniversity|Nanyang Technological University]], [[fudanuniversity|Fudan University]], Shanghai Jiao Tong University, [[chineseuniversityofhongkong|CUHK]], and HKUST-Guangzhou.

Lead author Jingdi Lei is also a co-author of the related "Error-free linear attention: exact solution from continuous-time dynamics" (2512.12602, Lei et al. 2025), suggesting Mind Lab's research direction includes both efficient attention and compact-state memory mechanisms.
