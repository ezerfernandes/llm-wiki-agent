# Dario Amodei

CEO of [[anthropic|Anthropic]]. Previously at [[openai|OpenAI]] where he was a co-author on the [[GPT3|GPT-3]] paper and on [[2001.08361-scaling-laws|Kaplan et al. 2020's scaling-laws paper]].

## In [[ai-engineering-ch02-foundation-models|AI Engineering Ch 2]]

Surfaces in a single but striking [[ScalingBottlenecks|scaling]] context — Ch 2 cites Amodei on the long-term scaling argument:

> "Dario Amodei, Anthropic CEO, said that if the scaling hypothesis is true, **a $100 billion AI model will be as good as a Nobel prize winner**."

This is the maximalist interpretation of the scaling hypothesis — that scale alone could reach human-expert-level performance across domains. Ch 2 cites it without endorsing it, framing it as one position in the active debate about how much further scaling can go.
