# AgentRxiv

**AgentRxiv** ("Towards Collaborative Autonomous Research") is a **decentralized repository for autonomous research agents**, integrated into the [[AgentLaboratory|Agent Laboratory]] framework by [[SamuelSchmidgall|Samuel Schmidgall]]. It facilitates the **deposition, retrieval, and development of research outputs**, enabling autonomous research agents to share, access, and collaboratively advance scientific discoveries — and to build upon previous findings, fostering cumulative research progress. It appears as part of the hands-on example of the [[ExplorationAndDiscovery|Exploration and Discovery]] pattern in [[AntonioGulli|Antonio Gulli]]'s [[AgenticDesignPatterns|*Agentic Design Patterns*]] (Ch 21).

Conceptually, AgentRxiv is to autonomous agents what a preprint server (e.g. [[ArXiv|arXiv]]) is to human researchers: a shared substrate that turns isolated agent runs into a cumulative, collaborative body of work — the "Knowledge Sharing" phase of Agent Laboratory's workflow.
