# PaLM 2

Google's prior flagship LLM (Anil et al., 2023), succeeded by [[Gemini]]. In [[2312.11805-gemini]], PaLM-2-L is the principal external comparison on text benchmarks (MMLU, GSM8K, MATH, BIG-Bench-Hard, WMT 23, HellaSwag, MGSM) and is the base model behind the original [[Bard]]. The Gemini paper also notes that Gemini's training-infrastructure improvements (single-controller Pathways/Jax, in-memory model-state redundancy) substantially exceed PaLM-2's recovery-time profile.
