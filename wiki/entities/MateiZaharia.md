# Matei Zaharia

Co-creator of **[[ApacheSpark]]** and **[[MLflow]]**; co-founder & CTO of [[Databricks]]; CS faculty at [[UCBerkeley]] (formerly Stanford). Senior co-author on [[2507.19457-gepa|GEPA]].

Research span: from distributed systems (Spark, Tachyon/Alluxio) and ML platforms (MLflow) into modern compound-AI-system optimization (the DSPy-adjacent optimizer line including [[MIPROv2]] and [[GEPA]]).
