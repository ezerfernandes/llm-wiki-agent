# KTH Royal Institute of Technology

Stockholm-based research university; affiliation of [[DavidBroman]] on the [[2406.11695-mipro|MIPRO paper]]. Together with [[stanforduniversity|Stanford]], [[UCBerkeley]], and [[BasisAI|Basis]], one of the four institutional affiliations on MIPRO.

## Tracked contributions

- **[[2406.11695-mipro]]** — [[DavidBroman]] is the KTH-side co-author. KTH is the only European institution represented on the paper's author list. The paper acknowledges support from KTH-affiliated funders: **WASP (Wallenberg AI, Autonomous Systems and Software Program, Knut and Alice Wallenberg Foundation)** and **Digital Futures at KTH**.
