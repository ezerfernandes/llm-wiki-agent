# Robert A. Millikan

**Robert A. Millikan** was an American physicist who, in 1909, conducted the **oil-drop experiment** to determine the charge on a single [[Electron|electron]]. He produced microscopic, electrically charged oil droplets and balanced gravity against an electric field; the charge on any droplet was always a whole-number multiple of **1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C**, establishing this as the fundamental (elementary) charge.

Combined with [[JJThomson|Thomson's]] charge-to-mass ratio, Millikan's charge value yielded the electron's mass, **9.107 × 10⁻³¹ kg**.
