# UK AI Safety Institute (UK AISI)

The **UK AI Safety Institute** is a UK-government-funded research body focused on **frontier-AI safety evaluation**. It maintains the [[ControlArena|`control_arena`]] Python library and the `inspect_ai` evaluation framework, and publishes research on [[AIControl|AI control]] protocols, capability evaluations, and red-team methodology.

Historically founded in late 2023 alongside the UK AI Safety Summit at Bletchley Park; sits under the Department for Science, Innovation and Technology (DSIT). Often referred to as just **"AISI"** in the AI-safety research literature, sometimes disambiguated to **"UK AISI"** when distinguishing from the US AISI (the analogous body under NIST).
