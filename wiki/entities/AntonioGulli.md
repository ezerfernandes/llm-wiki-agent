# Antonio Gulli

**Antonio Gulli** is the author of ***[[AgenticDesignPatterns|Agentic Design Patterns: A Hands-On Guide to Building Intelligent Systems]]*** (2025), affiliated with [[google|Google]]. In the book he argues that, precisely because AI is moving so quickly, practitioners need to step back and identify the underlying, stabilizing **patterns** — citing [[RAG]], [[Reflection]], [[Routing]], and [[MemoryManagement]] among the patterns that are "becoming fundamental building blocks."

## Role in the wiki
- Author and unifying voice of the [[AgenticDesignPatterns]] book hub.
- Frames agent development around the "canvas" metaphor (infrastructure + frameworks) and the practitioner-level definition of an [[AgenticAI|agentic system]].
- Organizes the field via a four-level [[AgentComplexitySpectrum|spectrum of agent complexity]] and five hypotheses about the future of agents.

In [[agentic-design-patterns-ch01-prompt-chaining|Chapter 1]] he opens the catalog with [[PromptChaining|prompt chaining]] (the "Pipeline pattern"), framing it as the foundational divide-and-conquer technique and introducing [[ContextEngineering|Context Engineering]] as the discipline that subsumes prompt engineering.
