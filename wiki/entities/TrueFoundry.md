# TrueFoundry

MLOps / LLMOps platform vendor cited in [[ai-engineering-ch10-architecture-feedback|*AI Engineering* Ch 10]] as one of the off-the-shelf [[ModelGateway|model-gateway]] options.

> *"There are many off-the-shelf gateways. Examples include Portkey's AI Gateway, MLflow AI Gateway, Wealthsimple's LLM Gateway, TrueFoundry, Kong, and Cloudflare."* — Ch 10

## Position

TrueFoundry sits in the broader ML-platform niche (model deployment, monitoring, lifecycle) and exposes a gateway as part of its product surface — one example of the *"orchestrators want to be gateways"* trend Huyen notes in Ch 10. The category boundary between gateway, orchestrator, and full ML platform is blurry, and TrueFoundry intentionally spans it.
