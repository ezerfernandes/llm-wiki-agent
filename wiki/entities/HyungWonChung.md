# Hyung Won Chung

NLP researcher; **first author of the Flan-T5 paper** — Chung et al., *"Scaling instruction-finetuned language models,"* arXiv:2210.11416 (2022) — the paper that extended the [[t5|T5]] / [[FLANT5|Flan-T5]] instruction-tuning recipe to **1,800+ tasks** and demonstrated that instruction-tuning scales with both model size and task count.

Formerly at [[google|Google Brain]]; subsequently OpenAI.

## In the wiki

Cited as **footnote 8 of [[hands-on-llm-ch04-text-classification|*Hands-On LLMs* Ch 4]]** when the chapter introduces Flan-T5:

> "This method of fine-tuning was extended in the paper 'Scaling instruction-finetuned language models', which introduced more than a thousand tasks during fine-tuning that more closely follow instructions as we know them from GPT models. [Hyung Won Chung et al., 2022] This resulted in the Flan-T5 family of models that benefit from this large variety of tasks." — Ch 4
