# Boxi Yu

Researcher at [[CUHKShenzhen|The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen]]. Corresponding author of [[dspy-guardrails|*DSPy Guardrails: Building Safe LLM Applications via Self-Refining Language Model Pipelines*]] (2024) with [[PinjiaHe]]. Email: boxiyu@link.cuhk.edu.cn (as listed on the paper).

The paper proposes [[DSPyGuardrails]] — using [[DSPy]] and [[BootstrapFewShot]] to autonomously optimize LLM safety guardrails against [[Jailbreak|jailbreaks]] like [[CodeAttack]], dropping [[AttackSuccessRate|ASR]] from 75% to 5% on gpt-3.5-turbo-instruct.
