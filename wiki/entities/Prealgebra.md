# Prealgebra (OpenStax 2e)

**Prealgebra 2e** is a free, openly licensed (CC BY) college/high-school mathematics textbook published by [[OpenStax]]. It builds arithmetic and pre-algebra foundations — whole numbers, integers, fractions, decimals, percents, measurement, geometry, and an introduction to the real numbers and the basics of algebra — progressing deliberately from concrete models (number lines, base-10 blocks, area diagrams) toward symbolic reasoning (variables, equations, graphs).

In this wiki it is the **first dedicated foundational-arithmetic / pre-algebra source** — the concrete bedrock beneath the corpus's higher-math material ([[Calculus]], the [[mml-book|MML]] linear-algebra/probability cluster, [[AnalyticGeometry]]). All 11 chapters were ingested from `openstax.org/books/prealgebra-2e`, one source page per chapter, via per-chapter opus subagents run sequentially.
