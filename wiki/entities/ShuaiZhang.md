# Shuai Zhang

[[Amazon]] researcher; **first author of D2L's *Recommender Systems* chapter** ([[d2l-recommender-systems]]). Recommender-systems specialist; co-author of the widely-cited *Deep Learning Based Recommender System: A Survey and New Perspectives* (Zhang, Yao, Sun & Tay, *ACM Computing Surveys* 2019) — the field-defining survey of the deep-learning-meets-recommenders transition.
