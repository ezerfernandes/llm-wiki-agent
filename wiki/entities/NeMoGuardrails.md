## What it is
NeMo Guardrails is an open-source toolkit from [[NVIDIA]] for adding programmable guardrails to LLM-based conversational systems — dialogue flow, topic constraints, fact-checking, jailbreak detection — defined via a domain-specific language called Colang.

## In LLM Engineer's Handbook
Ch. 11 ([[leh-ch11-mlops-and-llmops]]) mentions NeMo Guardrails in its discussion of LLMOps **input + output guardrails**, alongside [[Galileo|Galileo Protect]] and OpenAI's Moderation API, as a tool for preventing prompt injection, jailbreaks, sensitive-data leakage, and toxic output.
