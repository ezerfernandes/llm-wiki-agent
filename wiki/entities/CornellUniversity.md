# Cornell University

Private research university in Ithaca, NY (also Cornell Tech in NYC). Long-running CS / ML / NLP research programs; affiliated with arXiv (hosted by Cornell Tech).

Cornell appears in Ch 2 as the institutional affiliation of [[ShuoChen|Shuo Chen]] — the source of the US-radio-station playlist dataset used in the [[Word2VecRecommender|song-embedding worked example]].
