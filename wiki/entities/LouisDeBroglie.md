# Louis de Broglie

**Louis de Broglie** (French physicist) proposed in **1924** that matter, like light, has a wave nature, with wavelength

> **λ = h/(mv) = h/p**

This [[DeBroglieWavelength|de Broglie wavelength]] is significant for tiny particles (an electron's is ~10⁻¹¹ m, atomic-scale) and negligible for macroscopic objects. His standing-wave condition **2πr = nλ** explained the quantization of the [[BohrModel|Bohr model]]'s orbits. The prediction was confirmed by the Davisson-Germer electron-diffraction experiment (1925–26), where electrons obeyed the Bragg condition nλ = 2d sin θ.
