# Jimmy Lei Ba

Canadian machine-learning researcher; co-author with [[DiederikKingma|Kingma]] of the **[[Adam]]** optimizer (Kingma & Ba 2014). PhD with [[GeoffreyHinton|Hinton]] at the [[universityoftoronto|University of Toronto]]. Also co-author of **[[LayerNormalization|layer normalization]]** (Ba, Kiros & Hinton 2016) — the normalization variant that became default in [[transformer|Transformers]].

## Why he matters here

- **Adam (2014).** Co-introduced the dominant DL optimizer ([[d2l-optimization]] §adam).
- **Layer normalization (2016).** With [[JamieKiros|Kiros]] and [[GeoffreyHinton|Hinton]] — the BN variant that became default in Transformers ([[d2l-convolutional-modern]] §batch-norm).
