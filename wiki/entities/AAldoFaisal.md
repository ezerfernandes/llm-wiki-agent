# A. Aldo Faisal

Co-author of *Mathematics for Machine Learning* ([[mml-book]], 2020, [[CambridgeUniversityPress]]). Professor of AI & Neuroscience at [[ImperialCollegeLondon]] (Department of Bioengineering and Department of Computing); founding Director of the UKRI Centre for Doctoral Training in AI for Healthcare.

Research focus: machine learning for healthcare, brain–computer interfaces, behavioral neuroscience, sensorimotor control.

## Role in this wiki

- **Co-author** of [[mml-book]].
- His bioengineering / neuroscience background plausibly shapes the book's emphasis on geometric and probabilistic intuition — fields where the math has to map back to physical / biological signals.
