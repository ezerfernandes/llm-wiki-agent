## What it is
Azure Machine Learning is Microsoft Azure's managed end-to-end MLOps platform — workspaces, compute targets, registered models, pipelines, and managed inference endpoints.

## In LLM Engineer's Handbook
Ch. 10 ([[leh-ch10-inference-pipeline-deployment]]) lists Azure ML alongside [[GoogleCloudVertexAI]], [[AzureOpenAI]], [[AmazonBedrock]], [[Seldon]], [[BentoML]], [[NvidiaTriton]], [[Modal]], and [[Hopsworks]] as alternative ML deployment platforms one could pick instead of [[AmazonSageMaker]].
