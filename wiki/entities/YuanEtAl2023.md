# Yuan et al. 2023 — *GPT-4 is too smart to be safe: Stealthy chat with LLMs via cipher*

**Youliang Yuan, Wenxiang Jiao, Wenxuan Wang, Jen-tse Huang, [[PinjiaHe|Pinjia He]], Shuming Shi, Zhaopeng Tu. 2023.** *GPT-4 is too smart to be safe: Stealthy chat with LLMs via cipher.* arXiv:2308.06463.

Cipher-based [[Jailbreak]] attack — encodes harmful prompts through ciphers ([[GPT4_1|GPT-4]] decrypts and responds, bypassing safety training applied to plaintext inputs). Cited in [[dspy-guardrails|Yu & He 2024]] and shares [[PinjiaHe]] as a co-author with that paper — putting Pinjia He on both the attack and defense sides of the jailbreak literature.
