# Ludwig Boltzmann

**Ludwig Boltzmann** developed the statistical-mechanical model of entropy that connects a system's macroscopic entropy to the number of microscopic configurations (microstates) available to it.

## Contribution
- The [[BoltzmannEntropyEquation|Boltzmann entropy equation]] **S = k ln W**, where W is the number of microstates and k = 1.38 × 10⁻²³ J/K is the Boltzmann constant.
- This molecular-scale picture explains why systems evolve toward more dispersed, higher-entropy macrostates (they have vastly more microstates) and underpins the [[ThirdLawOfThermodynamics|third law]] (W = 1 ⇒ S = 0 at 0 K).
