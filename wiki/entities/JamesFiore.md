# James M. Fiore

Professor Emeritus at [[MohawkValleyCommunityCollege|Mohawk Valley Community College]] (MVCC), Utica NY; long-time author and contributor to free/shareware computer software, and the author of a sustained series of Open Educational Resource ([[OER]]) textbooks and lab manuals in electrical engineering technology and computer engineering technology — all released under Creative Commons share-alike licenses rather than via a traditional publisher.

OER titles in the series (per [[embedded-controllers-fiore|*Embedded Controllers Using C and Arduino*]]'s introduction):

- *Embedded Controllers Using C and Arduino* — the source ingested here.
- *Computer Programming with Python* (companion course in the same curriculum).
- *Science of Sound* (audio / DSP).
- *Operational Amplifiers & Linear Integrated Circuits*.
- *Semiconductor Devices*.
- *DC Electrical Circuit Analysis*.
- *AC Electrical Circuit Analysis*.

Each text has a matching laboratory manual. Web presence: [www.mvcc.edu/jfiore](https://www.mvcc.edu/jfiore), [www.dissidents.com](https://www.dissidents.com), [www.jimfiore.org](https://www.jimfiore.org); YouTube channel "Electronics with Professor Fiore".
