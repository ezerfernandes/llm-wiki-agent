# Hamel Husain

ML practitioner and writer. Cited in [[ai-engineering-ch05-prompt-engineering|*AI Engineering* Ch 5]] for his **"Show Me the Prompt"** essay (Feb 14, 2024) — the canonical articulation of the *inspect-the-prompts-your-tools-generate* philosophy for [[PromptEngineeringTools|prompt engineering tools]].

> "Hamel Husain codified this philosophy wonderfully in his blog post 'Show Me the Prompt' (February 14, 2024)." — Ch 5 footnote

## Why it matters

The essay frames a defense for the practitioner against [[PromptEngineeringTools|prompt engineering tools]]'s opacity: tools generate hidden API calls, default prompts may contain typos (Ch 5's [[LangChain]] screenshot), and tool behavior can change without notice. The defense is to always **read the actual prompts** the tool generates — not just trust the abstraction.
