# Zalando

German e-commerce company (founded 2008, Berlin) whose research arm — **Zalando Research** — released [[FashionMNIST|Fashion-MNIST]] in 2017 (Xiao, Rasul & Vollgraf) as a drop-in replacement for the venerable [[MNIST]] benchmark. Fashion-MNIST has since become the canonical pedagogical CV dataset for textbooks (including [[d2l-preface|*Dive into Deep Learning*]]) and tutorials, displacing MNIST in nearly all "first classifier" exercises.
