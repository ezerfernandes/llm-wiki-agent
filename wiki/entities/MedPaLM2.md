# Med-PaLM 2

[[google|Google]]'s **medical-domain LLM** — combines [[LargeLanguageModel|LLM]] capability with medical training data to answer medical queries with higher accuracy. Named in [[ai-engineering-ch02-foundation-models|*AI Engineering* Ch 2]]:

> "Google's Med-PaLM2 combined the power of an LLM with medical data to answer medical queries with higher accuracy."

## Position in Ch 2

Med-PaLM 2 is one of three biomedical [[DomainSpecificModel|domain-specific foundation models]] named in Ch 2, alongside [[AlphaFold]] and [[BioNeMo]]. Together they make Huyen's case that **biomedicine is the most active domain** for FM specialization — driven by privacy-restricted data (medical records, scans) and high stakes.
