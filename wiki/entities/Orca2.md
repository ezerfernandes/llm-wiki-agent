# Orca 2

A finetuned LLM family from [[microsoft|Microsoft]] Research (Mitra et al. 2023), trained on synthetic reasoning data to teach smaller models how to reason. Per [[ai-engineering-ch07-finetuning|*AI Engineering* Ch 7]], cited in the [[Ovadia2024FineTuningOrRetrieval|Ovadia et al. (2024)]] benchmark as one of the three test models for RAG-vs-finetuning comparison:

| Setup | Orca 2-7B |
|---|---|
| Base | 0.456 |
| Base + RAG | **0.876** |
| FT-reg | 0.511 |
| FT-par | 0.566 |
| FT-reg + RAG | 0.820 |
| FT-par + RAG | 0.826 |

Orca 2 base + RAG was the winner across the three models tested. This is one of the data points underpinning Ch 7's *"finetuning is for form, RAG is for facts"* thesis for current-events QA.

## What's distinctive about Orca 2

Orca 2's training methodology — synthetic reasoning chains generated by GPT-4, with explanation traces — was an early influential example of **reasoning-focused distillation**. The base model is Llama 2; the differentiator is the data curation rather than the architecture or training algorithm.
