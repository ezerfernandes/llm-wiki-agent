# Chegg

Public homework-help and tutoring service. Cited in [[ai-engineering-ch01-intro|*AI Engineering* Ch 1]] as the **canonical example of an incumbent disrupted by ChatGPT**: share price fell from **$28 when ChatGPT launched (November 2022) to $2 in September 2024**, as students migrated to free LLM-based help. Used by Huyen as the cautionary case against "wait-and-see" responses to foundation-model disruption — contrasted with [[KhanAcademy]] and [[Duolingo]], which embraced AI integration.
