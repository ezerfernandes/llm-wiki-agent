# a16z

**Andreessen Horowitz** — Silicon Valley venture capital firm whose **enterprise-AI research** is cited multiple times in [[ChipHuyen|Chip Huyen]]'s *AI Engineering*.

## From [[ai-engineering-ch03-evaluation-methodology|AI Engineering Ch 3]]

a16z's 2023 enterprise survey found that **6 of 70 decision-makers evaluated models by word of mouth** — a striking measure of how unsystematic real-world model selection still is, even at well-resourced enterprises.

## From [[ai-engineering-ch04-evaluate-ai-systems|AI Engineering Ch 4]]

a16z's 2024 enterprise study (Figure 4-8 in Ch 4) identifies the **two top reasons enterprises choose open-source models**: **control** and **customizability**. Other reasons (cost, privacy, performance) also feature but these two dominate.

This finding anchors the [[ModelBuildVsBuy|build-vs-buy]] discussion's control/access/transparency axis — proprietary models are over-censored, opaque about version changes, and revocable; open-source addresses all three even at the cost of engineering effort.
