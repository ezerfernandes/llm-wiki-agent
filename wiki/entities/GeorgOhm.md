# Georg Ohm

**Georg Simon Ohm** was the German physicist who experimentally established the relationship now called [[OhmsLaw|Ohm's law]] — that the [[ElectricCurrent|current]] in a metal conductor is directly proportional to the applied [[Voltage|voltage]], with [[ElectricalResistance|resistance]] as the constant of proportionality (V = IR). The SI unit of electrical resistance, the **ohm (Ω)**, is named in his honor.

(In this chapter Ohm appears specifically as the namesake and discoverer of the empirical voltage–current–resistance law underpinning circuit analysis.)
