## Overview
Christian Johann Doppler (1803–1853) was an Austrian physicist and mathematician who first described the frequency shift that occurs when a wave source and observer move relative to one another — the phenomenon now called the [[DopplerEffect]].

## Key Points
- Investigated how relative motion changes observed frequency using both stationary and moving musical sources and observers, including experiments with musicians playing on moving trains.
- His insight applies to all waves, not just [[Sound]]: it underlies the astronomical **redshift** of receding galaxies, Doppler weather radar, radar speed detection, and Doppler-shifted [[Ultrasound]] for measuring blood flow.
