## What it is
Langfuse is an open-source LLM engineering platform for prompt monitoring, tracing, evaluation, and prompt management, with a self-hostable option and a managed cloud tier.

## In LLM Engineer's Handbook
Ch. 2 ([[leh-ch02-tooling-and-installation]]) lists Langfuse alongside [[Galileo]] and [[LangSmith]] as prompt-monitoring alternatives to [[Opik]]; the authors choose Opik in part because it integrates with their Comet ML stack. Ch. 11 ([[leh-ch11-mlops-and-llmops]]) re-mentions Langfuse as a comparable LLMOps tracing tool alongside [[WeightsAndBiases]] prompt logging.
