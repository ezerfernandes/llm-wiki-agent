# John D. Hunter (1968–2012)

Creator of [[matplotlib]] (started 2002). Helped shape the scientific Python culture in the early 2000s. Close collaborator with [[FernandoPerez]] and Brian Granger ([[IPython]] / [[Jupyter]] pioneers). Mentored [[WesMcKinney]] in his early pandas work. Died of cancer in 2012 — *In Memoriam* dedication in the [[pydata-preface]].

## Connections
- [[matplotlib]] — original author.
- [[WesMcKinney]] — mentored him in early pandas era.
- [[FernandoPerez]] — collaborator on the Python scientific stack.
