# Iryna Gurevych

Professor and head of the **Ubiquitous Knowledge Processing (UKP) Lab** at TU Darmstadt, Germany. Senior author of **Sentence-BERT** (Reimers & Gurevych, EMNLP 2019, arXiv:1908.10084) with [[NilsReimers|Nils Reimers]]. Long career in NLP research spanning argumentation mining, scientific NLP, multilinguality, and embeddings.

## From [[hands-on-llm-ch02-tokens-and-embeddings|*Hands-On LLMs* Ch 2]]

Ch 2 cites the Reimers & Gurevych Sentence-BERT paper as the foundation for the [[SentenceTransformers]] library used in the worked text-embedding example.
