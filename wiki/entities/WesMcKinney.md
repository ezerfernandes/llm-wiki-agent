# Wes McKinney

Original creator of [[pandas]] and author of *Python for Data Analysis* (O'Reilly, 1st ed. 2012; 2nd 2017; 3rd 2022). Started building pandas in early 2008 at [[AQRCapitalManagement]] to fill gaps he saw in Python's tooling for tabular and time-series data. Stopped day-to-day pandas development in 2013; project has been community-led since.

## Background
- AQR Capital Management — quantitative investment firm; original pandas use case (time-series + relational ops).
- Later affiliations include Cloudera, Two Sigma, and Voltron Data; also a founder of [[Quarto]]-adjacent and Apache [[ArrowProject|Arrow]] work.

## Books
- [[pydata-preface]] / chapters [[pydata-preliminaries]] through [[pydata-data-analysis-examples]] — 3rd edition (open access at wesmckinney.com/book).
