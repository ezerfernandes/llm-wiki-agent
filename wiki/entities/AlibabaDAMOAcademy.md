# Alibaba DAMO Academy

**Alibaba DAMO Academy** (Academy for Discovery, Adventure, Momentum and Outlook) is [[alibaba|Alibaba]]'s global research lab, headquartered in Hangzhou, China. Founded in 2017. Research spans machine learning, NLP, computer vision, quantum computing, and chip design. Its NLP group is best known in the LLM-embedding community for the **GTE (General Text Embeddings)** family of sentence-embedding models (Li et al., 2023) — `gte-small`, `gte-base`, `gte-large` — which routinely top the [[MTEB|MTEB]] leaderboard across multiple size tiers.

## From [[hands-on-llm-ch05-text-clustering-topic-modeling|*Hands-On LLMs* Ch 5]]

Ch 5's chosen embedding model is **[[GTESmall|`thenlper/gte-small`]]** from Alibaba DAMO Academy — selected over Ch 4's `all-mpnet-base-v2` on the strength of its higher MTEB clustering score and faster inference at smaller dimensionality (384 vs 768).
