# Dominic Steinhöfel

**Dominic Steinhöfel** is a software-engineering researcher associated with [[CISPA]] (the home institution of [[AndreasZeller|Andreas Zeller]]), best known as the co-creator — with Zeller — of **[[ISLa]]**, the [[InputSpecificationLanguage|Input Specification Language]] and constraint solver for grammars. He and Zeller present ISLa in the paper **"Input Invariants"** at **ESEC/FSE 2022**, the work that [[fuzzingbook-17-fuzzing-with-constraints|Ch 17 of *The Fuzzing Book*]] is built around.

## Role in The Fuzzing Book
[[fuzzingbook-17-fuzzing-with-constraints|Ch 17]] credits "*Dominic Steinhöfel* and *Andreas Zeller*" with presenting "an infrastructure that allows producing inputs with arbitrary properties, but without having to go through the trouble of implementing producers or checkers" — namely [[ISLa]]. He is the maintainer of the [ISLa project](https://github.com/rindPHI/isla) (the `rindPHI` GitHub handle).
