# Léon Bottou

French computer scientist; long-time collaborator of [[YannLeCun]]; later at Microsoft Research / Facebook AI Research. Co-author of the canonical LeNet-5 paper (`LeCun.Bottou.Bengio.Haffner.1998`, "Gradient-based learning applied to document recognition") and the ATM-deployed OCR code. Independently a major figure in stochastic-gradient-descent theory ("The tradeoffs of large-scale learning"; SGD scaling analysis).

## Why he matters here

- Co-author and co-deployer of [[LeNet]] at AT&T [[BellLabs]] in the 1990s. D2L's [[d2l-convolutional-neural-networks]] notes "some ATMs still run the code that Yann LeCun and his colleague Leon Bottou wrote in the 1990s."
- Connects the [[CNN]] origin story to the [[StochasticGradientDescent|SGD]] / [[MinibatchSGD]] line of work D2L develops earlier in the curriculum.
