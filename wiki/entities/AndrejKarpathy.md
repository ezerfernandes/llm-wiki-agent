# Andrej Karpathy

A founding member of OpenAI and former Director of AI at Tesla, where he pioneered the application of deep learning to autonomous-vehicle fleets. In Reddi's *Machine Learning Systems* ([[mlsysbook-ch01-introduction|Ch 1]]) he is credited with the 2017 **[[Software2|"Software 2.0"]]** thesis: neural-network weights are the new "source code," forcing engineers to curate and version *data* rather than debug explicit logic.
