# Norm Matloff

Statistics professor at [[UCDavis|UC Davis]] and author of *Programming on Parallel Machines: GPU, Multicore, Clusters and More* — the wiki's first parallel-computing textbook (entry chapter [[parproc-ch01-intro-parallel-processing]]). Also author of *The Art of R Programming* and creator of the [[Rdsm]] R package (shared-memory threading via operator overloading on top of `snow` and `bigmemory`).

His book reflects a deliberately dual perspective: low-level systems programming ([[Pthreads]], [[MPI]], [[CUDA]]) alongside high-level scripting ([[Rlanguage|R]] with [[Snow]] and [[Rdsm]]). In Chapter 1 he flags this explicitly: "many algorithms are just too complex to understand or express easily in C/C++. So, a scripting language will be very handy, and R has good parallelization features (and is a language I use a lot)."

Hosts his global-variables essay (cited in Chapter 1) at `heather.cs.ucdavis.edu/~matloff/globals.html` — Matloff defends judicious use of global variables in threaded programming, against the standard "globals are evil" admonition.
