# Stanford OVAL

Open Virtual Assistant Lab at [[stanforduniversity|Stanford University]], led by [[MonicaLam]]. GitHub: <https://github.com/stanford-oval>. Home of the **[[STORM]]** and **[[CoSTORM|Co-STORM]]** systems for LM-powered information-seeking and long-form generation; live preview at <https://storm.genie.stanford.edu>.

## Tracked contributions

- **[[STORM]]** ([[Shao2024]], NAACL 2024) — Wikipedia-style long-form article generation via simulated multi-perspective question-asking.
- **[[2408.15232-co-storm]]** (2024) — Co-STORM: adds collaborative discourse with a moderator and a dynamic mind map, plus the [[WildSeek]] dataset.

## Research thread

OVAL's line of work targets **open-source virtual assistants and knowledge-grounded conversational systems** — distinct from the Stanford NLP / [[ChristopherPotts|Potts]] / [[OmarKhattab|Khattab]] line ([[2406.11695-mipro|MIPRO]] / [[2407.10930-better-together|BetterTogether]] / [[2507.19457-gepa|GEPA]]) that focuses on *optimizing* multi-stage LM programs. The two lines overlap in that Co-STORM and STORM are written in [[DSPy]], but OVAL's contribution is the *application-layer system design* rather than the *optimizer*.
