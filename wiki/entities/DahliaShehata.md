# Dahlia Shehata

[[universityofwaterloo|University of Waterloo]] researcher. Co-author with [[MingLi]] of [[2605.10698-bystander-effect-mas]] (Bystander Effect in MAS, May 2026) and of two cited 2026 precursors — [[SemanticHijacking]] (arxiv 2604.24512) and the [[InverseWisdomLaw]] paper (arxiv 2604.27274) — that supply the adversarial-injection methodology and the social-entropy framing used in the Bystander paper.
