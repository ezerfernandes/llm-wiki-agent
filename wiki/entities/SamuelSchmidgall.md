# Samuel Schmidgall

**Samuel Schmidgall** is the developer of **[[AgentLaboratory|Agent Laboratory]]**, an autonomous research-workflow framework (released under the MIT License) that uses specialized LLMs to automate stages of the scientific research process. Agent Laboratory and its integrated **[[AgentRxiv]]** repository are featured as the hands-on code example of the [[ExplorationAndDiscovery|Exploration and Discovery]] pattern in [[AntonioGulli|Antonio Gulli]]'s [[AgenticDesignPatterns|*Agentic Design Patterns*]] (Ch 21).
