# Goku Mohandas

Creator of [[MadeWithML]], the open-source MLOps course. Prior background at Apple ML and a Bay Area healthcare ML startup; now at [[Anyscale]]. Sole author of the 39-lesson curriculum that integrates the [[Ray]] / [[Anyscale]] stack with the wider MLOps tooling ecosystem ([[MLflow]], [[GitHubActions]], [[DVC]], [[GreatExpectations]], etc.).
