# Tianyu Gao

Researcher in NLP / embeddings / contrastive learning. **First author of [[SimCSE]]** (Gao, Yao & Chen 2021, arXiv:2104.08821 — *"SimCSE: Simple Contrastive Learning of Sentence Embeddings"*) with [[XingchengYao|Xingcheng Yao]] and [[DanqiChen|Danqi Chen]] (advisor) at Princeton.

PhD candidate at Princeton at time of SimCSE publication.

## From [[hands-on-llm-ch10-creating-text-embedding-models|*Hands-On LLMs* Ch 10]]

Ch 10 cites the SimCSE paper: *"Tianyu Gao, Xingcheng Yao, and Danqi Chen. 'SimCSE: Simple contrastive learning of sentence embeddings.' arXiv preprint arXiv:2104.08821 (2021)."*
