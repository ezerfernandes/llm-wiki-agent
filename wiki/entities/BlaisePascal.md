## Overview
Blaise Pascal (1623–1662) was a French philosopher, mathematician, and scientist after whom [[PascalsPrinciple]] and the SI unit of [[Pressure]] (the pascal, Pa) are named.

## Key Facts
- French; lived 1623–1662.
- Homeschooled by his father, who reportedly removed math texts and forbade mathematics until age 15 — which only fueled the boy's curiosity, so that by age 12 he was teaching himself geometry.
- Made major contributions to probability theory, number theory, and geometry.
- Credited as the inventor of the first mechanical digital calculator (the Pascaline).
- In fluid statics, his name is attached to the principle that a pressure change applied to a confined fluid transmits undiminished throughout it — the foundation of hydraulics.
