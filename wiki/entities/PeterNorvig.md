# Peter Norvig

**Peter Norvig** is the computer scientist best known for co-authoring (with [[StuartRussell|Stuart Russell]]) *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach* (Prentice Hall, 1995) — the field-defining AI textbook. He served as Director of Research and Director of Search Quality at [[google|Google]] for many years; previously held senior positions at NASA Ames.

## Position in the wiki

[[ChipHuyen|Huyen]] cites *AIMA* in [[ai-engineering-ch06-rag-agents|*AI Engineering* Ch 6]] as the definitional anchor for the **agent** concept — *"the study and design of rational agents"* (the book's framing of the AI field) and the agent definition (*"anything that can be viewed as perceiving its environment through sensors and acting upon that environment through actuators"*).
