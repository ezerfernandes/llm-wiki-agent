# Epoch AI

Independent AI-progress evaluation organization. Maintains the [[FrontierMath]] benchmark and ran the **blind** Tier-4 evaluation of the [[AICoMathematician|AI co-mathematician]] documented in [[2605.06651v2-ai-co-mathematician]] (23/48 problems = 48%, a new high score).

Related to but distinct from [[epochcapabilitiesindex|Epoch Capabilities Index]].
