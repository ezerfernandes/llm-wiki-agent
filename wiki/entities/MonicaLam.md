# Monica S. Lam

Senior author of [[2408.15232-co-storm|Co-STORM (2024)]] and the [[Shao2024|STORM (2024)]] paper. Faculty at [[stanforduniversity|Stanford]] Computer Science; leads the [[StanfordOVAL|Open Virtual Assistant Lab (OVAL)]]. Contact: lam@cs.stanford.edu.

## Tracked contributions

- **[[2408.15232-co-storm]]** (2024) — senior author. The Co-STORM system: collaborative-discourse LM-agent information-seeking with a dynamic mind map.
- **[[STORM]]** ([[Shao2024]], NAACL 2024) — senior author. The Wikipedia-style long-form generation system that Co-STORM extends.

## Research line

Lam's OVAL lab has a long arc of work on **open-source virtual assistants and knowledge-grounded conversational systems**. The STORM → Co-STORM pair represents the lab's move from *single-pass long-form generation* into *interactive learning assistance*, with the [[UnknownUnknowns]] framing as the central design motivation.
