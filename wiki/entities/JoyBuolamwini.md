# Joy Buolamwini

Joy Buolamwini is a computer scientist and AI-ethics researcher, founder of the Algorithmic Justice League, and lead author of the 2018 [[GenderShades|Gender Shades]] study at the [[MITMediaLab|MIT Media Lab]]. She is cited in [[mlsysbook-ch15-responsible-engineering|mlsysbook Vol 1 Ch 15]] as a central figure in algorithmic-fairness auditing.

## Why she matters here
- With co-author [[TimnitGebru|Timnit Gebru]], Buolamwini audited commercial facial-analysis systems and found error rates from 0.8% (light-skinned males) to 34.7% (dark-skinned females) — a >43× disparity invisible in aggregate accuracy, which catalyzed ~20× error reductions by vendors and the broader [[DisaggregatedEvaluation|disaggregated-evaluation]] methodology.
