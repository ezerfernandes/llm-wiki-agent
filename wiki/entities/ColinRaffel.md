# Colin Raffel

NLP researcher; **first author of the [[t5|T5]] paper** — Raffel et al., *"Exploring the limits of transfer learning with a unified text-to-text transformer,"* JMLR 21:140 (2020) — the paper that established the [[texttotextframework|text-to-text framework]] as a unified interface for NLP tasks and demonstrated encoder-decoder superiority on text-to-text tasks at matched compute.

Formerly at [[google|Google Brain]] (T5 era); subsequently UNC Chapel Hill and Hugging Face.

## In the wiki

Cited as **footnote 7 of [[hands-on-llm-ch04-text-classification|*Hands-On LLMs* Ch 4]]** when the chapter introduces T5:

> "An interesting family of models that leverage this architecture is the Text-to-Text Transfer Transformer or T5 model. ... its architecture is similar to the original Transformer where 12 decoders and 12 encoders are stacked together. [Colin Raffel et al., 2020]" — Ch 4
