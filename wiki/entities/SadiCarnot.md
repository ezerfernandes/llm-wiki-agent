# Sadi Carnot (1796–1832)

**Sadi Carnot** was a young French engineer who, in 1824 amid the Industrial Revolution's rise of heat-engine technology, devised the theoretical [[CarnotCycle|Carnot cycle]] — the most efficient possible cyclical process for a [[HeatEngine|heat engine]] operating between two temperatures.

## Contributions
- Formulated the [[CarnotCycle]] (reversible isothermal + adiabatic steps) and the principle that no engine between two temperatures can exceed a reversible engine's efficiency.
- His framework gave a theoretical ceiling on engine efficiency (Eff_C = 1 − T_c/T_h) and helped clarify what became the [[SecondLawOfThermodynamics|second law of thermodynamics]].
