# PromptBase

Public prompt marketplace where users **buy and sell** prompts. Cited in [[ai-engineering-ch05-prompt-engineering|*AI Engineering* Ch 5]] as an example of the commercial-prompt-marketplace tier.

> "Some even let users sell and buy prompts (see PromptBase)." — Ch 5

## Position

PromptBase represents the **paid** tier of public prompt sharing — distinct from the upvote-based [[PromptHero]] and [[CursorDirectory]]. Its existence is part of Ch 5's evidence that "functioning prompts can be quite valuable" — and the foundation for the chapter's debate about whether prompts can be patented.
