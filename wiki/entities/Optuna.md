# Optuna

Hyperparameter optimization library with define-by-run search spaces. Mentioned alongside [[HyperOpt]] as a [[RayTune]] search backend in [[madewithml-mlops-tuning]]. Also cited by [[d2l-hyperparameter-optimization]] §hyperopt-api as one of three production HPO libraries (alongside [[RayTune]] and [[SyneTune]]) implementing the searcher/scheduler decomposition the chapter formalizes.
