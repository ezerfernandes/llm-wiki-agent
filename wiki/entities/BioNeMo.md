# BioNeMo

[[NVIDIA]]'s foundation-model platform focused on **biomolecular data for drug discovery**. Named in [[ai-engineering-ch02-foundation-models|*AI Engineering* Ch 2]] as one of the canonical biomedical [[DomainSpecificModel|domain-specific foundation models]]:

> "NVIDIA's BioNeMo is another model that focuses on biomolecular data for drug discovery."

## Why it matters in the Ch 2 framing

BioNeMo, alongside [[AlphaFold]] ([[googledeepmind|DeepMind]]) and [[MedPaLM2]] ([[google|Google]]), illustrates Ch 2's argument that **biomedicine is the most-active domain for FM specialization** — driven by data formats that don't appear on the open internet (protein sequences, DNA/RNA, fMRI scans) and high stakes that motivate the specialization investment.
