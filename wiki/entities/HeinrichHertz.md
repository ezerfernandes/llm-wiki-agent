## Overview
Heinrich Hertz was the physicist who, in 1887, first experimentally generated and detected [[ElectromagneticWave|electromagnetic waves]], confirming the prediction of [[JamesClerkMaxwell|Maxwell's]] theory ([[MaxwellsEquations]]). The SI unit of frequency, the hertz (Hz), is named in his honor.

## Key Contributions
- Built an AC RLC circuit feeding a wire loop with a small spark gap; sparks across the gap radiated EM waves through the air.
- Detected the waves with a second, tuned receiving loop across the laboratory that sparked when waves arrived.
- Measured interference patterns to find the wavelength and confirmed the waves propagated at the speed of light, validating Maxwell's prediction and laying groundwork for radio and wireless communication.
