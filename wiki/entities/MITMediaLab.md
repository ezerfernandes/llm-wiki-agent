# MIT Media Lab

The MIT Media Lab is an interdisciplinary research laboratory at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology ([[MITAcademic|MIT]]). It is cited in [[mlsysbook-ch15-responsible-engineering|mlsysbook Vol 1 Ch 15]] as the home of the [[GenderShades|Gender Shades]] study.

## Why it matters here
- Where [[JoyBuolamwini|Joy Buolamwini]] (with [[TimnitGebru|Timnit Gebru]]) conducted the 2018 Gender Shades facial-analysis audit that exposed a >43× error disparity across skin tone and gender, catalyzing industry remediation and the disaggregated-evaluation methodology.
