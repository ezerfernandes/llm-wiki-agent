# Ray

Distributed compute framework from [[Anyscale]]. Core substrate for [[RayData]], [[RayTrain]], [[RayTune]], and [[RayServe]] used throughout the [[MadeWithML]] MLOps track; alternatives include [[ApacheSpark]] and [[Dask]].
