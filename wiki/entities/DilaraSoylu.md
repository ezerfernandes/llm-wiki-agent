# Dilara Soylu

[[stanforduniversity|Stanford]] NLP researcher; **lead author of [[2407.10930-better-together|Soylu, Potts & Khattab 2024 (BetterTogether)]]** — the first published bi-axial (prompts + weights) optimizer for [[CompoundAISystem|compound AI systems]], released as `dspy.BetterTogether` in [[DSPy]]. Supported by the Ravi Family Graduate Fellowship and IBM (founding member of the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI). Continues on the Stanford-side author cluster of [[2507.19457-gepa|GEPA]] (ICLR 2026 Oral).

## Tracked contributions

- **[[2407.10930-better-together]]** (arXiv 2024) — **lead author**. Introduces the [[BetterTogether]] algorithm and the [[CompoundAISystem]] formalism $\Phi_{\langle\Theta,\Pi\rangle}$ that [[2507.19457-gepa|GEPA]] later adopts.
- **[[2507.19457-gepa]]** (ICLR 2026 Oral) — Stanford-side co-author on the GEPA team alongside [[ChristopherPotts]].
