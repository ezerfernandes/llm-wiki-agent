# IUPAC

**IUPAC** (the International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry) is the international standards body whose conventions govern chemical naming and the layout of the [[PeriodicTable|periodic table]]. In OpenStax *Chemistry 2e* Chapter 2, IUPAC is cited as:

- The authority recommending that the periodic table's 18 columns be numbered **groups 1–18**.
- The source of the systematic [[ChemicalNomenclature|chemical nomenclature]] rules for naming ionic compounds, molecular (covalent) compounds, and acids.
