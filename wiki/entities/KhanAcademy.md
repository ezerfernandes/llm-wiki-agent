# Khan Academy

Non-profit education organization (founded by Salman Khan) providing free online courses. Cited in [[ai-engineering-ch01-intro|*AI Engineering* Ch 1]] as offering **AI-powered teaching assistants to students and course assistants to teachers** (Khanmigo). Representative of how incumbent education organizations integrate foundation models rather than treating them as a competitive threat — a counterpoint to [[Chegg]], whose stock collapsed when ChatGPT replaced its homework-help service.
