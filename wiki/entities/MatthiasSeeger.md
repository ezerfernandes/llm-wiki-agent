# Matthias Seeger

[[Amazon]] principal applied scientist; co-author of the D2L *Hyperparameter Optimization* chapter ([[d2l-hyperparameter-optimization]]). Long-standing contributor to Bayesian machine learning and Gaussian-process methods; one of the architects of [[SyneTune]]. Earlier career: EPFL / Saarland University; widely cited GP-classification and approximate-inference work.
