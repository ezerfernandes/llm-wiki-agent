## Description
Thomas Young is the physicist credited with the double slit experiment that demonstrated the wave nature of light through interference. His name is attached to [[DoubleSlitExperiment]] (Young's double slit experiment).

## Connections
- [[DoubleSlitExperiment]] — interference demonstration named for him
- [[WaveOptics]], [[WaveInterference]]
- [[ChristiaanHuygens]] — earlier wave theorist whose principle underlies the experiment
