# Michael I. Jordan

American machine-learning researcher; Pehong Chen Distinguished Professor at UC Berkeley (Departments of EECS and Statistics). One of the most influential figures in modern ML — pioneered variational inference, graphical models, Bayesian nonparametrics, and large-scale ML systems. Doctoral advisor to a generation of ML leaders ([[AndrewNg|Andrew Ng]] among them).

## From [[hands-on-llm-ch05-text-clustering-topic-modeling|*Hands-On LLMs* Ch 5]]

Cited as a co-author of [[LatentDirichletAllocation|LDA]] — Blei, Ng & Jordan, **JMLR** 3 (2003): 993–1022 — the classical Bayesian topic-modeling paper Ch 5 contrasts BERTopic against. (Distinguished from Michael Jordan the basketball player, who occasionally appears in his Berkeley NLP slides as a disambiguation joke.)
