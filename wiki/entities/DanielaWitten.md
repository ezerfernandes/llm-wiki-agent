# Daniela Witten

Biostatistics professor at [[UniversityOfWashington]] (assistant prof. at time of ISLR's first printing). Research focus: high-dimensional statistical machine learning, especially genomics applications. Member of the Institute of Medicine committee that led to the *Evolution of Translational Omics* report. Co-author of [[islr-seventh-printing|An Introduction to Statistical Learning]].
