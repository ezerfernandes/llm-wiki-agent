# Kuldip Paliwal

Indian-Australian electrical engineer; emeritus professor at Griffith University (Brisbane). Long career in speech processing, signal modeling, and digital communications. Co-author with [[MikeSchuster|Mike Schuster]] of the **bidirectional recurrent neural networks** paper (Schuster & Paliwal 1997).

## Why he matters here

- **Bidirectional RNN (1997).** The bi-RNN paper introduced the design pattern of running two unidirectional RNNs in opposite directions over the same input and concatenating their hidden states — useful for sequence encoding tasks (POS tagging, masked-token pretraining) where conditioning on full context is appropriate ([[d2l-recurrent-modern]] §bi-rnn).
