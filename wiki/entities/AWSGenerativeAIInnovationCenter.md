# AWS Generative AI Innovation Center

The **AWS Generative AI Innovation Center** is the [[Amazon]] research unit that pairs AWS engineers and applied scientists with customers to build production GenAI solutions. The lead institution on [[2604.14585-prompt-optimization-coin-flip]] — the wiki's first paper from this unit, and the wiki's first empirical audit of prompt optimization in [[CompoundAISystem|compound AI systems]] published under Amazon-internal sponsorship.

## Wiki footprint

- [[2604.14585-prompt-optimization-coin-flip]] — paper authored by [[XingZhang]], [[GuanghuiWang]], [[YanweiCui]] & [[PeiyangHe]] (corresponding) at the Center, with [[HSBC]] Technology Center China collaborators.
- Establishes the **49% coin-flip failure rate** for prompt optimization on [[ClaudeHaiku45]] across six methods × four tasks × three repeats.
- Introduces the [[ANOVAVarianceDecomposition|ANOVA variance decomposition]] methodology for testing agent coupling in [[CompoundAISystem|compound AI systems]].
- Benchmarks both [[ClaudeHaiku45|Claude Haiku 4.5]] and [[AmazonNovaLite|Amazon Nova Lite]] as executor models — a deliberately cross-vendor evaluation choice (Anthropic's Haiku and Amazon's own Nova Lite).
