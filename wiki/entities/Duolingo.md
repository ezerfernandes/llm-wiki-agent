# Duolingo

Public language-learning platform. Cited in [[ai-engineering-ch01-intro|*AI Engineering* Ch 1]] via the **Pajak and Bicknell (2022) Duolingo paper** on AI-assisted course creation — which found that of four stages (content sourcing, lesson assembly, lesson personalization, assessment), **lesson personalization benefits the most from AI**. This is the chapter's canonical reference for where in an education pipeline AI delivers the most leverage.
