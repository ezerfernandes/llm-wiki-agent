# Ashish Vaswani

NLP / deep-learning researcher; first author of **"Attention is All You Need"** ([[1706.03762-attention-is-all-you-need|Vaswani et al., 2017]]) at [[google|Google]] Brain — the paper that introduced the [[transformer|Transformer]] architecture and removed recurrence from sequence-transduction models, enabling the parallel-training-at-scale that defines modern LLMs. Cited in [[hands-on-llm-ch01-introduction-to-llms|*Hands-On LLMs* Ch 1]] as the architectural pivot from RNN+attention to pure-attention Transformers — *"the well-known 'Attention is all you need' paper released in 2017."*
