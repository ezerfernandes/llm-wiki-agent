# Jigsaw

[[google|Google]]'s technology subsidiary focused on countering threats to open societies — including online harassment, disinformation, and censorship. Per [[ai-engineering-ch04-evaluate-ai-systems|*AI Engineering* Ch 4]], Jigsaw's notable AI-engineering artifact is **[[PerspectiveAPI|Perspective API]]** — the canonical specialized toxicity-classification API.

## Significance

Perspective API is one of Ch 4's three named cheap specialized [[Safety|safety]] classifiers (alongside [[FacebookHateSpeech]] and [[SkolkovoToxicityClassifier]]). These are positioned as competitively-priced alternatives to running GPT-4-as-judge for toxicity detection.
