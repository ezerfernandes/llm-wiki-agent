# Konrad Zuse (1910–1995)

German civil engineer and computer pioneer. Designed and built the **[[Z3]]** (1941) — the first programmable electromechanical **binary** computer, completed in wartime Berlin in relative isolation from the contemporaneous Anglo-American computing efforts ([[Colossus]], [[ENIAC]], [[HarvardMarkI|Mark I]]).

Per [[dis-5-1-history|*Dive into Systems* Ch 5.1]], Zuse is named to emphasize that **modern computing did not descend from a single national or institutional line** — independent inventors in different countries arrived at binary, programmable, electromechanical machines roughly simultaneously, supporting Ch 5.1's *convergence-not-linear-progression* thesis.

Also designed the **Plankalkül** (1940s), one of the first high-level programming language concepts (though not implemented contemporaneously).
