# OpenStax

**OpenStax** is a nonprofit, open-education initiative based at Rice University that publishes free, openly licensed, peer-reviewed textbooks for college and high-school courses. Its catalog spans mathematics, the sciences, the social sciences, the humanities, and business; titles are released online at no cost and under open licenses so they can be freely used and adapted.

In this wiki, OpenStax is the publisher of **[[Prealgebra]] 2e**, the source of [[prealgebra-2e-ch01-whole-numbers|Chapter 1: Whole Numbers]] and any further chapters ingested from the same book.
