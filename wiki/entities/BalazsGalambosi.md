# Balázs Galambosi

Computed the **January 2024 [[BenchmarkCorrelation|benchmark-correlation]] table** for HuggingFace's [[OpenLLMLeaderboard|Open LLM Leaderboard]] — cited in [[ai-engineering-ch04-evaluate-ai-systems|*AI Engineering* Ch 4]]:

> "Table 4-5 shows the Pearson correlation scores among the six benchmarks used on Hugging Face's leaderboard, computed in January 2024 by Balázs Galambosi."

## Significance

Galambosi's correlation analysis is the empirical backbone of Ch 4's argument that **correlated benchmarks exaggerate biases** — the finding that [[ARCC]] / [[mmlu|MMLU]] / [[WinoGrande]] are correlated at ≈0.87-0.90 directly motivates HuggingFace's June 2024 leaderboard refresh.
