## What it is
Jim Dowling is the CEO and co-founder of [[Hopsworks]] and a leading writer on production MLOps architecture. He is the originator of the **Feature/Training/Inference (FTI) pipeline pattern**.

## In LLM Engineer's Handbook
Ch. 1 ([[leh-ch01-understanding-llm-twin-concept]]) explicitly credits Dowling's 2024 Hopsworks-blog essay "From MLOps to ML Systems with Feature/Training/Inference Pipelines" as the inspiration for the FTI architecture that structures the entire LLM Twin project. The chapter cites him by name when introducing FTI as the architectural backbone for the book.
