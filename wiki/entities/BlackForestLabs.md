# Black Forest Labs

**Black Forest Labs** is the AI research company behind the **Flux** family of text-to-image diffusion models — *Flux Schnell* (open weights, fastest), *Flux Dev* (open weights, non-commercial), and *Flux Pro* (closed, API-only, highest quality). The founding team is composed of researchers who previously worked on the [[StableDiffusion|Stable Diffusion]] line at Stability AI / CompVis.

## In the wiki

- **First wiki receipt: [[dspy-image-generation-prompting-tutorial]]** — the dspy.ai *Image Generation Prompt Iteration* tutorial uses [[FluxPro|Flux Pro v1.1 Ultra]] (hosted by [[FAL]] at `fal-ai/flux-pro/v1.1-ultra`) as the image generator inside a [[DSPy]] critic-then-revise loop.
