# Dmitri Mendeleev

**Dmitri Mendeleev** was a Russian chemist who in **1869** published an arrangement of the [[ChemicalElement|elements]] by increasing atomic mass that became the basis of the modern [[PeriodicTable|periodic table]]. Lothar Meyer (Germany) reached a similar arrangement independently in 1870.

Mendeleev's key advance was **predicting undiscovered elements** — leaving gaps for elements with properties like aluminum and silicon. The discovery of **gallium (1875)** and **germanium (1886)** matched his predictions, validating the periodic arrangement. His contributions are now the most widely recognized in the table's development.

The modern [[PeriodicTable|periodic law]] later refined the organizing principle from atomic mass to [[AtomicNumberAndMassNumber|atomic number]].
