# Karen Simonyan

British computer-vision researcher; co-author with [[AndrewZisserman|Andrew Zisserman]] of the **VGG** paper (Simonyan & Zisserman 2014) at the Visual Geometry Group, [[oxforduniversity|University of Oxford]]. Later joined [[googledeepmind|DeepMind]] and worked on the AlphaGo / AlphaZero / AlphaFold programs and on multimodal / video generation. Co-founder and Chief Scientist of Inflection AI; later joined [[Microsoft|Microsoft AI]].

## Why he matters here

- **VGG (2014).** Co-authored the architecture that introduced **blocks of small $3\times3$ convolutions** and the doctrine "deep and narrow beats shallow." VGG runner-up at ILSVRC 2014 (behind [[GoogLeNet]]) and became — alongside [[ResNet]] — one of the two most-used CNN backbones for transfer learning ([[d2l-convolutional-modern]] §vgg).
