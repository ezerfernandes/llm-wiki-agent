# Matthew J. Van Cleave

**Matthew J. Van Cleave** is a philosopher and logic instructor, author of *[[logic-text-v2|Introduction to Logic and Critical Thinking]]* (Version 2.0, January 4, 2016), an **open textbook** released under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)** license. He teaches at Lansing Community College (Michigan) and contact is given as `vancleave@mac.com`.

## In this corpus
Sole author of the wiki's first dedicated **logic / critical-thinking** source. His stated pedagogical aims:
- Treat logic and critical thinking as a **skill** acquired by practice ("It makes about as much sense to say that one could become a critical thinker by reading a critical thinking textbook as that one could become fluent in French by reading a French textbook").
- Foreground the **informal reconstruction and evaluation of arguments** (the principle of charity, standard form, the informal test of [[Validity|validity]]) over formal apparatus.
- **Motivate why fallacies are seductive** rather than make them sound obvious — an approach he credits to [[DanielKahneman|Daniel Kahneman]]'s work in psychology.
- Make high-quality textbooks **low-cost as a matter of access and justice** (citing Martha Nussbaum on education as a basic capability).

He names **Sinnott-Armstrong & Fogelin's *Understanding Arguments*** and **Copi & Cohen's *Introduction to Logic*** as influences on his presentation.
