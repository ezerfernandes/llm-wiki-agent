## What it is
Packt (Packt Publishing) is a UK-based technical publisher specializing in books and video courses on software engineering, data, and AI. It is the publisher of "LLM Engineer's Handbook" (2024).

## In LLM Engineer's Handbook
Packt published the book ([[LLMEngineersHandbook]]) and hosts its companion repository at `github.com/PacktPublishing/LLM-Engineers-Handbook`, cloned in Ch. 2 ([[leh-ch02-tooling-and-installation]]) as the starting point for every code example. Repo links to PacktPublishing appear throughout the book.
