# Yijia Shao

Co-first author of [[2408.15232-co-storm|Co-STORM (2024)]] and **first author of the predecessor [[STORM]] system** ([[Shao2024]], NAACL 2024 — *Assisting in Writing Wikipedia-like Articles From Scratch with Large Language Models*, arXiv 2402.14207). PhD-level researcher at [[stanforduniversity|Stanford]] / [[StanfordOVAL]]. Contact: shaoyj@cs.stanford.edu. Supported by a Stanford School of Engineering Fellowship.

## Tracked contributions

- **[[STORM]]** ([[Shao2024]], NAACL 2024) — first author. The Wikipedia-like article generation system that simulates multi-perspective question-asking over a search engine and produces a long-form draft. Direct ancestor of Co-STORM and the *static-report* baseline that Co-STORM extends with interactive discourse.
- **[[2408.15232-co-storm]]** (2024) — co-first author with [[YuchengJiang]]. Adds a moderator agent, a dynamic [[CoSTORMMindMap|mind map]], and user-in-the-loop discourse to STORM.
