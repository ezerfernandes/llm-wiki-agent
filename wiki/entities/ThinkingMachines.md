# Thinking Machines Corporation

1980s/1990s American supercomputer manufacturer best known for its massively parallel **Connection Machine** [[SIMD]] systems — the CM-1 (1985) and CM-2.

[[parproc-ch01-intro-parallel-processing]] cites Thinking Machines as one of the "famous SIMD systems in computer history" alongside the ILLIAC. The Connection Machine architecture put thousands of simple processors in lockstep, all executing the same instruction on different data — the SIMD model that [[GPU|GPUs]] would later popularize.
