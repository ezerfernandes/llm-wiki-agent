# Samuel Bowman

NLP researcher (NYU; previously [[StanfordUniversity|Stanford]] PhD with Christopher Manning; now also Anthropic). Prolific author of **NLP benchmark datasets**:

- **[[SNLI]]** — Stanford Natural Language Inference Corpus (Bowman, Angeli, Potts & Manning, EMNLP 2015), the canonical [[NaturalLanguageInference|NLI]] benchmark — 500k+ labeled sentence pairs.
- **[[CoLA]]** — Corpus of Linguistic Acceptability (Warstadt, Singh & Bowman 2019), the canonical grammaticality benchmark used in GLUE.
- **MultiNLI** — multi-domain follow-up to SNLI (not yet covered in the wiki).
- **GLUE** / **SuperGLUE** — multi-task NLP benchmark suites.

Both SNLI and CoLA are operationalized in [[d2l-nlp-applications]] (§`natural-language-inference-and-dataset` and §`finetuning-bert` respectively).
