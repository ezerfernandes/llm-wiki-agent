# Jensen Huang

Co-founder and CEO of [[NVIDIA]] (founded 1993). In [[ai-engineering-ch01-intro|*AI Engineering* Ch 1]], Huang is quoted on the [[FoundationModelUseCases|coding use case]] as taking the **maximalist position on AI-displacing-software-engineers**: he predicts AI will replace human software engineers and that "we should stop saying kids should learn to code." Huyen pairs this against the opposing view that software engineers will never be replaced for technical and emotional reasons, framing it as the high-stakes industry debate of the moment.
