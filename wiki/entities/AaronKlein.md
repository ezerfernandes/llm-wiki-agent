# Aaron Klein

[[Amazon]] research scientist; first author of the D2L *Hyperparameter Optimization* chapter ([[d2l-hyperparameter-optimization]]). Long track of [[AutoML]] / [[HyperparameterOptimization|HPO]] work: contributor to **[[SyneTune]]** (Amazon's distributed HPO library) and an originator of multi-fidelity HPO benchmarks. Prior to Amazon, PhD at Universität Freiburg (Hutter lab).
