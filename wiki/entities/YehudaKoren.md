# Yehuda Koren

Recommender-systems researcher most associated with **matrix factorization for collaborative filtering** and the [[NetflixPrize|Netflix \$1M Prize]] (2006–2009) — was a core member of the winning **BellKor's Pragmatic Chaos** ensemble. Cited in [[d2l-recommender-systems]] for two seminal papers:

- **Koren, Bell & Volinsky 2009** — *Matrix Factorization Techniques for Recommender Systems* (IEEE Computer): the canonical formalization of the user-latent / item-latent / user-bias / item-bias model that D2L's [[MatrixFactorization]] section is built on.
- **Hu, Koren & Volinsky 2008** — *Collaborative Filtering for Implicit Feedback Datasets*: the foundational reference for the [[ImplicitFeedback]] / [[ExplicitFeedback]] taxonomy and weighted-regularized-MF treatment of implicit signals.

Career: AT&T Labs Research → Yahoo Research → Google.
