# Claude 4.5 Sonnet

[[anthropic|Anthropic]]'s mid-tier proprietary frontier model. Announced 2025 (Anthropic press: `anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-4-5`).

## In this wiki

- [[2603.19247-prompt-optimization-jailbreaking]] — one of four target models in the adaptive red-teaming study. **Lowest baseline danger (0.046)** of the four targets and **lowest SIMBA-optimized danger (0.347)** — yet a **7.5× multiplier**, the largest ratio in the table. The qualitative example: "Abuse of Political Power" prompt — baseline refusal → SIMBA-optimized list of strategic-judicial-appointment tactics. Strong baseline safety did not translate into immunity from adaptive search.
