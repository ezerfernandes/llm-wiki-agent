# Percy Liang

**Professor at Stanford University**; director of the Stanford Center for Research on Foundation Models (CRFM). One of the most influential NLP / foundation-model researchers; **last author** of [[NelsonFLiu|Nelson F. Liu]] / Tianyi Zhang / Liang's 2023 *"Evaluating verifiability in generative search engines"* paper (arXiv:2304.09848) — the source Ch 8 cites for the four-axis [[RAGEvaluation|RAG-evaluation]] taxonomy.

## From [[hands-on-llm-ch08-semantic-search-and-rag|*Hands-On LLMs* Ch 8]]

Cited as last author of the 2023 generative-search-engines verifiability paper that defines the **fluency / perceived utility / citation recall / citation precision** axes Ch 8 names as the canonical RAG-evaluation framework. The Ch 8 footnote: *"Nelson F. Liu, Tianyi Zhang, and Percy Liang. 'Evaluating verifiability in generative search engines.' arXiv preprint arXiv:2304.09848 (2023)."*

## Broader role

Liang's research program through 2020s spans:
- **[[FoundationModel|Foundation-model]] evaluation** — co-leads HELM (Holistic Evaluation of Language Models) at CRFM ([[HELMLite]] / [[MedHELM]] are derived benchmarks already in the wiki); the verifiability paper is part of the same evaluation-research arc.
- **Compositional generalization** in NLP (SCAN, COGS).
- **Semantic parsing** (his earlier research career; spans Stanford to early Google).
- **Reasoning + safety** in LLMs.

The Ch 8 citation is one slice of his much larger evaluation-focused research line.
