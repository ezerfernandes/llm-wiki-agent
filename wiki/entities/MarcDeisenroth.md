# Marc Peter Deisenroth

Lead author of *Mathematics for Machine Learning* ([[mml-book]], 2020, [[CambridgeUniversityPress]]). DeepMind Chair of Artificial Intelligence at University College London ([[universitycollegelondon|UCL]]); previously Senior Lecturer at [[ImperialCollegeLondon]] and a Google Research scholarship recipient.

Research focus: probabilistic modeling, Gaussian processes, reinforcement learning, and data-efficient learning for robotics (PILCO). Co-author of the original *probabilistic inference for learning control* line of work that motivated much of the "model-based RL with uncertainty" thread.

## Role in this wiki

- **Lead author** of [[mml-book]] — the book is the wiki's bridge between Corpus I (algebrica.org pure math) and Corpus VI ([[pml1-murphy|Murphy's *Probabilistic ML*]]) / Corpus II (LLM research).
- The book's six-foundations-four-pillars framing (Fig. 1.1 — Linear Algebra / Analytic Geometry / Matrix Decomposition / Vector Calculus / Probability / Optimization → Regression / Dim-Reduction / Density Estimation / Classification) is the structural choice attributable to him as the lead author.
