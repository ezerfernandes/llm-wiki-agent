# Rudolf Clausius

**Rudolf Clausius** introduced **entropy** as a thermodynamic state property, building on [[SadiCarnot]]'s analysis of steam-engine efficiency. He defined the entropy change in terms of reversible heat flow and temperature, ΔS = q_rev/T.

## Contribution
- Named and formalized [[EntropyThermodynamics|entropy (S)]] and its reversible-heat definition **ΔS = q_rev/T**.
- His work framed the [[SecondLawOfThermodynamics|second law]] in entropy terms (the entropy of the universe tends to increase), later interpreted statistically by [[LudwigBoltzmann]].
