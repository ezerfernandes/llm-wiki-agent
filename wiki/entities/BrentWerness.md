# Brent Werness

[[Amazon]] applied scientist; first author (with [[RachelHu]]) of [[d2l-appendix-mathematics|D2L's Appendix: Mathematics for Deep Learning]] — the eleven-section math reference shelf that re-derives [[LinearAlgebra]], [[Eigendecomposition]], [[SingleVariableCalculus|single-variable]] / [[MultivariableCalculus|multivariable]] / [[IntegralCalculus|integral]] calculus, [[RandomVariable|random variables]], [[MaximumLikelihoodEstimation|MLE]], common [[ProbabilityDistribution|distributions]], [[NaiveBayes|naive Bayes]], [[Statistics|statistics]], and [[InformationTheory|information theory]] from first principles for the [[d2l-preface|D2L]] textbook.
