# Cheng Soon Ong

Co-author of *Mathematics for Machine Learning* ([[mml-book]], 2020, [[CambridgeUniversityPress]]). Principal Research Scientist at [[CSIROsData61]] (Australia's national data science research arm) and Adjunct Professor at the Australian National University.

Research focus: machine learning for the natural sciences (genomics, materials, astronomy), kernel methods, structured prediction.

## Role in this wiki

- **Co-author** of [[mml-book]].
- His kernel-methods background plausibly shaped the book's Ch 12 SVM treatment and the careful Ch 3 development of inner products as the substrate for kernels.
