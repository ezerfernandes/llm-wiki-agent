# James Chadwick

**James Chadwick** was a physicist who, in **1932**, discovered the **[[Neutron|neutron]]** — the uncharged subatomic particle in the [[AtomicNucleus|nucleus]] with mass approximately equal to that of a [[Proton|proton]]. Because the neutron carries no charge, it had long evaded detection.

His discovery resolved why protons account for only about half (or less) of nuclear mass, and explained [[Isotope|isotopes]]: atoms of one element have the same proton count but can differ in neutron count, giving different masses while remaining chemically identical.
