# Johns Hopkins University

Private research university in Baltimore, Maryland. Appears in this wiki as **Jared Kaplan's home institution** — Kaplan is co-lead author of [[2001.08361-scaling-laws]] (alongside Sam McCandlish at [[OpenAI]]). Kaplan is a theoretical physicist by training; the paper's framing of language-model scaling as analogous to **statistical mechanics / the ideal gas law** reflects that background.
