# Edwin Hubble

**Edwin Hubble** was an American astronomer whose observations established that the universe is expanding, a cornerstone of modern [[Cosmology]].

## Contributions
- Discovered that distant galaxies recede from us with velocity proportional to their distance, the relation now known as [[HubblesLaw]] (v = H₀ d).
- The proportionality constant, the **Hubble constant** (H₀ ≈ 20 (km/s)/Mly), measures the expansion rate of space.
- His findings provided the observational basis for the [[BigBang]] model and for estimating the age of the universe (~13–15 billion years).

## Connections
- [[HubblesLaw]] — the velocity-distance relation he established
- [[Cosmology]] — field his work helped found observationally
- [[BigBang]] — model his expansion evidence supports
- [[DarkEnergy]] — later refinements show his expansion is accelerating
