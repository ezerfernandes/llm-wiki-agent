# Trevor Hastie

Professor of statistics at [[stanforduniversity|Stanford University]]. Co-developer of much of the statistical-modeling software and environment in R/S-PLUS; inventor of principal curves and surfaces. With [[RobertTibshirani]], developed [[GeneralizedAdditiveModels|generalized additive models]] (1986) and co-authored the textbook of that name. Co-author of [[ElementsOfStatisticalLearning|The Elements of Statistical Learning]] (with Tibshirani & Friedman) and of [[islr-seventh-printing|An Introduction to Statistical Learning]].
