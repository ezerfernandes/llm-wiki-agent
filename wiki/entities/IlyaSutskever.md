# Ilya Sutskever

Russian-Israeli-Canadian computer scientist; PhD student of [[GeoffreyHinton|Geoffrey Hinton]] at the University of Toronto. Co-author of **AlexNet** ([[AlexKrizhevsky|Krizhevsky]], **Sutskever**, [[GeoffreyHinton|Hinton]] 2012) and of the **seq2seq** paper ([[1409.3215-seq2seq]]) at [[google|Google]]. Co-founder and former Chief Scientist of [[OpenAI]].

## Why he matters here

- **AlexNet (2012).** Co-designed [[AlexNet]] and the dual-GPU `cuda-convnet` implementation. Shared first-authorship intuition for using a deep CNN on [[ImageNet]] at all — at a time when CNNs were considered "small-data only" by most of the field ([[d2l-convolutional-modern]] §alexnet).
- **Seq2seq (2014).** First author with Vinyals & Le of [[1409.3215-seq2seq]] — the encoder-decoder LSTM that established sequence-to-sequence learning as the dominant framing for neural MT (later replaced by the [[transformer|Transformer]]). [[d2l-recurrent-modern]] §seq2seq distinguishes Sutskever et al.'s "$\mathbf{c}$ initializes the decoder state only" design from [[KyunghyunCho|Cho]] et al.'s "$\mathbf{c}$ broadcast to every decoder step" design.
- **OpenAI.** Co-founder, Chief Scientist; instrumental in GPT-series strategy.
