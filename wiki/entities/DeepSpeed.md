## What it is
Microsoft's distributed training framework (ZeRO family) implementing data, pipeline, and tensor parallelism.

## In LLM Engineer's Handbook
DeepSpeed is Microsoft's distributed training framework (ZeRO family of optimizers). Per [[leh-ch08-inference-optimization]] it implements data, pipeline, and [[TensorParallelism]] and provides inference optimization via *DeepSpeed Inference* (Aminabadi et al. 2022).
