# Bard

Google's consumer conversational AI service, originally powered by [[PaLM2]] and rebranded to **Gemini** / **Gemini Advanced** following the Gemini 1.0 launch ([[2312.11805-gemini]]). Used as the SxS baseline against which Gemini Apps quality gains are measured in the Gemini paper.
