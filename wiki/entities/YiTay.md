# Yi Tay

[[google|Google]] researcher; **third author of D2L's *Recommender Systems* chapter** ([[d2l-recommender-systems]]). Better known publicly for *efficient-Transformer* / scaling work (e.g., *Long Range Arena*, *Efficient Transformers: A Survey*, *Scaling Transformers* / Charformer), and as a co-founder of **Reka AI** (post-Google). Co-author with [[ShuaiZhang]] / [[AstonZhang]] on the 2019 *Deep Learning Based Recommender System* survey that underpins the D2L chapter.
