# Aravind Srinivas

**Aravind Srinivas** is the co-founder and CEO of [[Perplexity]]. He is cited in [[ai-engineering-ch06-rag-agents|*AI Engineering* Ch 6]] for the quote that anchors Huyen's framing of [[BM25]] as a *"formidable baseline"*:

> *"Making a genuine improvement over BM25 or full-text search is hard."* — Aravind Srinivas (Twitter, footnote in Ch 6)

The quote is load-bearing for the chapter's positioning: it justifies why [[TermBasedRetrieval|term-based retrieval]] remains relevant in the embedding-based-retrieval era — coming not from a classical-IR researcher but from the CEO of a frontier LLM-search startup, which gives the position its weight.
