## Overview
Gustav Robert Kirchhoff (1824–1887) was a German physicist best known in circuit analysis for **Kirchhoff's rules** — the junction (node) rule and the loop rule — which apply conservation of charge and conservation of energy to electrical networks. These rules let one solve complex multi-loop circuits that cannot be reduced by simple series/parallel combination.

## Key Facts
- Formulated the circuit laws that bear his name, foundational to [[KirchhoffsRules]].
- Junction rule = charge conservation at a node; loop rule = energy conservation around a closed path.
- Also known for broader contributions to spectroscopy and thermal (blackbody) radiation in physics (outside this chapter's scope).
