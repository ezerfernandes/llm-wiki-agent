# Kexin Wang

Researcher in NLP / embeddings / domain adaptation. **First author of [[TSDAE]]** (Wang, Reimers & Gurevych 2021, arXiv:2104.06979 — *"TSDAE: Using Transformer-based Sequential Denoising Auto-Encoder for Unsupervised Sentence Embedding Learning"*) and **first author of [[GPL]]** (Wang, Thakur, Reimers & Gurevych 2021, arXiv:2112.07577 — *"GPL: Generative Pseudo Labeling for Unsupervised Domain Adaptation of Dense Retrieval"*).

PhD researcher at [[IrynaGurevych|Iryna Gurevych]]'s UKP Lab (TU Darmstadt) at time of both papers.

## From [[hands-on-llm-ch10-creating-text-embedding-models|*Hands-On LLMs* Ch 10]]

Ch 10 cites both Wang papers:

- *"Kexin Wang, Nils Reimers, and Iryna Gurevych. 'TSDAE: Using Transformer-based Sequential Denoising Auto-Encoder for unsupervised sentence embedding learning.' arXiv preprint arXiv:2104.06979 (2021)."*
- *"Kexin Wang et al. 'GPL: Generative Pseudo Labeling for unsupervised domain adaptation of dense retrieval.' arXiv preprint arXiv:2112.07577 (2021)."*
