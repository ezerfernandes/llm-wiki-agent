# mistral-7b-instruct-v0.2

7B-parameter instruction-tuned LM from Mistral AI (Jiang et al. 2023, *Mistral 7B*; arXiv 2310.06825). HuggingFace `mistralai/Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.2`, Apache 2.0. One of three LMs evaluated in [[2407.10930-better-together|Soylu, Potts & Khattab (2024)]]; the [[BetterTogether]] paper's *Π → Θ → Π* strategy lifts its HotPotQA score from 17.2 (vanilla) → 37.6, and *Θ → Π* lifts its Iris score from 26.0 → 66.7.
