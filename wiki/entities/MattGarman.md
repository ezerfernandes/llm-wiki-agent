# Matt Garman

CEO of [[Amazon|AWS]] (Amazon Web Services). Quoted in [[ai-engineering-ch01-intro|*AI Engineering* Ch 1]] from a leaked recording in which he predicted that **most developers will stop coding in the near future** — though framed not as the end of software developers but as a transformation of their jobs. Paired alongside [[JensenHuang|Jensen Huang's]] more aggressive prediction as evidence that frontier-tech-executives are aligned on a large structural shift in software-engineering work driven by foundation models.
