# Germain Hess

**Germain Henri Hess** (Germain Hess) is the chemist for whom **[[HessLaw|Hess's law]]** — the law of constant heat summation — is named. The law states that the [[EnthalpyChemistry|enthalpy change]] of an overall reaction equals the sum of the enthalpy changes of the steps into which it can be divided, a consequence of enthalpy being a state function.

In OpenStax *Chemistry 2e* Chapter 5 (Thermochemistry), Hess's law is presented as a core tool for computing reaction enthalpies that are difficult to measure directly, including the formation-enthalpy summation ΔH°_rxn = Σn·ΔHf°(products) − Σn·ΔHf°(reactants).
