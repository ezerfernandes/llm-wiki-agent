# fast.ai

Research and education organization co-founded by [[JeremyHoward|Jeremy Howard]] and [[RachelThomas|Rachel Thomas]], known for free practical deep-learning courses and the fastai library. Its flagship course, *[[practical-deep-learning-for-coders|Practical Deep Learning for Coders]]*, teaches deep learning top-down (code first, theory later) on [[PyTorch]] with the high-level **fastai** library layered on top. Associated with the **explained.ai** essays/books by [[TerenceParr|Terence Parr]] and Howard, including **"The Matrix Calculus You Need For Deep Learning"** ([[matrix-calculus-for-deep-learning]], NN gradients for a Calculus-1 audience) and the applied **"The Mechanics of Machine Learning"** ([[mechanics-of-ml]], a Random-Forest-centric primer for programmers) — both carry fast.ai's code-first, top-down teaching style.
