Computer scientist at [[stanforduniversity|Stanford University]]; director of the [[StanfordNLPGroup|Stanford NLP Group]] and the [[StanfordAILab|Stanford AI Lab]] (SAIL). First author of *Introduction to Information Retrieval* (Cambridge University Press 2008, with [[PrabhakarRaghavan]] & [[HinrichSchutze]]) — the canonical pre-neural IR textbook ingested across [[iir-ch01-boolean-retrieval|chapters 1]]–[[iir-ch21-link-analysis|21]] of this wiki.

Earlier textbook (with Schütze): *Foundations of Statistical Natural Language Processing* (MIT Press 1999) — predecessor in the statistical NLP line.

Author or co-author of foundational papers on dependency parsing (Stanford Parser, Stanford Dependencies), word embeddings ([[GloVe]] 2014), tree-structured LSTMs, attention-based neural MT, and contrastive sentence embeddings. Co-instructor of the long-running Stanford CS224N (NLP with Deep Learning) and CS224U (NLU) courses whose recorded lectures are a public-good corpus of NLP pedagogy.
