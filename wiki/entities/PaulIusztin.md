## What it is
Paul Iusztin is an LLM/MLOps engineer and one of the three co-authors of "LLM Engineer's Handbook: Master the Art of Engineering Large Language Models from Concept to Production" (Packt, 2024). He is also one of the two configured "users" whose crawled Medium/Substack/LinkedIn/GitHub corpora seed the book's LLM Twin running project.

## In LLM Engineer's Handbook
Iusztin co-authors every chapter and his own digital writing is used as a concrete input to the LLM Twin's data-collection pipeline ([[leh-ch03-data-engineering]] ships a `digital_data_etl_paul_iusztin.yaml` config). Across the book he is consistently the voice driving the MLOps/LLMOps system-design narrative — Ch. 1 ([[leh-ch01-understanding-llm-twin-concept]]) and Ch. 11 ([[leh-ch11-mlops-and-llmops]]) on FTI architecture, prompt monitoring, and CI/CD/CT pipelines.
