# Richard Bellman (1920–1984)

American applied mathematician at the RAND Corporation; founder of **[[DynamicProgramming|dynamic programming]]** in the 1950s. Bellman's principle — *"the remainder of an optimal trajectory is also optimal"* — and the recursive **[[BellmanEquation|Bellman equation]]** that expresses it are the algorithmic foundation of [[reinforcementlearning|reinforcement learning]], [[MarkovDecisionProcess|MDP]] theory, and countless dynamic-programming algorithms in CS (shortest paths, edit distance, HMM inference, etc.). [[d2l-reinforcement-learning]] §value-iter dates the principle to "1950s" and cites Bellman directly.
