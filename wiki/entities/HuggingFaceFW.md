## What it is
Hugging Face's FineWeb team that publishes FineWeb, FineWeb-Edu, and the FineWeb-Edu classifier.

## In LLM Engineer's Handbook
Hugging Face's FineWeb sub-team responsible for the FineWeb / FineWeb-Edu pretraining corpora and the [[FinewebEduClassifier]]. Referenced in [[leh-ch05-supervised-fine-tuning]] as the source of the canonical encoder-only quality filter applied to instruction-dataset scoring.
