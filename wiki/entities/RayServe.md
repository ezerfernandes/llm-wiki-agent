# Ray Serve

[[Ray]] library for scalable model serving with autoscaling and request batching. Powers the deployment graph in [[madewithml-mlops-serving]] and the Anyscale Services in [[madewithml-mlops-jobs-and-services]]; typically wrapped with [[FastAPI]].
