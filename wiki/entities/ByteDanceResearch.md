# ByteDance Research

The research arm of **ByteDance** (parent company of TikTok / Douyin / Toutiao). Publishes research and releases datasets / models under the `bytedance-research` organization on [[HuggingFace]]. Notable AI work spans video generation, multimodal models, RL-from-human-feedback methodology, and tool-use benchmarks.

## Relevance to the wiki

Author of [[ToolHop]] — the multi-hop tool-use benchmark used in the [[dspy-tool-use-tutorial|DSPy tool-use tutorial]] to demonstrate [[SIMBA]] prompt optimization (35.0% → 60.7% with GPT-4o). **First wiki receipt of a ByteDance-released dataset operationalized inside a DSPy program.**

## Adjacent benchmarks (forward references)

ByteDance also releases tool-related benchmarks such as ToolBench / ToolEval (not yet ingested into this wiki); ToolHop is the multi-hop composition variant.
