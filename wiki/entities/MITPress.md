# The MIT Press

Cambridge, Massachusetts / London. Academic publisher; publisher of the *Adaptive Computation and Machine Learning* series (Thomas Dietterich, editor; Christopher Bishop, David Heckerman, Michael Jordan, Michael Kearns, associate editors). The series page in [[pml1-murphy]] lists, among others:

- Sutton & Barto, *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction*
- Koller & Friedman, *Probabilistic Graphical Models*
- Rasmussen & Williams, *Gaussian Processes for Machine Learning*
- Schölkopf & Smola, *Learning with Kernels*
- Schapire & Freund, *Boosting: Foundations and Algorithms*
- [[KevinMurphy|Murphy]], *Machine Learning: A Probabilistic Perspective* (2012)
- Mohri, Rostamizadeh & Talwalkar, *Foundations of Machine Learning*
- [[KevinMurphy|Murphy]], *Probabilistic Machine Learning: An Introduction* ([[pml1-murphy]], 2022)

[[pml1-murphy]] is released under a Creative Commons CC-BY-NC-ND license — the work is freely available online while reserving derivative and commercial rights.
