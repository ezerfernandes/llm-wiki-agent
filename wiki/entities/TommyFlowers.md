# Tommy Flowers (1905–1998)

British engineer at the General Post Office Research Station, Dollis Hill. Designed and built **[[Colossus]]** (1943) at [[BletchleyPark]] — the world's first programmable **electronic digital** computer, used to break the German Lorenz cipher during WWII.

Per [[dis-5-1-history|*Dive into Systems* Ch 5.1]], Colossus predates the [[ENIAC]] (1945) by two years as a fully electronic digital machine, but stayed **classified for decades**, distorting the popular historical narrative around the origin of electronic computing. The machine was operated by the **[[WomensRoyalNavalService|Women's Royal Naval Service (WRNS)]]** — another data point in Ch 5.1's theme of underrecognized women contributors to early computing.
