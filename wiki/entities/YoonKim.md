# Yoon Kim

NLP / deep-learning researcher (MIT EECS / CSAIL; formerly Harvard, NYU). Best known as the **author of [[TextCNN|textCNN]]** — "Convolutional Neural Networks for Sentence Classification" (EMNLP 2014) — the canonical CNN-for-NLP baseline that demonstrated 1-D convolutions over pretrained word embeddings could match RNN sequence models on sentence classification with vastly less compute.

The textCNN architecture is operationalized in [[d2l-nlp-applications]] §`sentiment-analysis-cnn` as the CNN-based alternative to the [[BidirectionalRNN]] sentiment baseline.
