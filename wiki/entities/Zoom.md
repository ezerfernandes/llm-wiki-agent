# Zoom

Video-conferencing company. Per [[ai-engineering-ch04-evaluate-ai-systems|*AI Engineering* Ch 4]]:

> "In August 2023, Zoom faced a backlash after people found out the company had quietly changed its terms of service to let Zoom use users' service-generated data, including product usage data and diagnostics data, to train its AI models."

## Significance

Zoom is Ch 4's case study for the **"provider can quietly change ToS"** risk in the data-privacy axis of [[ModelBuildVsBuy|build-vs-buy]]. Even if a commercial-model provider says today that they won't train on your data, *"their policies can change."* The Zoom incident is the proof.

Sibling to [[Samsung]]'s ChatGPT leak — both are canonical [[CommercialModel|commercial-model]] data-privacy risk anecdotes.
