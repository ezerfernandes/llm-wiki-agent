# Lillian Lee

Cornell University CS professor; co-author with [[BoPang|Bo Pang]] of the foundational early-2000s sentiment-analysis papers including:

> Bo Pang and Lillian Lee. *"Seeing stars: Exploiting class relationships for sentiment categorization with respect to rating scales."* arXiv preprint cs/0506075 (2005).

The 2005 paper introduced the **[[RottenTomatoes|Rotten Tomatoes]] movie-review dataset** (5,331 positive + 5,331 negative reviews) used in *Hands-On LLMs* Ch 4.
