# R

Open-source statistical programming language and environment, descendant of S/S-PLUS. The lingua franca of academic statistics: new methods often appear in R packages years before commercial-software ports. Implementation vehicle for the [[islr-seventh-printing|ISLR]] labs, distributed alongside the companion [[ISLRPackage]] for data sets used in those labs. [[TrevorHastie]] co-developed much of the R/S-PLUS modeling stack.
