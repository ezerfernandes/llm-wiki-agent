# Asad Aali

**First author and corresponding author of [[2507.03152-medval|MedVAL: Toward Expert-Level Medical Text Validation with Language Models]]** (arXiv:2507.03152v5, 2026). PhD researcher at [[stanforduniversity|Stanford University]]; affiliated with the [[StanfordMIMI]] lab. Contact: asadaali@stanford.edu.

## Tracked contributions

- **[[2507.03152-medval|MedVAL (2026)]]** — first author. Designed the three-stage self-supervised distillation pipeline, the $\mathcal{M}_\mathrm{MedVAL}$ consistency metric, the 4-class [[RiskLevelTaxonomy|risk taxonomy]], and the [[MedVALBench]] benchmark. Also extended the [[DSPy]] local PEFT pipeline to support [[QLoRA]] via a GitHub PR.
- **MIMIC-IV-BHC dataset and benchmark** — first author of *"A dataset and benchmark for hospital course summarization with adapted large language models"*, JAMIA 32(3):470–479, 2025 (ref [5] in MedVAL). The brief-hospital-course dataset that becomes the source for the `bhc2spanish` task in [[MedVALBench]].
