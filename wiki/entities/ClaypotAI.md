# Claypot AI

US startup co-founded by [[ChipHuyen|Chip Huyen]] (CEO). Building infrastructure for **real-time machine learning** and unified ML workflow versioning. Cited in [[ChipHuyen|Huyen]]'s [[dmls-ch10-infrastructure-mlops|DMLS Ch 10]] as her startup-of-record at the time of the book.

## Context
- Operates in the [[StreamProcessing|streaming-ML]] / [[ContinualLearning|continual-learning]] infrastructure space.
- DMLS Ch 10 mentions Claypot's choice of [[Conda]] + [[Docker]] + cloud dev environments ([[GitHubCodespaces]]) for their internal stack.
- Claypot's positioning aligns with Huyen's broader argument that real-time ML platforms need first-class streaming-feature support, not retrofit batch infrastructure.
