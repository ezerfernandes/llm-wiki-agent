## What it is
Creator of llama.cpp and the GGUF quantization format.

## In LLM Engineer's Handbook
Bulgarian engineer; creator of [[llamacpp]] (2023), the C/C++ inference library that runs quantized LLMs on CPUs, Android devices, and partially-offloaded GPUs. Designer of the [[GGUF]] quantization file format that is the de-facto standard for community-quantized models on Hugging Face.
