# ARPA-H — Advanced Research Projects Agency for Health

**U.S. federal agency** modeled on DARPA, focused on high-risk / high-reward biomedical research. Funder of the **CARE — Chatbot Accuracy and Reliability Evaluation** project under which [[2507.03152-medval|MedVAL]] was developed.

## MedVAL-relevant grants

- **Contract AY2AX000045** — to [[AsadAali|Aali]] and [[AkshayChaudhari|Chaudhari]] under the CARE project.
- **Contract 1AYSAX0000024-01** — to [[AkshayChaudhari|Chaudhari]].
- **MIDRC / NIH NIBIB contract 75N92020C00021** — co-funded by ARPA-H; underwrites the Medical Imaging and Data Resource Center contribution to the project.

## CARE program

[[2507.03152-medval]] §9 Acknowledgements thanks the ARPA-H CARE program managers **Mansoor Khan** and **Dr. Berkman Sahiner** for stakeholder-needs guidance. CARE's mandate covers reliability evaluation for clinical chatbots, of which [[MedicalTextValidation]] is a core technical component.
