# MLCommons

The nonprofit consortium (launched 2020, evolving from the 2018 [[MLPerf]] effort) that develops and governs the MLPerf benchmark suites. Members include [[google|Google]], [[NVIDIA]], Intel, and leading universities. Per [[mlsysbook-ch12-benchmarking|mlsysbook Ch 12]], MLCommons addresses benchmark credibility by requiring **open submissions with full system specifications**, preventing the cherry-picking that plagued earlier benchmarks (Whetstone, LINPACK). Published results regularly reveal **10× performance differences between vendors on identical workloads**, making MLCommons the closest the field has to a SPEC-style apples-to-apples hardware comparison. [[VijayJanapaReddi|Vijay Janapa Reddi]] is a central figure in its leadership.
