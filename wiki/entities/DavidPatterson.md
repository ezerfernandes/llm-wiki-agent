# David Patterson

Computer architect (UC Berkeley), co-author of the Hennessy & Patterson *Computer Architecture: A Quantitative Approach* tradition, and a founding leader of [[MLPerf]]. In [[mlsysbook-ch12-benchmarking|mlsysbook Ch 12]] he is cited for the **fallacy-of-peak-performance** framing: peak performance is *"the performance the manufacturer guarantees you will not exceed."* The chapter's insistence on measuring sustained (not peak) throughput, and its broader quantitative-systems posture, sits squarely in his lineage.
