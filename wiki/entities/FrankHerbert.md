# Frank Herbert

American science-fiction author (1920–1986), best known for the **Dune** novels.

## From [[hands-on-llm-ch02-tokens-and-embeddings|*Hands-On LLMs* Ch 2]]

Ch 2 quotes the *Dune* line *"Thou shalt not make a machine in the likeness of a human mind"* as the running-text example for the [[Word2Vec|word2vec]] [[SkipGram|sliding-window]] explanation:

> "Let's say, for example, we have the text 'Thou shalt not make a machine in the likeness of a human mind' from the Dune novels by Frank Herbert. The algorithm uses a sliding window to generate training examples." — Ch 2

The chapter uses this sentence to walk through the (center-word, context-word) pair-generation mechanic — a small literary nod given the book's general "AI primer" framing.
