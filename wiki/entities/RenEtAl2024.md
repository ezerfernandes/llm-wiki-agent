# Ren et al. 2024 — CodeAttack paper reference

**Qibing Ren, Chang Gao, Jing Shao, Junchi Yan, Xin Tan, Wai Lam, Lizhuang Ma. 2024.** *Exploring safety generalization challenges of large language models via code.* arXiv:2403.07865.

Introduces [[CodeAttack]] — reformulates harmful text-completion as code-completion. Reported >80% [[AttackSuccessRate|ASR]] across SOTA LLMs including [[GPT4_1|GPT-4]]. Cited as the **attack benchmark** in [[dspy-guardrails|Yu & He 2024]].
