Co-founder of [[google|Google]] (with [[SergeyBrin]]) — incorporated September 1998 out of a [[stanforduniversity|Stanford]] computer-science PhD project. Co-inventor of the **[[PageRank]]** algorithm — the stationary-distribution-of-a-teleporting-random-walk ranking signal that, combined with [[AnchorText|anchor-text]] propagation, gave early Google a measurable quality advantage over the keyword-matching search engines (AltaVista, Yahoo Directory, Excite, Lycos, Inktomi) that preceded it.

The original PageRank paper *"The PageRank Citation Ranking: Bringing Order to the Web"* (Page, Brin, Motwani, Winograd 1999, Stanford technical report) and the companion *"The Anatomy of a Large-Scale Hypertextual Web Search Engine"* (Brin & Page 1998, WWW7) are the founding documents of modern web search. Both are derived from Page's Stanford PhD work under [[TerryWinograd|Terry Winograd]]; the system was originally called BackRub.

Roles at Google: CEO 2001 (founding), president of products under Eric Schmidt 2001–2011, CEO again 2011–2015, CEO of parent Alphabet Inc. 2015–2019. Largely retired from operational roles post-2019. Major adjacent ventures: investments in Kitty Hawk (electric VTOL aircraft, wound down 2022) and Planetary Resources (asteroid mining, dissolved). Listed inventor on the original PageRank patent US 6,285,999 (assigned to Stanford, exclusively licensed to Google).
