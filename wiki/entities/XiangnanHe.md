# Xiangnan He

Recommender-systems researcher; first author of **[[NeuMF|Neural Collaborative Filtering / NeuMF]]** ([[XiangnanHe|He]], Liao, Zhang, Nie, Hu, Chua, *WWW 2017*) — the canonical paper that replaced the inner-product head of matrix factorization with a learnable MLP, sparking the *deep recommender-systems* wave. Cited in [[d2l-recommender-systems]] §neumf.

Also first author of *Neural Factorization Machines* (He & Chua 2017, SIGIR), an MLP-over-bilinear-interactions complement to [[DeepFM]] that the D2L chapter references as an alternative way to combine FM with deep nets.

Career: National University of Singapore (postdoc with Tat-Seng Chua) → University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) professor.
