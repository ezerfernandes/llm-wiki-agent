# Jeremy Howard

Co-founder of **[[FastAI|fast.ai]]** (with [[RachelThomas|Rachel Thomas]]) and instructor of the free *[[practical-deep-learning-for-coders|Practical Deep Learning for Coders]]* course — built on [[PyTorch]] + [[FastAI|fastai]] with a top-down, code-first pedagogy. He brings ~30 years of ML experience and was [[Kaggle]]'s top-ranked global competitor for two years running, later its President and Chief Scientist. Cited in [[ai-engineering-ch08-dataset-engineering|*AI Engineering* Ch 8]] for an experiment with [[JonathanWhitaker|Jonathan Whitaker]] showing that **LLMs can learn from a single example** — Ch 8's anchor for the extreme low-end of the [[DataQuantity|data-quantity]] spectrum. Co-author, with [[TerenceParr|Terence Parr]], of two explained.ai works: **"The Matrix Calculus You Need For Deep Learning"** ([[matrix-calculus-for-deep-learning]]) and the applied **"The Mechanics of Machine Learning"** ([[mechanics-of-ml]]), a Random-Forest-centric primer for programmers that shares the same code-first, top-down pedagogy as his fast.ai course.

## Why he matters in Ch 8

[[ChipHuyen|Huyen]] uses the Howard + Whitaker one-shot experiment to illustrate that "how much data you need is like asking how much money you need — the answer varies widely":

> "At one extreme, Jeremy Howard and Jonathan Whitaker did a fun experiment to show that LLMs can learn from a single example. At another extreme, some teams have finetuned models with millions of examples."
