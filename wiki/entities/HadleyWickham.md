# Hadley Wickham

Statistician at Posit (formerly RStudio), prolific R package author (ggplot2, dplyr, tidyr, plyr). Coined the term ***split-apply-combine*** (2011 *Journal of Statistical Software* paper) — the organizing concept implemented in pandas as `DataFrame.groupby` and covered in [[pydata-data-aggregation]].
