# Arizona State University (ASU)

Public research university in Tempe, AZ. The **School of Computing and AI** at ASU houses [[SubbaraoKambhampati]]'s **Yochan** automated-planning lab, which produces a sustained skeptical critique of LLM planning/self-verification claims and proposes the [[LLMModuloFramework]] as the principled alternative.

## Research line in this wiki
- Empirical falsification of LLM autonomous planning ([[PlanBench]], [[Blocksworld]] / Mystery BW).
- Empirical falsification of LLM self-verification (graph coloring, self-critique iterations).
- Constructive proposal: [[LLMModuloFramework]] (Generate-Test-Critique with external sound critics).
