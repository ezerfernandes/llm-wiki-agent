# Kong

API-gateway company (Kong Inc.) cited in [[ai-engineering-ch10-architecture-feedback|*AI Engineering* Ch 10]] as one of the off-the-shelf [[ModelGateway|model-gateway]] options.

> *"There are many off-the-shelf gateways. Examples include Portkey's AI Gateway, MLflow AI Gateway, Wealthsimple's LLM Gateway, TrueFoundry, Kong, and Cloudflare."* — Ch 10

## Position

Kong is an **established general-purpose API gateway** (open-source core: Kong Gateway) that has been retrofitted with AI-gateway features — rate-limiting, fallback, caching, observability — that map naturally onto the model-gateway role. Its appearance in Huyen's list reflects the convergence: general API-gateway companies extend into the LLM space because the operational requirements overlap heavily.
