# Erwin Schrödinger

**Erwin Schrödinger** (Austrian physicist) derived the wave equation describing electrons as three-dimensional stationary waves, or **wavefunctions (ψ)**:

> **Ĥψ = Eψ**

Solving it yields the allowed energies and the [[AtomicOrbital|atomic orbitals]] of an atom, characterized by the [[QuantumNumbers|quantum numbers]] n, l, and m_l. Max Born's interpretation that **|ψ|²** is the electron's probability density completed the quantum-mechanical model that replaced the [[BohrModel|Bohr model]].
