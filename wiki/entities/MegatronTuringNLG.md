Megatron-Turing NLG is a 530-billion-parameter dense Transformer language model developed jointly by Microsoft and NVIDIA and announced in October 2021. At release it was among the largest dense (non-mixture-of-experts) language models, trained using NVIDIA's Megatron and Microsoft's DeepSpeed parallelism stacks.
