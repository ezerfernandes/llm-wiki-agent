# Mohawk Valley Community College (MVCC)

Community college in Utica, New York, offering ABET-accredited Associate-in-Applied-Science (AAS) programs in Electrical Engineering Technology and Computer Engineering Technology. Institutional home of [[JamesFiore]], whose [[OER]] textbook series — including [[embedded-controllers-fiore|*Embedded Controllers Using C and Arduino*]] — is written for the second-year embedded-controllers course in that AAS curriculum.
