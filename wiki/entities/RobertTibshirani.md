# Robert Tibshirani

Professor of statistics at [[stanforduniversity|Stanford University]]. Proposed the [[Lasso]] (1996), the canonical $\ell_1$-penalized regression. Co-author of [[ElementsOfStatisticalLearning|The Elements of Statistical Learning]] (with Hastie & Friedman) and *An Introduction to the Bootstrap* (with [[BradleyEfron]]); co-author of [[islr-seventh-printing|An Introduction to Statistical Learning]]. With [[TrevorHastie]], coined [[GeneralizedAdditiveModels|generalized additive models]] (1986).
