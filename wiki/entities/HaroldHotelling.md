# Harold Hotelling

American mathematical statistician (1895–1973). Best known to the LLM-and-ML community as the originator of **[[PCA|Principal Component Analysis (PCA)]]** in its modern statistical form — *"Analysis of a complex of statistical variables into principal components"* (*Journal of Educational Psychology*, 1933). PCA had been independently formulated by Karl Pearson (1901), but Hotelling's 1933 paper is the standard modern reference and is the one cited in [[hands-on-llm-ch05-text-clustering-topic-modeling|*Hands-On LLMs* Ch 5]] (footnote 1) when the chapter contrasts PCA with [[UMAP]].

## From [[hands-on-llm-ch05-text-clustering-topic-modeling|*Hands-On LLMs* Ch 5]]

Hotelling is cited as a footnote when Ch 5 introduces PCA as the *linear-projection* baseline against which UMAP's nonlinear manifold reduction is contrasted. The chapter uses UMAP rather than PCA because *"it tends to handle nonlinear relationships and structures a bit better than PCA."*
