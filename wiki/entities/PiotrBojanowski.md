# Piotr Bojanowski

Research scientist at [[fair|Facebook AI Research (FAIR) Paris]]; first author of **[[FastText|fastText]]: Enriching Word Vectors with Subword Information** ([[EdouardGrave|Grave]], [[ArmandJoulin|Joulin]] et al., TACL 2017). fastText represents each word as the sum of its character-$n$-gram ([[SubwordEmbedding|subword]]) vectors, dramatically improving representations of rare and **out-of-vocabulary** words versus [[Word2Vec|word2vec]] and [[GloVe]]. Later contributed to large-scale self-supervised visual representation learning at FAIR (SwAV, DINO, MSN family).
