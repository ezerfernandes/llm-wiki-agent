# Marie Curie

**Marie Curie** was a pioneering physicist and chemist who advanced the study of [[Radioactivity|radioactivity]] (a term she coined) and isolated radioactive elements, building on [[HenriBecquerel|Becquerel's]] discovery. Her work is foundational to the nuclear physics of [[college-physics-2e-ch31]]; radium (²²⁶Ra), one of the elements central to her research, defines the historical [[HalfLife|activity unit]] the curie (1 Ci ≈ the activity of 1 g of ²²⁶Ra).
