# Cambridge University Press

Academic publisher; oldest university press in the world (founded 1534). Publishes [[mml-book]] (*Mathematics for Machine Learning*, Deisenroth/Faisal/Ong, 2020).

Notably, Cambridge UP supports the **free online distribution** of *Mathematics for Machine Learning* in addition to its print edition — the PDF and HTML versions are freely downloadable from <https://mml-book.com>. Foreword (p. 1) credits Cambridge UP's "strong support" of the open-access aim.

## Role in this wiki

- Publisher of [[mml-book]].
- The free-distribution policy makes the book accessible to the [[mml-book]]'s three target audiences (Astute Listener / Experienced Artist / Fledgling Composer) without paywall friction — contrast with [[pml1-murphy]] which is similarly free via [[KevinMurphy]] / [[MITPress]] under CC-BY-NC-ND.
