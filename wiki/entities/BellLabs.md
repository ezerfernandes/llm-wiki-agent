# Bell Labs (AT&T)

AT&T's storied research arm (Murray Hill, NJ); birthplace of the transistor, information theory ([[ClaudeShannon|Shannon]]), Unix, C, and — for the wiki — [[LeNet]]. [[YannLeCun]] and [[LeonBottou]] developed and deployed LeNet-5 for ATM check-deposit OCR at AT&T Bell Labs in the 1990s; some ATMs still run that code today ([[d2l-convolutional-neural-networks]]).

## Why it matters here

- Institutional home of [[LeNet]]'s development and the [[CNN]] line of research from ~1988–1998 (LeCun, Bottou, Denker, Jackel, Henderson, et al.).
- Connects deep learning's pre-revival history to industrial research labs.
