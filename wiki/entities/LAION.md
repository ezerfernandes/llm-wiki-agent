# LAION

A German non-profit organization that **builds and releases large open-source datasets for machine learning**. Best known for the LAION-5B image-text pair dataset. Per [[ai-engineering-ch02-foundation-models|*AI Engineering* Ch 2]], LAION is a contrast point for the cost of human-labeled [[DemonstrationData|demonstration data]]: not everyone can afford [[openai|OpenAI]]'s ~$130K InstructGPT-style labeling budget.

## Volunteer-labeled conversation data (Köpf et al. 2023, cited in Ch 2)

LAION mobilized **13,500 volunteers worldwide** to generate:
- **10,000 conversations**
- **161,443 messages**
- **35 different languages**
- **461,292 quality ratings**

## The bias caveat

> "Since the data was generated by volunteers, there wasn't much control for biases. In theory, the labelers that teach models the human preference should be representative of the human population. The demographic of labelers for LAION is skewed. For example, in a self-reported survey, **90% of volunteer labelers identified as male** (Köpf et al., 2023)." — Ch 2

This is Ch 2's clearest data point that **demographic skew in labeling propagates into model behavior**.

## Why it matters in the wiki

LAION illustrates the **cost-quality trade-off** of [[DemonstrationData|demonstration data]] collection:

| Source | Cost | Volume | Quality / Representativeness |
|---|---|---|---|
| InstructGPT-style paid labelers | ~$130K / 13K pairs | Low volume | High quality, college-educated, ≥1/3 master's |
| **LAION volunteers** | **Free** | **High volume** | **Lower quality control, demographic skew (90% male)** |
| DeepMind heuristic filtering | Minimal | Vast | No labeling at all; varying quality |
| AI-generated synthetic | Marginal compute | Vast | Bounded by the generator's quality |
