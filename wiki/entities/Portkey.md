# Portkey

Vendor of the **Portkey AI Gateway** — a [[ModelGateway|model-gateway]] product cited in [[ai-engineering-ch10-architecture-feedback|*AI Engineering* Ch 10]] as one of the canonical off-the-shelf gateway implementations.

> *"There are many off-the-shelf gateways. Examples include Portkey's AI Gateway, MLflow AI Gateway, Wealthsimple's LLM Gateway, TrueFoundry, Kong, and Cloudflare."* — Ch 10

## Significance

Portkey is one of several startups occupying the *unified model-API* niche — providing a single endpoint that abstracts over [[openai|OpenAI]], [[anthropic|Anthropic]], [[gemini|Gemini]], self-hosted models, and other providers, with access control, fallback policies, observability, and cost management bolted in. Its presence in Huyen's named list places it alongside the established platform vendors (Kong, Cloudflare) and the ML-platform incumbents (MLflow).
