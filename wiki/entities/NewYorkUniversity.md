## From [[ai-engineering-ch02-foundation-models|AI Engineering Ch 2]]

NYU is the institutional home of the **[[InverseScalingPrize|Inverse Scaling Prize]] (2023)** — a research contest with $5K / $20K / $100K tiers designed to find tasks where larger language models perform worse. Per Ch 2: 99 submissions; 11 third prizes awarded; no first or second prize awarded because submitted failures didn't transfer to real-world tasks.

Key Ch-2 result from the prize: larger LMs are sometimes worse on **tasks that require memorization** and **tasks with strong priors**.
