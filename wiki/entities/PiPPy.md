## What it is
PyTorch library for pipeline parallelism.

## In LLM Engineer's Handbook
PiPPy (Pipeline Parallelism for PyTorch, `pytorch/PiPPy`, 2022) is the official PyTorch library implementing [[PipelineParallelism]]. Referenced in [[leh-ch08-inference-optimization]] alongside [[MegatronLM]] and [[DeepSpeed]] as a primary PP implementation.
