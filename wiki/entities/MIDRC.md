# MIDRC — Medical Imaging and Data Resource Center

U.S. multi-institutional research data resource for medical imaging, funded by the **National Institute of Biomedical Imaging and Bioengineering (NIBIB)** under contract **75N92020C00021** and channeled through [[ARPAH|ARPA-H]]. Co-funder of [[2507.03152-medval|MedVAL]].
