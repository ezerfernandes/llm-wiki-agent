# Kevin P. Murphy

ML researcher at [[google|Google]] (Doug Eck named as his manager in the *PML* preface). Author of three [[MITPress]] textbooks in the *Adaptive Computation and Machine Learning* series:

1. *Machine Learning: A Probabilistic Perspective* (2012) — the predecessor, listed in the series page of [[pml1-murphy]].
2. *Probabilistic Machine Learning: An Introduction* (2022; 860 pp; third printing January 2025) — [[pml1-murphy]] in this wiki.
3. *Probabilistic Machine Learning: Advanced Topics* (2023; referenced as `[Mur23]` in [[pml1-murphy]]; covers RL, PGMs, generative modeling — the "PML2" sequel).

The unifying methodological commitment across all three is the [[ProbabilisticPerspective]]: every unknown quantity is a random variable endowed with a distribution; learning is inference over those distributions.
