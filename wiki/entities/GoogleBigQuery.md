# Google BigQuery

Google Cloud's columnar, serverless data warehouse. ELT destination for [[Airbyte]] extracts from [[PostgreSQL]] in [[madewithml-mlops-data-engineering]]; alternatives include [[Snowflake]], [[AmazonRedshift]], and [[ApacheHive]].
