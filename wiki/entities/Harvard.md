# Harvard University

The institutional home of [[VijayJanapaReddi|Vijay Janapa Reddi]] and the open-access textbook *Machine Learning Systems* (mlsysbook.ai, 2026), the source of the mlsysbook chapter wiki pages including [[mlsysbook-ch09-data-selection|Ch 9: Data Selection]]. The book is developed in the open as a community resource for teaching ML-systems engineering.
