# Charles Babbage (1791–1871)

English mathematician and inventor. Designed the **Difference Engine** (1822) and **[[AnalyticalEngine|Analytical Engine]]** (1837) — mechanical computing machines that anticipated, by over a century, the structural ideas of programmable digital computation (separated *store* and *mill*, conditional branching, punched-card programming).

Neither engine was completed in Babbage's lifetime, but the **Analytical Engine** design is widely regarded as the **conceptual antecedent** of the [[VonNeumannArchitecture|stored-program computer]]. Per [[dis-5-1-history|*Dive into Systems* Ch 5.1]], Babbage and [[AdaLovelace]] are named as the 19th-century conceptual antecedents of the 1930s–1940s electronic / electromechanical convergence.
