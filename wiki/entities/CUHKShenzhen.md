# The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen)

CUHK-Shenzhen is a research university in Shenzhen, China, affiliated with but **administratively distinct from** [[ChineseUniversityofHongKong|CUHK]] (Hong Kong campus). Email domain `link.cuhk.edu.cn` (vs. CUHK proper's `cuhk.edu.hk`).
