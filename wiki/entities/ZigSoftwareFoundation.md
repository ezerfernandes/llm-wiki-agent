# Zig Software Foundation

The Zig Software Foundation (ZSF) is the non-profit organization that stewards the development of the [[Zig]] programming language and its toolchain. The official getting-started page ([[zig-getting-started]]) solicits donations to the foundation as a way to "help speed up the development" of Zig, and the project's website carries a dedicated `/zsf/` section.

The page frames Zig as a "young project" that does not yet have the capacity to produce extensive documentation and learning materials, positioning community support and ZSF funding as levers for accelerating maturity.
