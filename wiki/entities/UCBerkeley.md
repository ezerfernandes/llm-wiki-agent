# UC Berkeley

University of California, Berkeley. EECS / RISELab. Primary affiliation of the lead authors of [[2507.19457-gepa|GEPA]] (Agrawal, Tan, Khare, Singhvi, Sen) and the senior authors [[DanKlein]], [[IonStoica]], [[AlexandrosDimakis]], [[KoushikSen]]. [[OmarKhattab]] (DSPy creator, [[Databricks]] principal scientist) and [[MateiZaharia]] (Spark / Databricks co-creator, Berkeley professor) are co-affiliated.

## Tracked contributions

- **[[2406.11695-mipro]]** (EMNLP 2024) — [[OmarKhattab]] and [[MateiZaharia]] (UC Berkeley faculty / Databricks-affiliated) are senior authors on **[[MIPROv2|MIPRO]]**. UC Berkeley is one of four institutional affiliations alongside [[stanforduniversity|Stanford]], [[KTH]], and [[BasisAI|Basis]].
- **[[2507.19457-gepa]]** (2026, ICLR Oral) — Genetic-Pareto reflective prompt optimizer; outperforms GRPO RL by up to 20% with 35× fewer rollouts; outperforms MIPROv2 by >10%.

## Notes

Berkeley is the home of the broader DSPy ecosystem (Khattab's [[DSPy]] research originated there; the [[MIPROv2]] / GEPA / TextGrad / Trace lineage all overlap Berkeley + Stanford collaborators).
