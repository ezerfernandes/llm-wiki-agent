# Bo Pang

NLP researcher; co-author with [[LillianLee|Lillian Lee]] of the foundational early-2000s sentiment-analysis papers including the one that introduced the **[[RottenTomatoes|Rotten Tomatoes]] movie-review dataset** used in *Hands-On LLMs* Ch 4:

> Bo Pang and Lillian Lee. *"Seeing stars: Exploiting class relationships for sentiment categorization with respect to rating scales."* arXiv preprint cs/0506075 (2005).
