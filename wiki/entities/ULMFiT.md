ULMFiT (Universal Language Model Fine-tuning, Howard & Ruder 2018) is a bidirectional-RNN transfer-learning method for NLP. It popularized the pretrain-then-finetune paradigm by pretraining a language model on a large corpus and then fine-tuning it for downstream tasks.
