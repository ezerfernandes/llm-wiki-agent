# Sarvesh Soni

Biomedical NLP researcher; co-organizer (with [[DinaDemnerFushman]]) of the [[ArchEHRQA2025|ArchEHR-QA 2025]] shared task at [[BioNLP2025]]. Co-authored the task overview paper and the dataset paper "A dataset for addressing patient's information needs related to clinical course of hospitalization" (Soni & Demner-Fushman, 2025).
