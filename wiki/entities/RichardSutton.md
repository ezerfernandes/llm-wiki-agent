# Richard Sutton

A reinforcement-learning pioneer whose 2019 essay **["The Bitter Lesson"](https://www.incompleteideas.net/IncIdeas/BitterLesson.html)** crystallized the dominant pattern of AI history: general methods that leverage computation outperform hand-engineered expertise, "by a large margin." Quoted as the organizing principle of Reddi's *Machine Learning Systems* ([[mlsysbook-ch01-introduction|Ch 1]]).
