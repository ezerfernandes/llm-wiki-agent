# Andrew Ng

Computer scientist, professor at Stanford, co-founder of **Google Brain**, **Coursera**, and **DeepLearning.AI**; founder of **Landing AI**. One of the most publicly recognized figures in modern AI — both for foundational ML research and for democratizing ML education through Coursera's *Machine Learning* course (one of the most-taken online courses in history).

## From [[hands-on-llm-ch05-text-clustering-topic-modeling|*Hands-On LLMs* Ch 5]]

Cited as a co-author of [[LatentDirichletAllocation|LDA]] — [[DavidBlei|Blei]], Ng & [[MichaelJordan|Jordan]], **JMLR** 3 (2003): 993–1022 — the foundational Bayesian topic-modeling paper Ch 5 contrasts BERTopic against. Ng was Blei's doctoral collaborator at UC Berkeley before joining Stanford; the LDA paper is one of his most-cited works.
