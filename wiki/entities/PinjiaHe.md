# Pinjia He

Researcher at [[CUHKShenzhen|The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen]]. Co-author (corresponding) of [[dspy-guardrails|*DSPy Guardrails: Building Safe LLM Applications via Self-Refining Language Model Pipelines*]] (2024) with [[BoxiYu]]. Also a co-author on the [[YuanEtAl2023|GPT-4 cipher-attack paper]] (*GPT-4 is too smart to be safe: Stealthy chat with LLMs via cipher*, 2023), placing him in the LLM-safety / jailbreak-research line on **both the attack and defense sides**.
