# Jacob Devlin

NLP researcher; first author of **BERT** (*"BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding"*, [[1810.04805-bert|Devlin et al. 2018]]) at [[google|Google]] AI Language. The paper introduced the **bidirectional Transformer encoder + [[maskedlanguagemodel|masked language modeling]] + next-sentence-prediction pretraining** recipe that became the canonical [[RepresentationModel|representation-model]] template for years. Cited in [[hands-on-llm-ch01-introduction-to-llms|*Hands-On LLMs* Ch 1]] as the originator of the encoder-only template the book uses to anchor its representation-model thread.
