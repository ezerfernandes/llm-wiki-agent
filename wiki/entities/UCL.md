# UCL — Université catholique de Louvain

Belgian university in **Louvain-la-Neuve**, Belgium. [[PeterVanRoy]] holds the chair in the Department of Computing Science and Engineering and teaches the second-year programming course **FSAB1402** (since 2005, second-year course since 2003) using [[Oz]] and the [[CTM]] textbook. The course is *"particularly interesting since it covers the three most important paradigms, functional, object-oriented, and dataflow concurrent programming, with many practical techniques and a formal semantics."* — [[vanroy-programming-paradigms-for-dummies|Van Roy 2009]].

## Affiliated Ph.D. theses referenced in [[vanroy-programming-paradigms-for-dummies]]

- **Raphaël Collet** (2007) — *The Limits of Network Transparency in a Distributed Programming Language* (Mozart simplifications + fault-tolerance abstractions)
- **Gustavo Gutiérrez** (in preparation, MANCOOSI project) — Graph Constraint Library for [[Gecode]]
- **Luis Quesada** (2006) — *Solving Constraint Graph Problems using Reachability Constraints based on Transitive Closure and Dominators*
- **Grégoire Dooms** (2006) — *The CP(Graph) Computation Domain in Constraint Programming*
- **Stefano Zampelli** (2008) — *A Constraint Programming Approach to Subgraph Isomorphism*
- **Fred Spiessens** (2007) — *Patterns of Safe Collaboration* (authority-propagation graph constraints for secure systems)

## Position in the wiki

The wiki's first **Belgian university** entity and the institutional home of the second of the wiki's two concurrent-constraint-programming research clusters (the first being [[DFKI]]). UCL's research line connects PL theory ([[Oz]] / [[DistributedOz]]) to applied [[ConcurrentConstraintProgramming|constraint programming]] via the [[Gecode]] graph-constraint library (jointly with DFKI / KTH).
