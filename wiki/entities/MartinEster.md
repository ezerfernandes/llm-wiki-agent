# Martin Ester

Computer scientist; professor at Simon Fraser University, Canada. First author of the original **[[DBSCAN]]** paper — *"A density-based algorithm for discovering clusters in large spatial databases with noise"* (Ester, Kriegel, Sander & Xu, **KDD '96**, Vol. 96, No. 34, pp. 226–231). DBSCAN remains one of the most widely cited papers in data mining, and is the conceptual parent of [[HDBSCAN]] ([[LelandMcInnes|McInnes]] et al.) — the density-based algorithm at the heart of the modern [[TextClustering|text-clustering]] pipeline.

## From [[hands-on-llm-ch05-text-clustering-topic-modeling|*Hands-On LLMs* Ch 5]]

Cited in a footnote (footnote 4) when Ch 5 introduces HDBSCAN as a hierarchical variant of DBSCAN. The chapter does not implement DBSCAN directly but uses its successor; the lineage is acknowledged.
