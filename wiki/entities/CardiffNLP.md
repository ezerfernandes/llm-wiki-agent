# Cardiff NLP

The NLP research group at **Cardiff University** (Wales, UK) — author of the [[TwitterRoBERTa|TweetEval / Twitter-RoBERTa]] family of social-media-trained Transformer checkpoints distributed on the [[HuggingFace|Hugging Face]] Hub under the `cardiffnlp/` namespace.

## In *Hands-On LLMs* Ch 4

[[hands-on-llm-ch04-text-classification|Ch 4]] uses [[TwitterRoBERTa|`cardiffnlp/twitter-roberta-base-sentiment-latest`]] as its **task-specific representation-model** demo for sentiment classification (F1 = 0.80 on [[RottenTomatoes|Rotten Tomatoes]]).
