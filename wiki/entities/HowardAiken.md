# Howard Aiken (1900–1973)

American physicist and computer engineer at [[HarvardUniversity|Harvard]]. Designed the **[[HarvardMarkI|Harvard Mark I]]** (completed 1944) — a large electromechanical computer that ran ballistic and atomic-bomb calculations for the U.S. Navy and the [[ManhattanProject]]. Built in collaboration with [[IBM]] (originally the *IBM Automatic Sequence Controlled Calculator*).

Per [[dis-5-1-history|*Dive into Systems* Ch 5.1]], the Mark I represents the **electromechanical** branch of the 1930s–1940s computing convergence — slower than the fully electronic [[ENIAC]] but arriving earlier (1944 vs 1945) and demonstrating that large-scale automatic computation was practically achievable.
