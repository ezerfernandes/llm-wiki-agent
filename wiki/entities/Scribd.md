# Scribd

Document and audiobook platform. Cited in [[ai-engineering-ch01-intro|*AI Engineering* Ch 1]] via **Matt Ross, senior manager of applied research at Scribd**, who told Huyen that **the estimated AI cost for his use cases dropped two orders of magnitude from April 2022 to April 2023**. This is the chapter's anchor data point for the [[AIInvestmentBoom|AI cost-collapse]] driving the surge in viable AI applications.
