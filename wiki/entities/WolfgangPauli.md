# Wolfgang Pauli

**Wolfgang Pauli** (Austrian physicist) formulated the [[PauliExclusionPrinciple|Pauli exclusion principle]]: no two electrons in the same atom can have exactly the same set of all four [[QuantumNumbers|quantum numbers]]. The consequence — that any [[AtomicOrbital|orbital]] holds at most two electrons of opposite spin — sets the capacity of every subshell and shell and is one of the three rules (with [[AufbauPrinciple|Aufbau]] and [[HundsRule|Hund's]]) that determine [[ElectronConfiguration|electron configurations]]. College Physics 2e (Ch.30) emphasizes that the principle applies because electrons are half-integer-[[ElectronSpin|spin]] particles, and that it directly produces the 2(2l+1) and 2n² capacities and the structure of the [[PeriodicTable|periodic table]].
