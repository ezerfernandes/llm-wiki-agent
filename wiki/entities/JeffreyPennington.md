# Jeffrey Pennington

NLP / deep-learning-theory researcher; first author of **[[GloVe]]: Global Vectors for Word Representation** ([[StanfordUniversity|Stanford NLP]], EMNLP 2014, with [[RichardSocher|Socher]] and [[ChrisManning|Manning]]). GloVe reframed [[Word2Vec|word2vec]]'s skip-gram objective as a **weighted squared-loss factorization** of the global word-word co-occurrence matrix, justified by the ratio of co-occurrence probabilities, and supplied the public 50/100/200/300-dim Common Crawl / Wikipedia / Twitter pretrained embeddings that became the dominant static-word-vector resource of the pre-[[BERT]] era. Later at [[google|Google Brain]] / [[GoogleResearch]], a frequent contributor to deep-learning theory ([[NeuralTangentKernel|NTK]], wide-network limits, signal propagation).
