# Andrew Zisserman

British computer-vision researcher; long-time professor at the [[oxforduniversity|University of Oxford]] and head of the Visual Geometry Group (VGG). Co-author with [[KarenSimonyan|Karen Simonyan]] of the **VGG** paper (Simonyan & Zisserman 2014). Pre-deep-learning era: co-author of the standard textbook *Multiple View Geometry in Computer Vision* (Hartley & Zisserman 2000), cited in [[d2l-convolutional-modern]] as part of the geometry-first computer-vision tradition that AlexNet displaced.

## Why he matters here

- **VGG (2014).** Senior author of the paper that introduced **blocks of small $3\times3$ convolutions** as a CNN-design template — and the doctrine "deep and narrow beats shallow" ([[d2l-convolutional-modern]] §vgg). Visual Geometry Group's eponymous architecture became one of the two dominant pre-ResNet backbones.
- **Pre-CNN geometry era.** Hartley & Zisserman's textbook is the canonical reference for the multi-view-geometry CV tradition; [[d2l-convolutional-modern]] cites it explicitly as the regime "feature, geometry, and engineering, rather than novel learning algorithms, drove progress" — until AlexNet.
