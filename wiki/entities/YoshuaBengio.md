# Yoshua Bengio

Canadian deep-learning pioneer; co-recipient (with [[GeoffreyHinton|Geoffrey Hinton]] and [[YannLeCun|Yann LeCun]]) of the **2018 Turing Award**. Professor at Université de Montréal, founder and scientific director of Mila (Quebec AI Institute). Co-author of *"Neural Machine Translation by Jointly Learning to Align and Translate"* (Bahdanau, Cho & Bengio, arXiv 1409.0473, 2014) — the paper that introduced **attention** to recurrent encoder-decoder neural machine translation. Cited in [[hands-on-llm-ch01-introduction-to-llms|*Hands-On LLMs* Ch 1]] as a co-author of the 2014 attention paper that precedes (and motivates) the [[transformer|Transformer]].

Bengio is also a co-author of the foundational 2003 neural-language-model paper (*"A Neural Probabilistic Language Model"*, Bengio, Ducharme, Vincent, Jauvin) — implicitly referenced in the wiki via [[LanguageModel|Language Model]] and forward-referenced throughout the LLM literature.
