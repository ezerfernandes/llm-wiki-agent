# Shuo Chen

Researcher who, while at [[CornellUniversity|Cornell University]], collected the **US-radio-station playlist dataset** used in *Hands-On LLMs* Ch 2's [[Word2VecRecommender|song-embedding worked example]]. The dataset comprises playlists from hundreds of US radio stations and their associated song metadata, hosted at `storage.googleapis.com/maps-premium/dataset/yes_complete/`.

## From [[hands-on-llm-ch02-tokens-and-embeddings|*Hands-On LLMs* Ch 2]]

> "The dataset we'll use was collected by Shuo Chen from Cornell University. It contains playlists from hundreds of radio stations around the US." — Ch 2

The dataset's structure — each line is a playlist as a sequence of song IDs, plus a separate `song_hash.txt` mapping IDs to title + artist — is the exact format the chapter's [[Word2Vec|word2vec]]-as-recommender demo consumes.
