# Peter Van Roy

Professor at [[UCL|Université catholique de Louvain]] (Louvain-la-Neuve, Belgium), Department of Computing Science and Engineering. Long-time collaborator of [[GertSmolka]] and the [[DFKI]] Programming Systems Lab on the [[Oz]] / [[OPM|Oz Programming Model]] research line; principal of the **PERDIO** (Persistent and Distributed Programming in Oz) project at DFKI in 1995 that led to **[[DistributedOz|Distributed Oz]]** and the **[[MozartProgrammingSystem|Mozart Programming System]]** (released 1999). Co-author with [[SeifHaridi]] of the textbook *Concepts, Techniques, and Models of Computer Programming* ([[CTM]], MIT Press 2004). Teaches the [[UCL]] second-year course **FSAB1402** (concurrent + functional + object-oriented programming with formal semantics) since 2003.
