# Flowise

Open-source low-code / visual builder for [[AIPipelineOrchestration|AI pipeline orchestration]]. Cited in [[ai-engineering-ch10-architecture-feedback|*AI Engineering* Ch 10]] as one of the named AI orchestration tools:

> *"There are many AI orchestration tools, including LangChain, LlamaIndex, Flowise, Langflow, and Haystack."* — Ch 10

## Position

Flowise sits in the **visual / no-code** segment of the LLM-orchestration market, offering a drag-and-drop UI for assembling chains over LangChain primitives. Its peer [[Langflow]] competes in the same segment.

Where [[LangChain]] and [[LlamaIndex]] are code-first frameworks for engineering teams, Flowise and Langflow target faster prototyping by non-coders or for rapid iteration.
