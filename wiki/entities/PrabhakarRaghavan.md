Computer scientist and technology executive; co-author of *Introduction to Information Retrieval* (Cambridge University Press 2008, with [[ChristopherManning]] & [[HinrichSchutze]]) — the canonical IR textbook ingested across this wiki's `iir-ch01`–`iir-ch21` sources.

Theoretical-CS work on randomized algorithms, online algorithms (k-server, paging), web information retrieval, and algorithmic mechanism design. Co-author of *Randomized Algorithms* (Cambridge University Press 1995, with Rajeev Motwani) — another foundational textbook.

Industry roles: research head at IBM Almaden; head of Yahoo Research (where he overlapped with [[AndreiBroder]] on web-search and computational-advertising research); Google's head of Search (since 2018) and now senior vice president (since 2020) — the executive responsible for Google's user-facing search products. The combination of theoretical-CS depth + textbook-quality exposition + production-search executive role is unusual; the IIR textbook is in part a snapshot of what production web search looked like in the mid-2000s at the level of detail an executive could publish openly.
