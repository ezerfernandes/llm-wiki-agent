# Magic

Cited in [[ai-engineering-ch01-intro|*AI Engineering* Ch 1]] for raising **$320 million in August 2024** — the largest of the two AI-coding fundraises Huyen calls out by name (alongside [[Anysphere]]'s $60M in the same month), and an illustration of the capital flowing into the AI-coding category post-[[ChatGPT|ChatGPT]].
