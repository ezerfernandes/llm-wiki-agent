# Pratik Chaudhari

Assistant Professor at the University of Pennsylvania (ESE / GRASP) and scientist at [[Amazon]]; researcher in [[reinforcementlearning|reinforcement learning]], [[ContinuousOptimization|optimization]] for deep learning, and robotics. Guest co-author (with [[RasoolFakoor|Fakoor]] and [[KavoshAsadi|Asadi]]) of D2L's *Reinforcement Learning* chapter ([[d2l-reinforcement-learning]]), the textbook's introduction to [[MarkovDecisionProcess|MDPs]], [[ValueIteration|Value Iteration]], and [[QLearning|Q-Learning]].
