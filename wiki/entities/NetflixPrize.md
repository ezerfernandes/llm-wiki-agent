# Netflix Prize

\$1M open competition announced by Netflix in 2006 to improve their **Cinematch** recommender's RMSE by 10% — won in 2009 by **BellKor's Pragmatic Chaos**, a merged team of BellKor (incl. [[YehudaKoren|Koren]]), Pragmatic Theory, and BigChaos. The final solution was an ensemble of ~100 models, but **[[MatrixFactorization]] was the load-bearing component** and the technique that most influenced the subsequent field (Töscher, Jahrer & Bell 2009 technical report).

Cited in [[d2l-recommender-systems]] §mf as the historical event that put MF on the recommender-systems map and motivated the subsequent decade of latent-factor research.
