# Photoroom

AI image-editing startup focused on background removal and photo-product workflows for sellers and creators. [[ChipHuyen|Chip Huyen]] discloses in [[ai-engineering-ch01-intro|*AI Engineering* Ch 1]] that she is an investor. Cited as an example of a startup whose product *could've been a feature of [[google|Google]] Photos* — but which has succeeded as an independent business, illustrating the [[AIProductDefensibility|defensibility]] discussion: many startups overtake incumbents by building a feature the incumbent overlooks.
