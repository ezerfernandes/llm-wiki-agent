## What it is
Open-source post-training framework supporting SFT, DPO, and other preference-alignment algorithms.

## In LLM Engineer's Handbook
Axolotl is an open-source post-training framework that wraps [[TRL]] with reusable YAML configs and automatic dataset-format normalization. [[leh-ch05-supervised-fine-tuning]] lists it as one of three recommended SFT libraries alongside [[TRL]] and [[Unsloth]]; [[leh-ch06-preference-alignment]] mentions it as an alternative for preference-alignment training.
