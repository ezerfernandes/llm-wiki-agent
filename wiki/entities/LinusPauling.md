# Linus Pauling

**Linus Pauling** was, in the words of OpenStax *Chemistry 2e*, "one of the most famous chemists of the twentieth century." He proposed the most widely used **[[Electronegativity|electronegativity]]** scale (the Pauling scale), a dimensionless measure of an atom's tendency to attract bonding electrons, on which **fluorine is the most electronegative element (EN = 4.0)**. The scale underpins predictions of [[BondPolarity|bond polarity]] and bond type from electronegativity differences.
