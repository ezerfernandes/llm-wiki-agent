# Josiah Willard Gibbs

**Josiah Willard Gibbs** is the namesake of the [[GibbsFreeEnergy|Gibbs free energy]], the thermodynamic state function G = H − TS used in chemistry to determine whether a process is spontaneous at constant temperature and pressure.

## Contribution
- The free-energy function **G = H − TS** and its change **ΔG = ΔH − TΔS = −TΔS_univ**, which lets spontaneity be judged from system properties alone (ΔG < 0 spontaneous).
- The link between standard free energy and the [[EquilibriumConstant|equilibrium constant]], [[GibbsFreeEnergyAndEquilibrium|ΔG° = −RT ln K]].
