# Wen-wai Yim

First author of **AciBench** (Yim, Fu, Ben Abacha, Snider, Lin & Yetisgen, *Scientific Data* 10(1):586, 2023) — the ambient-clinical-intelligence benchmark dataset for automatic visit-note generation, and the source of the [[MEDCON]] medical-concept-coverage metric reused in [[ArchEHRQA2025]].

- [[MEDCON]] — metric introduced with AciBench.
