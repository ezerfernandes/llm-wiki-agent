# Koushik Sen

[[UCBerkeley]] EECS professor. Research span: programming languages, software engineering, testing, symbolic execution, concolic testing. Senior co-author on **[[2312.13382-dspy-assertions|DSPy Assertions (2024)]]** and **[[2507.19457-gepa|GEPA (ICLR 2026)]]** — the PL-side connection to the DSPy-adjacent research line. The PL framing is especially visible in [[2312.13382-dspy-assertions|DSPy Assertions]] (big-step operational semantics, retry states $\sigma_r$, error-handler dispatch — the language-design discipline applied to LM pipelines).
