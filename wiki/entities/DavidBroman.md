# David Broman

Professor at [[KTH|KTH Royal Institute of Technology]] in Stockholm; co-author of the [[2406.11695-mipro|MIPRO paper (EMNLP 2024)]]. KTH-side author of the otherwise Stanford / Basis / UC Berkeley team.

## Tracked contributions

- **[[2406.11695-mipro]]** — co-author; **KTH** is named as one of the four institutional affiliations alongside [[stanforduniversity|Stanford]], [[BasisAI|Basis]], and [[UCBerkeley]].

## Research arc

Broman's broader research is in programming languages and modeling for cyber-physical systems; the MIPRO co-authorship is a contribution from the programming-languages perspective to the **"programming, not prompting"** thesis of [[DSPy]] — LM programs as first-class compilable artifacts.
