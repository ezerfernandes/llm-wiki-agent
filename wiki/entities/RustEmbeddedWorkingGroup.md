# Rust Embedded Working Group

Official [[RustLanguage|Rust]] community working group coordinating embedded-systems support in the Rust ecosystem. Homed at `github.com/rust-embedded/wg`. Maintains the [[EmbeddedRustBookshelf]] of canonical resources including [[TheEmbeddedRustBook]] (governed by the WG's **resources team**), the [[DiscoveryBook]], the [[Embedonomicon]], and the embedded FAQ.

## Connections

- [[TheEmbeddedRustBook]] — the WG's resources team coordinates this book in `github.com/rust-embedded/book` ([[rust-embedded-book-intro-index]]).
- [[RustLanguage]] — the language whose embedded story this WG owns.
