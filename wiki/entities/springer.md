# Springer

Springer Science+Business Media — academic publisher. Publishes *Springer Texts in Statistics* (series editors: G. Casella, S. Fienberg, I. Olkin), which includes [[islr-seventh-printing|An Introduction to Statistical Learning]] (2013). Also publishes [[ElementsOfStatisticalLearning|The Elements of Statistical Learning]].
