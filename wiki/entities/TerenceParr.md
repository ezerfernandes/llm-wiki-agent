# Terence Parr

Software engineer and educator; creator of the **ANTLR** parser generator and longtime professor at the University of San Francisco (later at Google). Co-author, with [[JeremyHoward|Jeremy Howard]], of two explained.ai works: **"The Matrix Calculus You Need For Deep Learning"** ([[matrix-calculus-for-deep-learning]]) — a Calculus-1-level rederivation of the matrix calculus behind neural-network training — and the applied **"The Mechanics of Machine Learning"** ([[mechanics-of-ml]]), a Random-Forest-centric practical primer for programmers. The two bracket his explained.ai pedagogy: theory (gradients for NNs) and practice (tabular ML workflow), both "intuition first."
