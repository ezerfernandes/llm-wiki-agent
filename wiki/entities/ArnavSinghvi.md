# Arnav Singhvi

[[UCBerkeley]] researcher; co-first author on **[[2312.13382-dspy-assertions|DSPy Assertions]]** (Singhvi, Shetty, Tan, Potts, Sen, Zaharia, Khattab 2024). One of the framework-side contributors to [[DSPy]] — `dspy.Assert` and `dspy.Suggest` are the productized form of the paper.

## Tracked contributions

- **[[2312.13382-dspy-assertions]]** (2024) — co-first author with [[ManishShetty]] and [[ShangyinTan]]; introduces [[LMAssertions|LM Assertions]] (`Assert`, `Suggest`), [[AssertionDrivenBacktracking|assertion-driven backtracking]], [[AssertionDrivenExampleBootstrapping|assertion-driven example bootstrapping]], and [[CounterexampleBootstrapping|counterexample bootstrapping]]. Released as `dspy.Assert` / `dspy.Suggest`.
