## What it is
Alex Vesa is one of the three co-authors of "LLM Engineer's Handbook: Master the Art of Engineering Large Language Models from Concept to Production" (Packt, 2024) alongside [[PaulIusztin]] and [[MaximeLabonne]].

## In LLM Engineer's Handbook
Vesa is credited across all 11 chapters as a co-author of the book; the chapters do not single out individual authorship for sections, but the deployment-engineering and infrastructure threads of Ch. 10 ([[leh-ch10-inference-pipeline-deployment]]) and Ch. 11 ([[leh-ch11-mlops-and-llmops]]) reflect his systems-engineering perspective on the LLM Twin.
