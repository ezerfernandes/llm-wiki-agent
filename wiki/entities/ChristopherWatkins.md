# Christopher Watkins

British computer scientist; introduced **[[QLearning|Q-Learning]]** in his 1989 Cambridge PhD thesis "Learning from Delayed Rewards" and formalized its convergence theorem with Peter Dayan in *Watkins & Dayan, "Q-learning"* (Machine Learning, 1992) — the citation [[d2l-reinforcement-learning]] §qlearning uses. The Watkins–Dayan convergence result — that Q-Learning **converges to the optimal policy under standard step-size conditions even from a random behavior policy** — is the theoretical foundation underpinning every modern model-free off-policy method (including DQN).
