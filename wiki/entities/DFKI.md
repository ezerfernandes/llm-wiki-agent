# DFKI

**Deutsches Forschungszentrum für Künstliche Intelligenz** — the **German Research Center for Artificial Intelligence**, with sites including Saarbrücken (Stuhlsatzenhausweg 3, 66123 Saarbrücken), Kaiserslautern, Bremen, and Berlin. Founded 1988.

## In this wiki

- **Programming Systems Lab** (Saarbrücken) is the institutional home of the [[Oz]] language and the [[OPM|Oz Programming Model]] research program led by [[GertSmolka]]; cited in [[vol1000-oz-programming-model]].
- The lab developed [[Oz]] from 1991 onward; *DFKI Oz* — an efficient, robust, interactive implementation for Unix platforms with a programming interface based on GNU Emacs, a concurrent browser, an object-oriented Tcl/Tk interface, and an incremental compiler — was freely available via `http://ps-www.dfki.uni-sb.de/`.
- Funded by **BMBF** (Bundesministerium für Bildung und Forschung, contract ITW 9105), **Esprit Basic Research Project ACCLAIM** (EP 7195), and **Esprit Working Group CCL** (EP 6028) for the OPM / Oz research line.
