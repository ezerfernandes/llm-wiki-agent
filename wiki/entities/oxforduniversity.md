# University of Oxford

University in Oxford, England; one of the oldest universities in the English-speaking world. Within this wiki, Oxford appears as the institutional home of the **Visual Geometry Group (VGG)** — [[KarenSimonyan|Karen Simonyan]] & [[AndrewZisserman|Andrew Zisserman]] — and of the eponymous [[VGG]] CNN architecture (Simonyan & Zisserman 2014, [[d2l-convolutional-modern]] §vgg). Also a co-affiliation on the agentic-world-modeling survey ([[2604.22748-agentic-world-modeling]]).
