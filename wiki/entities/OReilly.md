# O'Reilly Media

Sebastopol, CA-based technical book and online-learning publisher; one of the canonical homes for software engineering and ML practitioner books. Publisher of [[ChipHuyen|Chip Huyen]]'s *AI Engineering: Building Applications with Foundation Models* ([[ai-engineering-chip-huyen]], December 2024, ISBN 978-1-098-16630-4) and her earlier *Designing Machine Learning Systems* (2022). The publisher's animal-cover series and "Definitive Guide" / "Cookbook" / "Up & Running" naming conventions are widely-recognized markers of practitioner-targeted technical writing.

## In this wiki

The wiki's first explicitly-tagged O'Reilly source is *AI Engineering* (Chs 1–10). O'Reilly's editorial process, as recorded in the book's front matter, includes Acquisitions Editor Nicole Butterfield, Development Editor Melissa Potter, and Production Editor Beth Kelly. The publisher hosts the book's accompanying online platform and supports errata submission via `bookquestions@oreilly.com`.

The wiki's second O'Reilly book is **[[HandsOnLLM|*Hands-On Large Language Models*]]** by [[JayAlammar]] and [[MaartenGrootendorst]] (2024, ISBN 978-1-098-15096-9) — ingested starting with [[hands-on-llm-ch01-introduction-to-llms|Ch 1]]. Two O'Reilly LLM books in the 2024 publishing year share the publisher's "animal cover + Definitive Guide-style positioning" identity.
