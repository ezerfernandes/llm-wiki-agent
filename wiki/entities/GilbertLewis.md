# Gilbert N. Lewis

**Gilbert Newton Lewis** was an American chemist whose name is attached to the **[[LewisStructure|Lewis symbols and Lewis structures]]** used throughout chemistry to represent valence electrons, bonding pairs, and lone pairs. The electron-dot notation and the associated **[[OctetRule|octet rule]]** — the tendency of main-group atoms to acquire eight valence electrons — form the foundation of how [[CovalentBonding|covalent bonding]] is taught and visualized.

In 1923 he also proposed the **[[LewisAcidsAndBases|Lewis model of acids and bases]]**, defining a [[LewisAcid|Lewis acid]] as an electron-pair acceptor and a [[LewisBase|Lewis base]] as an electron-pair donor that together form a [[CoordinateCovalentBond|coordinate covalent bond]] — the most general acid–base framework, subsuming the Brønsted-Lowry proton-transfer picture (Ch 15).
