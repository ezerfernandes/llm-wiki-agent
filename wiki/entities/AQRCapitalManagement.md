# AQR Capital Management

Quantitative investment management firm. [[WesMcKinney]] worked here when he began building [[pandas]] in early 2008. AQR's needs (labeled-axis data structures, integrated time series, missing-data handling, SQL-style merges) shaped pandas's API and emphasis on time-series operations.
