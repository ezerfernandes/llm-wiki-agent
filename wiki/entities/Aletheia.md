# Aletheia

[[googledeepmind|Google DeepMind]]'s autonomous mathematics research system (Feng et al., *Towards Autonomous Mathematics Research*, 2026; arXiv 2602.10177). Strong informal prover.

In [[2605.06651v2-ai-co-mathematician]] Aletheia is named as the alternative to [[AlphaProof]] for the prover sub-agent slot: it would add considerable power "at an associated computational cost." The [[AICoMathematician|AI co-mathematician]] is explicitly framed as a *harness over* autonomous reasoners like Aletheia rather than a replacement.
