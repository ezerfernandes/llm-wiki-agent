# Modin

Drop-in pandas accelerator that parallelizes DataFrame ops on [[Ray]] or [[Dask]]. Mentioned in [[madewithml-foundations-pandas]] and [[madewithml-mlops-distributed-data]] as a path from single-node pandas to distributed workloads.
