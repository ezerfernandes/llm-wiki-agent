# Werner Heisenberg

**Werner Heisenberg** (German physicist) formulated the [[HeisenbergUncertaintyPrinciple|uncertainty principle]]: it is fundamentally impossible to know both the position and the momentum of a particle exactly at the same time,

> **Δx · Δpₓ ≥ ℏ/2**

A consequence of [[WaveParticleDuality|wave-particle duality]], this principle rules out the precise classical orbits of the [[BohrModel|Bohr model]] and underlies the probabilistic [[AtomicOrbital|orbital]] description of electrons. College Physics 2e also states the energy-time form ΔE · Δt ≥ h/(4π) and traces the limit to measurement disturbing the system.
