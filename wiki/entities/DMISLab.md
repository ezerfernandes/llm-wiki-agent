# DMISLab

Data Mining and Information Systems Lab — biomedical NLP research group. **Winning team** (1st place, overall 53.7) at the [[ArchEHRQA2025|ArchEHR-QA 2025]] shared task; best on Factuality, BLEU, ROUGE, BERTScore, AlignScore, MEDCON, overall AVG-relevance. Beaten on SARI (36.7 vs. Neural's 73.1) and lenient F1 strict precision.
