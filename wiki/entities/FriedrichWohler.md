# Friedrich Wöhler

**Friedrich Wöhler** (German chemist) is credited in OpenStax *Chemistry 2e* with the experiment that founded modern [[OrganicChemistry|organic chemistry]]. In **1828** he **synthesized urea** — a compound found in body fluids — from **non-living (inorganic) materials**.

## Significance

The result challenged and ultimately overturned **vitalism**, the prevailing theory that organic molecules could only be produced by a "vital force" present in living things. By making an organic molecule from inorganic starting materials, Wöhler demonstrated that **organic molecules follow the same natural laws as inorganic substances** — there is no special vital force required. This unified the study of carbon compounds with the rest of chemistry.
