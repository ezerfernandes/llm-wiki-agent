# Percy Lavon Julian

**Percy Lavon Julian** was a chemist cited in OpenStax *Chemistry 2e* Ch. 1 as an example of how chemistry advances medicine. Animal-derived hormones such as progesterone (available as a medicine in 1935) were once extremely scarce and expensive. Julian developed processes that used abundant **soybean** sources — isolating soy protein and working with soy sterols — to rapidly produce progesterone, testosterone, and other hormones, and he later developed a synthesis for cortisone (which had previously required a 36-step process). Because soybeans were plentiful, these drugs became widely available, saving many lives.

He illustrates the chapter's theme of [[Chemistry]] as "the central science" with direct, life-saving impact.
