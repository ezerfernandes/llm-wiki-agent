# EleutherAI

Open-source research collective focused on language models and **evaluation tooling**. Per [[ai-engineering-ch04-evaluate-ai-systems|*AI Engineering* Ch 4]]:

> "EleutherAI's lm-evaluation-harness supports over 400 benchmarks."

## Significance

EleutherAI is the maintainer of the **de facto standard open-source [[EvaluationHarness|evaluation harness]]** — [[lm-evaluation-harness]]. It powers HuggingFace's [[OpenLLMLeaderboard|Open LLM Leaderboard]] under the hood for many benchmarks.

Beyond evaluation, EleutherAI is known for early open-source LLMs (GPT-Neo, GPT-J, Pythia) and for community-driven research and replication work.
