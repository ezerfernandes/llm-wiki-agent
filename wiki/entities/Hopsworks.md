## What it is
Hopsworks is an MLOps platform company built around an enterprise feature store, founded by [[JimDowling]]. It is the source of the **Feature/Training/Inference (FTI)** pipeline pattern that the book adopts.

## In LLM Engineer's Handbook
Ch. 1 ([[leh-ch01-understanding-llm-twin-concept]]) credits Hopsworks' blog post by Jim Dowling for the FTI architectural pattern that frames the entire LLM Twin design. Ch. 10 ([[leh-ch10-inference-pipeline-deployment]]) lists Hopsworks as one of several alternative deployment platforms, and Ch. 11 ([[leh-ch11-mlops-and-llmops]]) names Hopsworks alongside [[Tecton]] and Featureform as the canonical feature-store options when surveying MLOps components.
