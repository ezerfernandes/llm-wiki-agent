# Greg Brockman

Co-founder of [[openai|OpenAI]]; longtime president and CTO. Quoted in [[ai-engineering-ch08-dataset-engineering|*AI Engineering* Ch 8]] for the often-repeated remark on the under-appreciated value of manual data inspection:

> "Manual inspection of data has probably the highest value-to-prestige ratio of any activity in machine learning."

## Why he matters here

The Brockman quote is [[ChipHuyen|Huyen]]'s anchor for **the "data-processing" section's core practice**: before all the dedup / clean / filter / format machinery, just *stare at the data*. Ch 8 frames this as both a discipline and a culture problem — manual data inspection is unglamorous and skipped, but produces outsized insights.
