# Sam Altman

CEO of [[openai|OpenAI]]. In [[ai-engineering-ch01-intro|*AI Engineering* Ch 1]], Huyen quotes Altman from a September 2022 interview making what is effectively the **founding argument for [[AIEngineering|AI engineering]] as a discipline**: because building foundation models is restricted to a handful of big corporations, governments, and well-funded labs, *"the biggest opportunity for the vast majority of people will be to adapt these models for specific applications."* This thesis underlies the entire book — that [[ModelAdaptation|model adaptation]], not model creation, will be the high-leverage activity for most engineering teams.
