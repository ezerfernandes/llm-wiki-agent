# Nils Reimers

First author of **Sentence-BERT** (*"Sentence-BERT: Sentence Embeddings using Siamese BERT-Networks"*, EMNLP 2019, arXiv:1908.10084) with [[IrynaGurevych|Iryna Gurevych]] at TU Darmstadt's UKP Lab. Original creator and core maintainer of the [[SentenceTransformers|sentence-transformers]] Python library.

Now Director of Machine Learning at [[Cohere]] (per public profile), leading the embeddings / retrieval research line.

## From [[hands-on-llm-ch02-tokens-and-embeddings|*Hands-On LLMs* Ch 2]]

Ch 2 cites the Sentence-BERT paper (Reimers & Gurevych, 2019) as the foundation for the [[SentenceTransformers]] library and the `all-mpnet-base-v2` worked example.
