# Khattab et al. 2023 — DSPy paper reference

**Omar Khattab, Arnav Singhvi, Paridhi Maheshwari, Zhiyuan Zhang, Keshav Santhanam, Sri Vardhamanan, Saiful Haq, Ashutosh Sharma, Thomas T Joshi, Hanna Moazam, et al. 2023.** *DSPy: Compiling declarative language model calls into self-improving pipelines.* arXiv:2310.03714.

The foundational [[DSPy]] paper, cited by [[dspy-guardrails|Yu & He 2024]] as the basis of the [[DSPyGuardrails]] machinery. See [[DSPy]] entity page for the framework itself, and [[OmarKhattab]] for the lead author.
