# Andrew Gordon Wilson

[[NewYorkUniversity|NYU]] Courant / Center for Data Science associate professor and [[amazon|Amazon]] Scholar; guest author of [[d2l-gaussian-processes|D2L's Gaussian Processes chapter]]. Research program centers on **scalable [[GaussianProcess|Gaussian processes]]**, [[BayesianDeepLearning|Bayesian deep learning]], and the kernel-methods ↔ deep-networks bridge. Co-author of [[GPyTorch]] ([[JacobGardner|Gardner]], Pleiss, Weinberger, Bindel & Wilson 2018, NeurIPS) — the [[PyTorch]]-integrated GP library underpinning state-of-the-art GP scaling (SKI / KISS-GP).

## Selected positions in the D2L chapter

- "GPs and deep neural networks are not competing approaches, but highly complementary, and can be combined to great effect." — [[d2l-gaussian-processes]] preamble.
- "Perhaps all the fuss about overparametrized neural networks is misplaced. As we will see, GPs with RBF kernels do not overfit, and in fact provide especially compelling generalization performance on small datasets." — gp-priors.
- Cited as the author of "Bayesian deep learning and a probabilistic perspective of generalization" (2020) which reproduces fit-random-labels neural-network experiments with GPs.
