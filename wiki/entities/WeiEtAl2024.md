# Wei et al. 2024 — *Jailbroken: How does LLM safety training fail?*

**Alexander Wei, Nika Haghtalab, Jacob Steinhardt. 2024.** *Jailbroken: How does LLM safety training fail?* Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS) 36.

Foundational empirical study of jailbreak failure modes in safety-trained LLMs — separates **competing objectives** (helpful vs. harmless) from **generalization mismatch** (safety training doesn't cover the input distribution of attacks). Cited in [[dspy-guardrails|Yu & He 2024]] as part of the jailbreak-research lineage.
