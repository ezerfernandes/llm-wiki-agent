# NXP Semiconductors

Dutch semiconductor company; producer of [[ARMCortexM|ARM Cortex-M]] microcontrollers and the [[MCULink|MCU-Link]] debug probe; vendor of the MCUXpresso IDE ([[rust-embedded-book-intro-tooling]]).
