# Grammarly

Writing-assistant product available as a browser extension, web app, and desktop integration. Cited in [[ai-engineering-ch01-intro|*AI Engineering* Ch 1]] as one of the **earliest production examples of a finetuned foundation model** in consumer use — the company *"finetunes a model to make users' writing more fluent, coherent, and clear."* Also used as the canonical example of a **browser-extension AI interface** alongside [[GitHubCopilot]] (IDE plug-in), [[ChatGPT]] (standalone web), and [[Midjourney]] (Discord integration).
