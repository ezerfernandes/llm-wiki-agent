# Van Veen et al. (Nature Medicine 2024)

**Adapted large language models can outperform medical experts in clinical text summarization** — Van Veen, Van Uden, Blankemeier, Delbrouck, Aali, Bluethgen, Pareek, Polacin, Pontes Reis, Seehofnerová et al., *Nature Medicine* 30(4):1134–1142, 2024. Ref [4] in [[2507.03152-medval]].

**Significance for the wiki**: this is the paper that **defined the canonical train/test splits** [[2507.03152-medval|MedVAL]] inherits across its [[Openi]] / [[ACIBench]] / [[MeQSum]]-derived tasks. By following these splits MedVAL ensures *"no overlap between the samples used in `report2impression` and `report2simplified`."*
