# Gareth James

Professor of statistics at the [[USC]] Department of Data Sciences and Operations. Methodological work concentrates on high-dimensional and functional data. First author of [[islr-seventh-printing|An Introduction to Statistical Learning]] ([[springer|Springer]], 2013) — the conceptual framework grew out of his MBA elective courses at USC.
