# Henri Becquerel

**Henri Becquerel** was a French physicist credited with the discovery of [[Radioactivity|nuclear radioactivity]] — the spontaneous emission of penetrating radiation by uranium compounds — which opened the field of nuclear physics covered in [[college-physics-2e-ch31]]. His finding that certain substances emit radiation without an external energy source revealed that the [[AtomicNucleus|nucleus]] itself can be unstable.
